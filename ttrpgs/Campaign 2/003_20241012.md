- After being slain by the monstrous Sky Dragon, the party found themselves deep in the realm of [[Eden]], in a region bathed in night and bearing nothing but the most monstrous of [[Abominations]]. On a small island, far away from the mainland, the party meets a young man named [[Makoto]], and learns of the name of their Penances. Based off of a rune in the constellations, [[Umak]] decides to jump off the side of the island in a literal leap of faith, where he is saved by a colossal Thunderbird that only responds in Enochian. Safe from death, he reunites with the party and begins to journey to a desert island in the distance. In the center of this colossal desert island is a sinkhole, and the constellations above seem to hint once again towards taking the risk of plunging into the depths, and therefore the party flies there. The sinkhole leads down into this vast underground region with a starlit ceiling, undoubtedly due to some magical phenomena, along with cyclopean ruins of a civilization long gone. Among the ruins, too, are horrific [[Abominations]] of the deep, and colossal bones of the beasts that led to this society's undoing. The party paved their way forward, fending off both a swarm of giant wasps and a sandstorm, before finding refuge in a small building... a building that is the resting place of a small cohort of mummies. Finding victory, the party rests--tired from the battle, and as ready as ever to get the hell out of here.
