- [[Nhekaush]] and [[Pin]] travel from Valentia by ferry, speaking with a [[Edenic Mechanics#The Redeemers|Redeemer]] named [[Alden]] about life at sea. In the distance, they spot [[Pangaia]]. Meanwhile, [[Turing]] and [[Aule]], who arrived weeks earlier, enjoy a meal at a fine-dining restaurant, learning from the waiter about an armorsmith in the city's eastern quadrant. After splitting the bill, they continue their journey. [[Nhekaush]] and [[Pin]] arrive at the city outskirts and find [[Turing]] and [[Aule]] in a bar, where they plan to meet others for a mission to defeat an Abomination in the north and gain access to [[Edenic Mechanics#Void Fruits|Void Fruits]]. The bartender knocks them unconscious, and they wake up in a forest. They find [[Umak]], barely alive, and discover there are others in the forest too. They learn their enemy is likely a dragon with electric powers. Moving north, they are ambushed by another group but defeat them, sparing and interrogating one survivor. The party continues to the mountain where the dragon resides. [[Aule]] sneaks past the dragon and eventually climbs a Void Tree, consuming a [[Edenic Mechanics#Void Fruits|Void Fruit]]. The party follows, all consuming the fruits, before attempting to escape. However, it was to no avail, as the dragon kills them all in an instant.

## Strong start

> *Today, I would like to welcome you to [[Aravoth]]. The year is 2643 TE, or [[3rd Epoch]]. A planet said to have been the paradise of the gods themselves, once. Orbited by two moons, the [[Shepherds]], at the center of [[Aravoth]] is what many believe to be the birthplace of magic itself--the mysterious continent of [[Eden]]. Impossible landscapes, horrifying foes, and grand riches await all those who challenge [[Eden]]'s hungry maw. All of those who challenge [[Eden]] are reborn, the Redeemers, blessed with magical abilities and a terrifying Penance, for the purpose of fighting their way towards the center of the continent... and reviving a dead god herself.*
> 
> *Beyond the vast landmass of [[Eden]], a vast number of continents dot the landscape, from the desert region of [[Tarsis]] to the mountainous isles of [[Nidavellir]]. The history of [[Aravoth]] is filled with vibrant culture, incredible discoveries, political intrigue, and most of all--war.*
> 
> *Our story, however, begins in an island in the southeast of the continent of [[Ophiri]], on the edge of the Empire of [[Drachma]]'s influence, and closer to [[Eden]] than any other populated area. The island of [[Pangaia]], where colossal forests and wide mountain ranges composed of horrific [[Abominations]] are commonplace, where travelers and traders trudge through the dangerous wilds to seek their fortunes. The city of [[Evemerus]] is located on its southern tip, large in population but even larger in conflict. [[Evemerus]], due to its proximity to [[Eden]], is constantly plagued by [[Abominations]] journeying from its shores. Mercenaries are hired by the crown in order to defend against the less powerful of threats, but outside help is hired for the especially powerful ones.* 
> 
>  *Here, in [[Pangaia]], there are whispers that the influence of [[Abominations]] has allowed for the growth of a Void Tree, a plant lifeform that bears the Void Fruits, allowing one to become a Redeemer themself. Large swathes of warriors from across the world converge on this small nation for the purpose of discovering the Void Tree... and slaying its guardian. This is where our story begins.*
