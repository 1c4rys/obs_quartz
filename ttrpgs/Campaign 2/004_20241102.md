- After the series of unfortunate events that resulted in the party finding themselves in a small watchtower between two colossal bone structures deep in the center of the underground desert world, the party healed up the weakened party members and made their way forward, avoiding that irritating sandstorm to the north. Moving eastward, the party found solace below a large aqueduct following it towards a large temple to the north. In doing so, the party heard a horrific screeching sound, and [[Nhekaush]] noticed his shadow vaguely shifting unnaturally, but believes its just a figment of his imagination. Eventually, the party reaches the temple, utilizing the Thunderbird to reach the top of its base, thereby away from the sandstorm's reach and entering the temple. Inside, the party discovers [[Tiniathuil]], with [[Raphael's Burden]] burden slotted into it, along with [[Azâzêl's Blame]]. Immediately after, the lights go out and the room is plunged into darkness. A fight with two Wraiths began. [[Aule]] swiped at a Wraith with his newly-wielded [[Tiniathuil]], and when that Wraith was slain, a man descended from its form. The other Wraith was slain with the man's help, wherein the man introduced himself... as [[Tyrmorn]]. After explaining that this archipelago was once known as [[Dûdâêl]], he outlines the battle between the [[Watcher]]s and the [[Archon]]s, before suggesting that the only way to escape this place is to slay its ruler, the [[Watcher]] [[ttrpgs/Campaign 2/Notes/Azâzêl]]. He explained how [[ttrpgs/Campaign 2/Notes/Azâzêl]] is trapped in the constellations, and the only way to allow him to escape is to gather all of his armaments, each located at areas of great importance to his story. The next location is, as [[Tyrmorn]] outlines, deep in the city of Azazelir--on the edge of the central island. The party utilizes the Thunderbird to escape, and discovers the colossal gothic city--bathed in true darkness. Descending downwards, the shadow from [[Nhekaush]] leaks onto the Thunderbird, before upon landing near the desiccated castle, the Thunderbird shifts into the form of a Shadow Dragon. Fight!
