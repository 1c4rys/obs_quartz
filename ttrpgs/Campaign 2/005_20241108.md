- The party destroyed the Dragon in a battle, then he flew deep into the abyss below. The party left the weakened form of the Thunderbird behind with [[Tyrmorn]], and began to enter the abyss behind the shadow. The party noticed that this abyss was in fact the dungeons of the castle, and it appeared that traditional light sources did not work, as mad whispers wormed their ways deep into the party's minds. But, upon all members entering, lights become useable now, and a mural appears in the small cage. It depicts a red bearded thief stealing life force from the shadows, a dark temple of a handsome devil wearing a cloak of darkness, standing next to the door of a temple, and the final depicts that same devil reaching towards the darkness. The party determined that, in darkness, the mural became real. After numerous attempts, only after [[Nhekaush]] attempted to access the center door did it became tangible, and he was able to enter, being transported to a garden with a large tree, a sarcophagus, and a large throne. The sarcophagus read 'Here lies [[Sariel]], firstborn of Nihil', and within was a corpse wearing the cloak of shadow from before. Upon donning the cloak, [[Nhekaush]] was thrown from this location, and appeared again within the room. Eventually using the door of the red bearded thief, the party was able to escape. After some hijinks with a clan of crustaceans that had made a small cave their home, the party pushed through and found themselves in a small room, a temple, with a giant corpse. The corpse began to rise to life, imbued with shadow, and roll initiative!
