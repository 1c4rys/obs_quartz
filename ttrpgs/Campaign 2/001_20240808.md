## Strong start

> *The six of you have distant memories of battle from even a young age, your allies decimated around you as you battle in wars against the Orcs to the west and the [[Tarsisian]]s in the desert within the northern continent of [[Ophiri]]. You're a grizzled veteran in the army of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], a vast country controlling the entirety of the southern continent of Meridiem. Recently, you were appointed by King [[Denarion]] himself to lead his Kingsguard. You find yourselves accompanying him upon a carriage throughout the countryside in central Meridiem, as he journeys back to the capital city of [[Empyr]].*

## Scenes

- [ ] Travel by carriage through countryside --> Orc scrimmage
	- [x] The countryside is nothing short of beautiful, a large path before you, surrounded by rolling hills and beautiful meadows. Over the hours, you pass by small towns and minor mountains as you follow parallel to a river to the east. In the carriage with you is the man you were appointed to protect, an older gentleman with a wrinkled face, and a lighter colored robe mixing colors of white with a golden accent, punctuated by a large, radiant sun-like logo in the center. His face feels fatherly, eliciting a sense of warmth in his presence, contrasted by his crooked nose repeatedly broken by battle. Of his features, however, the most noticeable is his fiery red hair, long and heavily braided. 
	- [x] Perception check: DC 18? reveals that ahead, there's a shuffling sound that reminds you of ambushes from previous. It reminds you of Orcs? No, that can't be. This far east?
	- [x] 30 seconds later... they jump out at you, reaching for the driver with a well-made blade. An Orc Warrior surrounded by 6 Orc Brutes, but they undoubtedly look stronger than you remember. Their muscles are larger, veins popping, and you look deep into their eyes and see nothing but an animalistic intent.
	- [x] BATTLE: Party vs. 1 Elite Orc Warchief, 7 Orc Brutes. Party must defend their king. 
- [ ] Final journey to Empyr
	- [x] After the battle, Denarion remarks: "Orcs this far east... this cannot help but inspire worry. To make the journey from [[Abaddon]] to here is thousands of miles. And why weren't they stopped by [[The Wall]]? We must fly, my Soldiers. And determine what we must do. Hurry to Empyr."
	- [x] After another hour or two, a large, multi-leveled, mountainous city is visible on the horizon. There are multiple levels, each tiered by the mountain, with a large castle near the top.  ("[[Thordin]], that mad genius. What a wonderful invention. Perhaps he can journey down from [[Nidavellir]] to reinforce the Wall?)
	- [x] You see multiple guards give you nods as they notice this is the king's entourage, allowing you access to successive elevators, before you find yourself exiting the third and final elevator to what appears to be the castle. 
	- [x] Following him, you make it to his large throneroom, wherein an advisor awaits directly next to the throne. His hair is dark black and greying, slicked back with a tailored robe in similar colors to that of the king, but far less regal: "My liege, the Scouts have sent word by [[Emperor Bird]]. I and the Generals await you in the War-Room."
	- [x] Denarion: "Ah, wonderful. My Kingsguard, I can do without you for the next few hours. You are dismissed. Oh, speaking of which, Commander Sven wished to see you on the training grounds. Best bring your weapons."
- [ ] War room?
	- [ ] Possible, if they really try hard to convince.
	- [ ] Just have him be informed of midnight information, then ask the question. Then they'll be with him up until he goes to bed, then brief downtime.
- [ ] Commander Sven
	- [ ] The party makes it to the training grounds, just one level below as they walk through this medieval looking city. Guards on every corner, well-adorned in armor with that same sun logo, along with a healthy number of artisans travelling throughout the city. It is obvious that this is a very populous city, with great trade and lots of opportunity. You eventually make it to the military quarter, nodding to a guard as he presents the path for you. 
	- [ ] You walk past some barracks and other guards, before you approach a larger training ground, where you see the familiar face of your dear friend, [[Commander Sven]], surrounded by a dozen or so teenagers in oversized armor wielding unwieldy blades. He has dark blonde hair, nearly to his shoulder's in length, and these dark blue eyes. He is muscular and relatively tall, his face scarred from battle. Commander Sven is training one boy, rather short and mousy in appearance at the moment.
	- [ ] Kid: "Step, parry, strike--Damn it!, it just isn't landing."
	- [ ] Commander Sven walks over
	- [ ] Sven: "You certainly have some force to your attacks, but you don't yet have the  fundamentals down. Spread your feet further apart." Sven shows him how "Like so."
	- [ ] The kid moves to brace his feet better

They had such a vigor to them, a might we've never seen. My entire regiment was slaughtered, as we attempted to take back Pentrich. I rode as fast as the wind flies." The Scout bows, and walks away.

And you realize that you recognize this man. His dark blonde, shoulder-length hair, his dark blue eyes... Commander Sven. As your friend's bloody entrails follow as he crawls, he croaks out: "Or... Or-" before his head shatters skull fragments all over the floor ahead, as a large, dark green foot rises from it: the largest Orc you've ever seen.
