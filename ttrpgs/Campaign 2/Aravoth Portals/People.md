## [[Meridiem]]
### Esrithil
Those from the central regions of [[Meridiem]] are referred to as the **Esrithil**. Their faces were angular, and in general were considered beautiful by many of the people of the world. Their build was lean to medium.
> *Metacommentary: Think British but before they became soy*
#### Skin Color
They are generally fair of skin, although it was not uncommon to find an Esrithil that was especially tan.
#### Hair Color
They possess hair colors ranging from brown to blonde to red, although red-hair was most desirable. The majority of people had blonde hair, but the noble class was entirely red. 
#### Eye Color
The majority of their eyes were a dark green, but many had blue or brown eyes as well. 
#### Height
They were a bit taller than most other people, although not by much, ranging from 5'7" to 6'1" in general. 
### Blessed
Those from southern [[Meridiem]] and the [[Isles of the Blessed]] were referred to as the **Blessed**. Their faces were often broad with high cheekbones. Their bodies were relatively evenly distributed with fat, and their build was stocky with shorter limbs.
> *Metacommentary: Think Inuit*
#### Skin Color
Light bronze to pale with slight variation.
#### Hair Color
Typically black, straight, and thick.
#### Eye Color
Dark brown to black.
#### Height
Shorter than the average, ranging from 5'2" to 5'8". 
### Valentine
Those from northern [[Meridiem]], especially [[Cyndra]] and [[Valentia]], were often referred to by the colloquial term the **Valentine**. Their faces were very diverse in shape, although olive-shaped eyes and aquiline noses were common, along with strong jawlines. Their build was considered slender but strong, due to their rich diet of seafood.
> *Metacommentary: Think Mediterranean*
#### Skin Color
Olive to a tan white, to even some lighter black.
#### Hair Color
Dark brown, although auburn and chestnut was also common. 
#### Eye Color
Predominantly brown, some hazel and green.
#### Height
Very average, ranging from 5'3" to 5'9".
### Cambion
Those from far western [[Meridiem]], nearing the [[Orcs]] of [[Abaddon]] and interbreeding with them, were often referred to as the **Cambion**. Their faces were relatively round although they had strong jaws, and it was not uncommon to have some incredibly sharp fangs visible. Their build was lean but muscular, with broad shoulders but stockier legs. 
> *Metacommentary: Think Half-Orc*
#### Skin Color
Subtle grey to ashen tint.
#### Hair Color
Mostly blonde or brown, coarse.
#### Eye Color
Dark green or blue, with occasional brown
#### Height
Ranging from 6'0" to 6'6", taller than average by a lot.
## [[Ophiri]]
### Drachmi
> *Main article: [[Drachmi]]*

The ethnicity present in mostly eastern [[Ophiri]] towards [[The Splintered Sea]] were referred to as the **Drachmi**. Their faces were relatively angular, with high cheekbones and a slightly  rounded chin. They had a sturdy but not muscular frame. 
> *Metacommentary: Think Russian*
#### Skin Color
Ranged from fair to a light olive.
#### Hair Color
Brown to a dark black, with some blonde.
#### Eye Color
Mostly blue or grey.
#### Height
Average, from 5'5" to 5'11" on average.
### Northern Tarshish
Those living from central to northern [[Tarsis]] were known as the **Northern Tarshish**. They had oval-shaped faces with high-cheekbones, full lips, and almond-shaped eyes. Their build was slender and graceful, lean but toned.
> *Metacommentary: Think Arab*
#### Skin Color
Warm bronze to light brown, with some golden or sun-kissed undertones.
#### Hair Color
Predominantly dark brown to black; thick, wavy or curly
#### Eye Color
Rich brown to amber, rare occurrences of blue or hazel
#### Height
Medium to tall, 5'6" to 6'0.
### Southern Tarshish
Those from the southern reaches of [[Tarsis]], closer to the equator, were referred to as the **Southern Tarshish**. Their face was broad with rounded features, fuller lips, and wider noses. Their build was strong and athletic. 
> *Metacommentary: Think Sub-Saharan African*
#### Skin Color
Dark brown to deep ebony.
#### Hair Color
Mostly black, often tight coiled or kinky, natural volume.
#### Eye Color
Mostly deep brown or black, some rich amber.
#### Height
Medium to tall, 5'7" to 6'1".
### Nidavellirian
Formed from interbreeding between the [[Dwarves]] of [[Nidavellir]] and other people, they are referred to as the **Souther Nidavellirian** and are mostly present in northern [[Ophiri]]. They had angular faces with narrow noses and strong jawlines. Their build was lean with long limbs.
> *Metacommentary: Think Northern European, Norse*
#### Skin Color
Extremely fair, almost pale, with some having an alabaster undertone.
#### Hair Color
Predominantly light blonde, sometimes platinum or icy blonde, occasional light ash brown or silver hues.
#### Eye Color
Icy blue, grey, or pale green.
#### Height
Tall, ranging from 5'9" to 6'3".
### Sylvan
Also present in northern [[Ophiri]], particularly the north-northwest, the **Sylvan** people are those interbred with [[Elves]] over the years. Their faces are delicate and angular, with high cheekbones and slightly pointed chins. Their build is lithe and slender. 
> *Metacommentary: Think Half-Elf*
#### Skin Color
Fair to light olive with a smooth complexion.
#### Hair Color
Dark brown to black, with some blonde, copper, or even some silvery-white.
#### Eye Color
Hazel, emerald green, or deep brown.
#### Height
Taller than average, 5'8" to 6'2".
## Other
### Easterners
Those born in [[Kisia]] were referred to as the **Easterners**, and appeared wildly different than the rest of the world. Their faces were ovals with a soft jawline and clear skin. Their build was lean but not very muscular. 
> *Metacommentary: Think Japanese mixed with a bit of Mongol*
#### Skin Color
Light to medium with a yellow or golden undertone, often slightly tanned.
#### Hair Color
Predominantly straight black or dark brown, often tied in topknots or intricate styles.
#### Eye Color
Dark brown to black, with almond-shaped eyes.
#### Height
Below average height, but not by much; typically 5'4" to 5'8".
