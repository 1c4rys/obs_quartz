## Sentient Languages
### Meridice Languages
The Meridice languages are languages found upon the continent of [[Meridiem]] and originating from the same original language, but, in most cases, not related to [[Old Dawnic]].
#### New Dawnic
**New Dawnic** is the main language spoken across much of the world and especially [[Esrith]]. It is the primary language of [[Esrith]], the [[Free City of Letnya]], and also widely spoken in the [[Isles of the Blessed]]. It is considered to be based off of [[Old Dawnic]].

> *Metacommentary: Think English/Common of this world. Most people will speak this or Drachmi.*

#### Valentine
**Valentine** is the main language of [[Valentia]]. It uses the same script as New Dawnic. The third most spoken language, it is most spoken during trade and in much of northern [[Meridiem]] and southern [[Ophiri]], especially by [[Halflings]]. 

> *Metacommentary: Think Spanish.*

#### Enochian
**Enochian** is the primary language of the [[Orcs]] in [[Abaddon]]. It is a far more guttural version of New Dawnic, with many similar words and grammar. However, it also includes much inspiration from an ancient form of Elder Speech, much to the confusion of linguists. 
### Ancient Languages
The Ancient languages are languages written thousands of years ago by the elder races. 
#### Elder Speech
**Elder Speech** is spoken by the few [[Elves]] remaining in the world, but as of now almost completely dead. However, due to the great historical benefit found by speaking Elder Speech, many still choose to learn it. 

> *Metacommentary: Think traditional Tolkien-esque Elvish*

#### Dvergramál
**Dvergramál** (pronounced "vurr-gurr-mall") is the language spoken by the [[Dwarves]] of [[Nidavellir]], viewed as their mother tongue. However, in recent times, New Dawnic has been adopted by most younger [[Dwarves]], with Dvergramál becoming a relic of the past only used for religious reasons and for runecrafting. 

> *Metacommentary: Think Norse*

#### Old Dawnic
> *Main article: [[Old Dawnic]]*

**Old Dawnic** is the language spoken in the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] and presumably by the [[Archon]]s in the faith of [[Dawnism]]. It has since been lost, with recovered ruins seemingly being complete nonsense. It is the only language in ruins across [[Eden]]. 
### Other
#### Drachmi
**Drachmi** is the language used in [[Drachma]]. It is very similar to Enochian in the usage of words from Elder Speech, although it is far more higher-class. It is also written in a unique script. It is the main language of all of [[Drachma]] and therefore the second-most spoken language. It can be heard both in the motherland, in the [[Isles of the Blessed]], in some reaches of [[Tarsis]], and [[Cyndra]]. 

> *Metacommentary: Think Greek*
#### Tarsish
**Tarsish** is the main language of western [[Ophiri]], in the desert region of [[Tarsis]]. It has similarities to many of the Meridice languages, but uses a different script. It is thought to be the closest language to the true [[Old Dawnic]]. It is spoken only in [[Tarsis]] and some portions of [[Valentia]]. 

> *Metacommentary: Think Phoenician/Arabic*

#### Kisian
**Kisian** (pron. "kish-uhn") is the language spoken by [[Kitsune]] in [[Kisia]] and abroad. It has an entirely unique script and should only be learned if one plans on visiting--illegally--the nation of [[Kisia]]. Or, if you have [[Kitsune]] friends.

> *Metacommentary: Think Japanese*

## PF2e Languages: Abominations
> *Metacommentary: All languages here are traditional PF2E languages. They will only be spoken by monsters.*

### Common Languages
#### Fey
**Fey** is spoken by fey creatures, centaurs, and plant-based lifeforms. It has a fluid and whimsical structure, with strong ties to nature.
#### Goblin
**Goblin** is spoken by goblins, hobgoblins, and bugbears. It is a rough and direct language, used frequently in battle and command.
#### Sakvroth
**Sakvroth** is spoken by subterranean civilizations and serpentfolk. It is a secretive and hissing language, often used in covert communications.
### Uncommon Languages
#### Aklo
**Aklo** is spoken by evil fey and otherworldly monsters. It is a cryptic and unsettling language, rarely understood by outsiders.
#### Chthonian
**Chthonian** is spoken by demons. It is a chaotic and destructive language, often used in dark rituals and magic.
#### Diabolic
**Diabolic** is spoken by devils. It is a structured and precise language, used in infernal contracts and deals.
#### Empyrean
**Empyrean** is spoken by angels and celestial beings. It is a radiant and divine language, often heard in religious rituals and prayers.
#### Kholo
**Kholo** is the language spoken by the hyena-like kholos. It is a primal and savage tongue, rarely spoken outside their clans.
#### Necril
**Necril** is spoken by ghouls and intelligent undead. It is a cold and eerie language, often associated with necromancy and the undead.
#### Petran
**Petran** is spoken by earth elemental creatures. It has a slow and steady tone, resembling the movement of stone and earth.
#### Pyric
**Pyric** is spoken by fire elemental creatures. It is a crackling and intense language, often associated with heat and flames.
#### Shadowtongue
**Shadowtongue** is spoken by netherworld creatures. It is a dark and secretive language, often used in shadowy dealings.
#### Sussuran
**Sussuran** is spoken by air elemental creatures and flying beings. It has a soft and flowing tone, resembling the movement of wind.
#### Thalassic
**Thalassic** is spoken by aquatic creatures and water elementals. It has a fluid and rhythmic sound, akin to the flow of water.
