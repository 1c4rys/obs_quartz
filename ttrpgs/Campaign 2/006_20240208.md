- The party fought their final battle against the [[Shadow Avatar|Shadow Beast]] at the end of Sariel's Temple, finally claiming victory once and for all. Aule was rewarded with [[Azâzêl's Sins]], while the beast dropped a multitude of Shadow runes. Finally, within the pocket of the [[Cloak of Sariel]], [[Nhekaush]] discovered a book titled [[On the Metaphysics of Severance]], which could teach him to learn the spell *Simulacrum*. Upon the boss's death, it teleported the party to the [[Realm at the End of Time]], a depressing place bathed in grey. They met a man who they later learned was [[Koart]], who, in return for some blood to feed his gun, guided the party to a large mountain in the distance. Upon ascending the mountain, Aule realized that this place was identical to his homeland, [[The Silver Havens]]. At the top of the mountain, the party discovered a small temple, and Koart left. After battling against some gargoyles on the path, the party tricked the talking gargoyle Butternut into letting them in, which resulted in his death. Butternut mentioned that his master's name was Samiel, which is the same name as the man who slew [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]]. Within the temple, the party discovered [[Janus's Gift]], a mirror that allowed them to view into an opposite dimension. Utilizing the mirror, the party slew the statue guarding the hall, and discovered the [[Azâzêl's Banishment]]. Finally, the party collected a drop of ichor from the statue, and placed it within the mirror, which thereby turned into a large mirror that the party then used to escape... only to appear in the temple, in a realm of vast, white nothingness.
