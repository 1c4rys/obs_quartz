| 1            | August 08, 2024    | In the late [[2nd Epoch]], the Kingsguard protects Dawn King [[Denarion (ruler)\|Denarion]], King of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], as they journey throughout the countryside, returning to the capital, [[Empyr]]. An Orc party ambushes the party, but are swiftly beaten down. One Orc is interrogated by Lars, wherein it is revealed that the Orcs have taken over a town nearby and have made the journey to the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] from [[Abaddon]]. Carson discovers a tattoo of two moons in alignment. This greatly worries the king, and he states that you must hurry back to [[Empyr]]. After returning, a young Commander Sven has Andrewbot duel him in combat in order to show how a true battle is fought. Andrewbot loses, but the recruits learned greatly nonetheless. After Carson hides information pertaining to the moons in [[Empyr]]'s library, the party falls asleep. They are awaken by Cmdr. Sven, who informs them that the King wishes to see them. At this point, a scout informs the party that the Orcs have pushed too far, before being himself killed by encroaching Orcs. In the throne room, the party defends the King from the Orcs, but it isn't enough, and the King's advisor's form gives way for that of [[Neton (ruler)\|Neton]]: A shadowy form of an Orc wields a large, shadow blade and stabs Denarion through the heart. > - Denarion: "Sam-Samiel... why...? After all she did for us... you ally with her killer?"> - Samiel: "You don't understand. She was dead. It was here. He will escape, Cassiel. The world will see. To the next galaxy I go."> - At this moment, a dozen more Orcs appear, as they file into the room, outnumbering you greatly.> - A deep white aura begins to pulse from the King's body, going up the blade and resorbing itself into Samiel's body. His body gradually becomes more corporeal, as his skin shifts to a dark red color and as his from grows more feminine. He grows dark black horns, and large wings. He quickly wraps himself in shadow, as he places a large hat above his head, and a portal appears. In the portal, a subterranean city can be seen, with a store in front. A small halfling seems to approach it, a comically large hat visible within. |
| 2            | September 25, 2024 | [[Nhekaush]] and [[Pin]] travel from Valentia by ferry, speaking with a [[Edenic Mechanics#The Redeemers\|Redeemer]] named [[Alden]] about life at sea. In the distance, they spot [[Pangaia]]. Meanwhile, [[Turing]] and [[Aule]], who arrived weeks earlier, enjoy a meal at a fine-dining restaurant, learning from the waiter about an armorsmith in the city's eastern quadrant. After splitting the bill, they continue their journey. [[Nhekaush]] and [[Pin]] arrive at the city outskirts and find [[Turing]] and [[Aule]] in a bar, where they plan to meet others for a mission to defeat an Abomination in the north and gain access to [[Edenic Mechanics#Void Fruits\|Void Fruits]]. The bartender knocks them unconscious, and they wake up in a forest. They find [[Umak]], barely alive, and discover there are others in the forest too. They learn their enemy is likely a dragon with electric powers. Moving north, they are ambushed by another group but defeat them, sparing and interrogating one survivor. The party continues to the mountain where the dragon resides. [[Aule]] sneaks past the dragon and eventually climbs a Void Tree, consuming a [[Edenic Mechanics#Void Fruits\|Void Fruit]]. The party follows, all consuming the fruits, before attempting to escape. However, it was to no avail, as the dragon kills them all in an instant.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 3            | October 12, 2024   | After being slain by the monstrous Sky Dragon, the party found themselves deep in the realm of [[Eden]], in a region bathed in night and bearing nothing but the most monstrous of [[Abominations]]. On a small island, far away from the mainland, the party meets a young man named [[Makoto]], and learns of the name of their Penances. Based off of a rune in the constellations, [[Umak]] decides to jump off the side of the island in a literal leap of faith, where he is saved by a colossal Thunderbird that only responds in Enochian. Safe from death, he reunites with the party and begins to journey to a desert island in the distance. In the center of this colossal desert island is a sinkhole, and the constellations above seem to hint once again towards taking the risk of plunging into the depths, and therefore the party flies there. The sinkhole leads down into this vast underground region with a starlit ceiling, undoubtedly due to some magical phenomena, along with cyclopean ruins of a civilization long gone. Among the ruins, too, are horrific [[Abominations]] of the deep, and colossal bones of the beasts that led to this society's undoing. The party paved their way forward, fending off both a swarm of giant wasps and a sandstorm, before finding refuge in a small building... a building that is the resting place of a small cohort of mummies. Finding victory, the party rests--tired from the battle, and as ready as ever to get the hell out of here.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 4            | November 02, 2024  | After the series of unfortunate events that resulted in the party finding themselves in a small watchtower between two colossal bone structures deep in the center of the underground desert world, the party healed up the weakened party members and made their way forward, avoiding that irritating sandstorm to the north. Moving eastward, the party found solace below a large aqueduct following it towards a large temple to the north. In doing so, the party heard a horrific screeching sound, and [[Nhekaush]] noticed his shadow vaguely shifting unnaturally, but believes its just a figment of his imagination. Eventually, the party reaches the temple, utilizing the Thunderbird to reach the top of its base, thereby away from the sandstorm's reach and entering the temple. Inside, the party discovers [[Tiniathuil]], with [[Raphael's Burden]] burden slotted into it, along with [[Azâzêl's Blame]]. Immediately after, the lights go out and the room is plunged into darkness. A fight with two Wraiths began. [[Aule]] swiped at a Wraith with his newly-wielded [[Tiniathuil]], and when that Wraith was slain, a man descended from its form. The other Wraith was slain with the man's help, wherein the man introduced himself... as [[Tyrmorn]]. After explaining that this archipelago was once known as [[Dûdâêl]], he outlines the battle between the [[Watcher]]s and the [[Archon]]s, before suggesting that the only way to escape this place is to slay its ruler, the [[Watcher]] [[ttrpgs/Campaign 2/Notes/Azâzêl]]. He explained how [[ttrpgs/Campaign 2/Notes/Azâzêl]] is trapped in the constellations, and the only way to allow him to escape is to gather all of his armaments, each located at areas of great importance to his story. The next location is, as [[Tyrmorn]] outlines, deep in the city of Azazelir--on the edge of the central island. The party utilizes the Thunderbird to escape, and discovers the colossal gothic city--bathed in true darkness. Descending downwards, the shadow from [[Nhekaush]] leaks onto the Thunderbird, before upon landing near the desiccated castle, the Thunderbird shifts into the form of a Shadow Dragon. Fight!                                                                                  |
| 5            | November 08, 2024  | The party destroyed the Dragon in a battle, then he flew deep into the abyss below. The party left the weakened form of the Thunderbird behind with [[Tyrmorn]], and began to enter the abyss behind the shadow. The party noticed that this abyss was in fact the dungeons of the castle, and it appeared that traditional light sources did not work, as mad whispers wormed their ways deep into the party's minds. But, upon all members entering, lights become useable now, and a mural appears in the small cage. It depicts a red bearded thief stealing life force from the shadows, a dark temple of a handsome devil wearing a cloak of darkness, standing next to the door of a temple, and the final depicts that same devil reaching towards the darkness. The party determined that, in darkness, the mural became real. After numerous attempts, only after [[Nhekaush]] attempted to access the center door did it became tangible, and he was able to enter, being transported to a garden with a large tree, a sarcophagus, and a large throne. The sarcophagus read 'Here lies [[Sariel]], firstborn of Nihil', and within was a corpse wearing the cloak of shadow from before. Upon donning the cloak, [[Nhekaush]] was thrown from this location, and appeared again within the room. Eventually using the door of the red bearded thief, the party was able to escape. After some hijinks with a clan of crustaceans that had made a small cave their home, the party pushed through and found themselves in a small room, a temple, with a giant corpse. The corpse began to rise to life, imbued with shadow, and roll initiative!                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 6            | February 08, 2025  | The party fought their final battle against the [[Shadow Avatar\|Shadow Beast]] at the end of Sariel's Temple, finally claiming victory once and for all. Aule was rewarded with [[Azâzêl's Sins]], while the beast dropped a multitude of Shadow runes. Finally, within the pocket of the [[Cloak of Sariel]], [[Nhekaush]] discovered a book titled [[On the Metaphysics of Severance]], which could teach him to learn the spell *Simulacrum*. Upon the boss's death, it teleported the party to the [[Realm at the End of Time]], a depressing place bathed in grey. They met a man who they later learned was [[Koart]], who, in return for some blood to feed his gun, guided the party to a large mountain in the distance. Upon ascending the mountain, Aule realized that this place was identical to his homeland, [[The Silver Havens]]. At the top of the mountain, the party discovered a small temple, and Koart left. After battling against some gargoyles on the path, the party tricked the talking gargoyle Butternut into letting them in, which resulted in his death. Butternut mentioned that his master's name was Samiel, which is the same name as the man who slew [[Denarion (ruler)\|Denarion]]. Within the temple, the party discovered [[Janus's Gift]], a mirror that allowed them to view into an opposite dimension. Utilizing the mirror, the party slew the statue guarding the hall, and discovered the [[Azâzêl's Banishment]]. Finally, the party collected a drop of ichor from the statue, and placed it within the mirror, which thereby turned into a large mirror that the party then used to escape... only to appear in the temple, in a realm of vast, white nothingness.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
