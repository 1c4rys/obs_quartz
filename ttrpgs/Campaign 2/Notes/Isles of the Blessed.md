# [[Isles of the Blessed]]
## Overview
The **Isles of the Blessed** are a collection of islands south of [[Meridiem]] full of history and even more flush with treasure. This presence of treasure has attracted the navy of the [[Free City of Letnya]] and that of [[Drachma]] to hunt it down, although they are constantly deterred by the many pirates of this region. [[Abominations]] in [[Nexomancy#Automatons|Automaton]] forms were sent here, and therefore are the main threat in the region. 

The so-called isles are actually incredibly large, naturally-forming spires, thousands of feet above sea level. Thus, the main form of transportation is through flying ships known as Sky Skiffs.
