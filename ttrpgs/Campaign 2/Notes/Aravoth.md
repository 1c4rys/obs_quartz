# [[Aravoth|Aravoth]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Aravoth Image]]
## Overview
Aravoth is the third planet from the Sun in the Aravothil System, the only known habitable world in the system. Aravoth is orbited by two moons, the [[Shepherds]], and was once orbited by an ancient moon known as [[Aeternum]]. In addition, a large quantity of rings orbit Aravoth, colloquially referred to as the Grandchildren. Every Winter Solstice, the Shepherds pass one another, shepherding the Grandchildren to create a beautiful celestial event known as the [[Arc]].  

Aravoth is home to the [[Aravothil]] and the chief deity of [[Elohim]]. 
## Geography
Aravoth is approximately the same size as the galactic average. Aravoth consists of several continents and islands, including [[Meridiem]], [[Ophiri]], [[The Silver Havens]], [[Nidavellir]], [[Kisia]], [[Tarsis]], [[Abaddon]], and [[Isles of the Blessed]]. 

Following the alignment of the [[Shepherds]] in 2639 TE, the mysterious and deeply magical continent of [[Eden]] appeared off the eastern shore of [[Ophiri]], and [[The Silver Havens]] was mysteriously annihilated. 

In addition, the largest ocean is [[The Splintered Sea]], with the Ocean of Tarsis, the Northern Channel, the Indigo Strait, and Rhiannon's Rift within it.
### Continents
- **[[Meridiem]]**: A colossal continent to the southwest, a diverse landscape inhabited by members of every race and nearly every culture. 
- **[[Ophiri]]**: The largest continent, it is composed of almost entirely desert except for the mountainous east which is covered in tundra, mostly composed of Humans and some Halflings, along with Dwarves from [[Nidavellir]].
- [[The Silver Havens]]: The smallest and and northernmost continent, it is entirely composed of Elves. 
- [[Nidavellir]]: A collection of isles in the deep north, and the location wherein the wide majority of Dwarves reside. Heavily mountainous. 
- [[Kisia]]: Referred to as Kyushima by its inhabitants, it is an archipelago to the southeast, and deeply isolationist. Little is known beyond that.
- [[Tarsis]]: A desert region in western [[Ophiri]] composed of dark-skinned Humans. Deeply arid, but just as deep in history.
- [[Abaddon]]: A volcanic and deadly location in western [[Meridiem]], where the Orcs reside. It is now separated by [[Meridiem]]-proper by [[The Wall]]. 
- [[Isles of the Blessed]]: Its name deeply ironic, the [[Isles of the Blessed]] are a string of islands south of [[Meridiem]] that is home to incredible trade and great production of herbs like sugar. However, it is also the home of great conflict due to an epidemic of pirating. 
## History
### The 1st Epoch
> *Main article: [[1st Epoch]]*

The **1st Epoch** was a period of time lasting from an indeterminate period of time ago until around a million years ago. Little is known beyond that life existed only in [[Eden]]. 
### The 2nd Epoch
> *Main article: [[2nd Epoch]]*

The **2nd Epoch** was the period wherein life was first evident outside of [[Eden]], beginning in 0 SE and ending around 100,000 years later. The [[Dawn Kingdoms]] were established and destroyed, and [[Nidavellir]] and [[The Silver Havens]] were first founded as true, united nations.

### The 3rd Epoch
> *Main article: [[3rd Epoch]]*

The **3rd Epoch**, also referred to as **The Present Epoch** was the time period that started after the fall of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], in 0 TE, and continues up until the present. A time of constant struggle, it was also a time wherein the continent of [[Eden]] first rose and [[The Silver Havens]] were destroyed.
