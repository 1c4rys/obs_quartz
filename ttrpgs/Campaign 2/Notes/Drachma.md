# [[Drachma|Drachma]]
>[!infobox]
>![[Drachma Image]]
## Overview
Drachma is a vast theocratic empire on the continent of [[Ophiri]] in [[Aravoth]], primarily composed of [[Humans]] with a significant slave population of [[Orcs]]. It is the second-largest economy in [[Aravoth]] and possesses one of the world's largest militaries. Drachma's recent expansions into [[Tarsis]] and [[Cyndra]] have stretched its resources thin, leading to internal conflicts. The empire's culture, government, and social structure are deeply intertwined with the [[Helladism]] religion.
## History
Approximately 500 years ago, Drach I converted to Helladism, transforming Drachma into a theocracy within a generation. Subsequent rulers systematically rewrote the nation's history to align with Helladist doctrine. In recent years, Drachma's growth has stagnated, prompting renewed expansionist efforts. Within the last century, Drachma conquered most of Cyndra on the continent of Meridiem, and within the last decade annexed Tarsis, a loosely organized nation to the west following the [[Elohim]] faith. These conquests, while expanding Drachma's territory, have led to significant internal strife and resource strain.
## Social
Drachma has a population of 68 million, the largest of any country in the world. The wide majority is Human, although there is a strong Orc slave population and a hearty artisan class of [[Dwarves]] migrated from [[Nidavellir]]. There is also a small group of [[Halflings]], although the majority live within [[Valentia]]. There is a very strict social hierarchy within Drachma, based on religious standing, with those higher in the religion possessing more power, and vice versa. Drachma is also the largest and one of the few remaining nations to possess widescale slavery, utilizing their large Orc population to enable large city planning and conquering. Conquered populations in primarily Tarsis face great discrimination and cultural suppression, as the [[Drachmi]] attempt religious conversion to little success. 
## Politics
Drachma has a theocratic government currently led by [[Drach VII]]. It is believed that only the Drach directly serves [[Hella]], meaning all followers of Helladism serve Drach and only Drach. The religious and political hierarchy is:

In conquered territories, religious leaders serve as regional governors, directly appointed by the Drach. Recent expansions, however, have lead to widespread political instability and resource management challenges. Drachma is located near the Isles of Nidavellir, which due to its chaotic waters, remains largely inaccessible by Drachma and independent, one of the few nearby countries to remain unconquered except for [[Valentia]] and [[Esrith]]. Although, recently, the establishment of [[Pytheae]] allowed for the empire to gain its first foothold in [[Nidavellir]], although an isle on the outskirts.


## Economic
Drachma is the second-largest economy in Aravoth, second only to Esrith. Drachma is a major exporter of hemp, flax, linen, leather, grain, leather, and scrap iron, while they import luxury items, Valentine wine, sugar, silk and woolen goods, and dyestuffs, mostly from Valentia and Esrith. Their currency is the Dein, which is stamped with a profile of Hella. Drachma possesses trade relations with many of the countries of Aravoth, exluding [[Kisia]]. 
## Religion
The state religion is Helladism, which is deeply intertwined with the government. Drach is the leader of Helladism, and a complex religious hierarchy makes up the Drachmi government. Presently, the people of Tarsis follow strictly in the faith of Elohim, leading to widespread conflict as the Drachmi vie for religious conversion. 
## Military
Drachma possesses the largest military in the world, although their lack of technological ability means they would likely lose in total war against many of the former [[Dawn Kingdoms]], particularly Esrith. However, Drachma prefers opportunistic expansion over such wars, although they are currently stretched very thin over recent conquests in Cyndra and Tarsis. Much of Drachma's military is currently fighting minor battles with militias in Tarsis, as they fight for independence. 

They wield a third of all existing [[Nexusborns]], and are behind many of the other nations of the world but are rapidly expanding throughout [[Eden]] and collecting many powerful [[Edenic Mechanics#Fruits|Fruits]] to use as a deterrent against other nations.
