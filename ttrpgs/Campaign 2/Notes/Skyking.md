# [[Skyking]]
## Overview
**Skyking** is a Fruit discovered within [[Dûdâêl]], forged from the Sky Tyrant’s lightning-infused claw. The weapon manifests as a jagged greatsword crackling with trapped storm energy.

## Info 
### Archetype
**Offensive**: Greatsword  
### Level
6
## Description
*“The heavens themselves trembled as his claws split the sky.”*  

## Intrinsic
### Storm’s Retribution
After landing a critical hit with Skyking, the wielder summons a **lightning strike** (15-ft line, 3d6 electricity damage, DC 20 Reflex). The sword becomes **drained 1** for 1 minute, reducing its damage die by one size.
