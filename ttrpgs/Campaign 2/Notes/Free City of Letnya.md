# [[Free City of Letnya]]
## Overview
The **Free City of Letnya** was a city-state located in former Telran in southeastern [[Meridiem]], bordering the [[Isles of the Blessed]]. It is the second-largest city within [[Meridiem]]. 

Letnya is the southernmost major city in the eastern hemisphere, and acts as a buffer against the [[Isles of the Blessed]]. They are paid handsomely by their ally, [[Esrith]], for this duty. 
## History
The region Letnya now lies in was once part of the [[Dawn Kingdoms|Dawn Kingom]] of Telran. Following the catastrophe that resulted in the destruction of the kingdoms, the country slowly fell apart, until it was reduced to a single settlement--the Free City of Letnya.

In modern day, Letnya now holds great political importance in the region. It's the largest city on the shores of the [[Isles of the Blessed]]. As such, any ships sailing through it stop there to rest and resupply, and there's always trade occurring.
