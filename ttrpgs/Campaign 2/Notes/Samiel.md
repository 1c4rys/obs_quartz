# [[Samiel]]
## Overview
**Samiel** is a being who ruled the [[Orcs]] and slew [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]].  %%One of the seven children of [[Aeternum]] and the bearer of the [[Splinter]] of [[Might]], they are the patron god of the [[Halflings]], and are represented in religion by [[Neton (Archon)|Neton]] in [[Dawnism]].%% 

Although the temple in the [[Realm at the End of Time]] was dedicated to him, his current location is unknown. 
## History
%%A member of the [[Precursors]], they were gifted with the [[Splinter]] of [[Might]] by [[Aeternum]] during [[The Splintering]]. Samiel would later betray [[Aeternum]], leading an army of [[Orcs]] to kill [[Cassiel]], his brother. He stole the [[Splinter]] of [[Perseverance]] for himself, as well.%%

%%His twin sibling, [[Gabriel]], is someone of deep importance for him. He believes that by working for [[Nihil]], he can be with them forever--just the two of them. This relationship blurs the line of platonic and romantic.%%

His conversation with [[Cassiel]]/[[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]] before eventually slaying him involved abandoning "her" and instead following "it". It is unknown what this meant.
