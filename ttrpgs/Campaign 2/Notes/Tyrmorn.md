# [[Tyrmorn]]
## Overview
**Tyrmorn** is a [[Cosmology of Eynsof#Planeswalkers|Planeswalker]] from an adjacent galaxy, taking the epithet of [[Noah]]. He was the herald of war and an ally of [[Haniel]][^1]. He was once an ally of [[Koart]]. 

He assisted the party in the journey in [[Dûdâêl]], appearing after a Wraith was slain by [[Aule]]'s [[Tiniathuil]] in the large desert island of [[Dûdâêl]]. Currently, he is taking care of [[ttrpgs/Campaign 2/Notes/Azâzêl]]'s Thunderbird within the capital city of [[Dûdâêl]].

[^1]: [[Haniel's Message]]
## Description
He is a vaguely [[Elves|Elvish]] monk, bald and wearing nothing but a robe and large snowboots. He wields a large spear when in combat. He appears aged, around his early 60s if he were a [[Humans|Human]].
