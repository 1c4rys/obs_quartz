# [[Valentia]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Valentia Image]]
## Overview
**Valentia**, formerly known as Verden, is an elective monarchy established in the mid-[[2nd Epoch]] in northeastern [[Meridiem]]. It is the oldest nation with a continuous government in all of [[Aravoth]]. Valentia is considered the cultural and learning capital of the world, along with being a fiercely effective commerce state. 
## History
### Founding
Verden was founded by escaped slaves from somewhere in primitive [[Meridiem]], swearing a vow that no one in their city would ever be a slave or a slaver. This became the first of many laws within Verden. Since it was established, it has not grown in land size, and the population has remained relatively steady due to their large quantity of rich, old citizens.

Sometime during this time, the swordfighting technique of **Tempo** was invented. 
### As a Dawn Kingdom
Years after their founding, the Dawn King [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]] from the [[Meridiem]] nation of Esrith began to apply pressure to surrounding states, including Verden. After many diplomatic meetings, and entirely bloodlessly, Verden agreed to enter the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] out of necessity, for they needed the protection of the military against invading forces from [[Tarsis]], but informed [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]] that they would never allow a slaver to enter their borders.

Verden enjoyed being a member of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], and were great supports of the Dawn King. With their newfound strong military, they were able to beat back [[Tarsis]]h invaders, while still being allowed to build their universities and remain out of much of the conflict of the world. Occasionally, the Dawn King would ask the Arch-Vicar to broker deals with eastern [[Ophiri]], along with building ships for the navy of the nation, but otherwise was relatively hands-off. 

When the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] fell with the [[Brothers' War]], the Arch-Vicar sent the wide majority of his army to [[Empyr]] to battle back the [[Orcs]]. With their help, the [[Orcs]] were swiftly massacred and pushed back. Multiple [[Nexusborns]] were acquired during this battle, but the Arch-Vicar decreed that these would be provided to the former royal family in order to ensure they would not fall in the coming years. 
### Independence
Verden renamed themselves to Valentia, for an unknown reason. Due to the lack of military defense from the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], Valentia was forced to hire mercenaries from [[Tarsis]] with their vast quantity of wealth, resulting in a large portion of [[Valentia]] to be composed of [[People#Northern Tarshish|Tars]][[People#Southern Tarshish|hish]] in modern day. This was generally enough to defend against most of the threats at the time--[[Abominations]] and bandits from [[Meridiem]]. Many [[Orcs]] remained here after the [[Brothers' War]], too, and the Arch-Vicar allowed them to stay--begrudgingly.

Sometime during this time, Valentia began to trade heavily for spices from [[Tarsis]], along with other exotic goods, increasing their wealth twofold. 
### Modern Day
When [[Esrith]] was reunited, Valentia was the largest nation in [[Meridiem]], so were viewed as a great threat to [[Esrith]]. However, there was no blood shed in the Valentine theatre of [[Meridiem's Second War]], since both sides were willing to let the other exist as long as [[Esrith]] swore to protect Valentia from harm and Valentia would provide [[Uther]] with half of their [[Nexusborns]] for the next decade, along with building a few dozen ships for [[Esrith]]'s navy.

A blot on Valentia's peaceful history, an uprising occurred in the capital city of **Concordia** in the early 2400s TE that resulted in the destruction of [[House Siurana]]. In its space, House Trastámara hoisted themselves into power. Some argue that House Trastámara caused this uprising, although others believe that they simply took advantage of the opportunity. Regardless, they still hold control of Concordia to this day, their current Countess being [[Jacqueline de Trastámara]], a [[Humans|Human]]. Some say that a member survived this massacre, but there are no confirmed reports. 

More recently, the current Arch-Vicar has reached the end of his life, a [[Halflings|Halfling]] named [[Guillaume Alighieri|Guillaume]] of House Alighieri. Therefore, the bloody conflict that usually ensues upon the death of an Arch-Vicar is ramping up, with Countess [[Jacqueline de Trastámara|Trastámara]] vying for the position herself.  
## Government
The government of Valentia is led by the Arch-Vicar, a citizen of Valentia elected by the nation's Counts and gentry who serves for life. The choosing process is intense, commonly turning to violence. It is not uncommon that some might attempt to cut the Arch-Vicar's life short to effect a change in policy.

Beyond the Arch-Vicar and the Counts, who are outside of the hierarchy, there are five **guilds** and numerous smaller ones led by a given family. These guilds were separated by their specific industry. The following are the five largest guilds:
- **Cofradía Mercantil** - (trans. Merchant Guild) the guild composed of merchants and traders, mostly seafaring.
- **Hermandad Artesana** - (trans. Artisan Brotherhood) the guild composed of artisans and craftmen, mostly skilled workers such as goldsmiths, sculptors, painters, and architects.
- **Gremio Constructor** - (trans. Builder Guild) the guild composed of builders and mason, mostly those responsible for the construction and maintenance of Valentia’s impressive structures—cathedrals, city walls, bridges, and palaces.
- **Orden Textil** - (trans. Order of the Clothiers) the guild composed of clothiers and textile workers, cloth-makers, dyers, tailors, and weavers. 
- **Cámara Bancaria** - (trans. Banking Chamber) the guild composed of bankers and monylenders, overseeing all matters of finance, loans, and currency. 

Of these, the one currently with the most power is the Cofradia Mercantil, benefitting extensively from the increased trade from [[Eden]]. 
## Culture
Valentia is renowned for its grand architecture, with ornate cathedrals, palaces, and civic buildings adorning its cities. Wealthy Valentines favor somber hues like charcoal grey, deep purple, and near-black blues, while duelists and young nobles often dress in bright, eye-catching colors. 

The patronage system flourishes in Valentia, with noble houses and wealthy merchants sponsoring artists, poets, and scholars. This has led to a renaissance in painting, sculpture, and literature. 

Education is highly prized, with prestigious universities attracting scholars from across Aravoth. The **University of Concordia**, housed in a magnificent complex of Gothic-inspired buildings, is particularly famous for its schools of law, medicine, and philosophy.

Valentia's famous courtesans are celebrated in song and showered with gifts. Each has her own ornate barge and retinue of servants. They often host salons where intellectuals, artists, and politicians gather to discuss ideas and enjoy poetry readings. Instead of horses, Valentines prefer to travel by ornate gondolas through the city's extensive canal system.

Religious processions and civic festivals are frequent occurrences. The **Feast of [[Hespera]]**, the nation's patron [[Archon]], is particularly grand, featuring elaborate pageants, bullfights, and a regatta in the harbor.
### Tempo
The **Tempo** style of swordsmanship is an integral part of Valentian culture. Duels, while not officially sanctioned, are common among the youth and are often fought over matters of honor or to impress courtesans. In Valentine culture, a noble must have the training to be an able warrior and social talents such as wit, the ability to dance, and “a certain grace that makes all his activities seem effortless and unconscious.” 

With the appearance of [[Eden]], the duels have become a method to decide upon the warriors that will be given [[Edenic Mechanics#Void Fruits|Void Fruits]] and sent to [[Eden]]. The Arch-Vicar himself has held a few, with the Feast of [[Hespera]] being the day of a grand tournament that determines which of Valentia's greatest can journey on to the magical continent. 

Tempo is a refined form of fencing in which the practitioner stands sideways and wields a slender blade. It is a swift and deadly style that focuses on speed, balance and grace, thus requiring slender pointed blades far lighter than the longswords of [[Esrith]] knights and warriors. Trainees learn to wield their sword as though it is part of the arm, and to see with all the senses.
## Society
Valentine society is stratified between three groups: nobles, guildsmen, and commoners. The largest of these groups is undeniably the guildsmen, of which almost the entire population is a member of at least one of the five. 

Second in size is the commoners--mostly young, unskilled workers who have yet to gained enough renown to well and truly join a guild. These laborers did not have job protection and were dependent on their employers. Workers who violated rules could have their wages withheld or be discharged from their jobs.

Of the three, the smallest was the nobles, composed of almost entirely former Clans and especially historic merchanting or banking families. The nobles lived on large estates outside city walls, owning most of the land in the region. ## Economy
Valentia is a very wealth nation. Their skill in shipbuilding not only has allowed them to trade with merchants across the world, but also allow them to become allies with many of the largest nations, [[Esrith]] and [[Drachma]] in particular. Many merchants from [[Esrith]] and beyond make the trek to Valentia to engage in trade for the many exotic goods present within the nation, spices from [[Tarsis]] being among them. 

Valentine ships paint their hulls purple and their merchant ships sail to many distant lands and bring their trade and wealth back home. One hears of purple sails as far away as [[Kisia]] and the upper reaches of [[Drachma]]. In the present, Valentia’s capital city of Concordia is one of the greatest ports of the world, where all but slavers are allowed to dock.

The Valentines are also well known for their production of fine wines and cheeses, along with some of the finest art on the planet. Due to this, Valentia has some of the highest standard of living throughout the continents, although their strict immigration process means their population has not risen greatly. 
## Geography
Valentia is characterized by a diverse landscape that blends coastal features with inland terrain. The nation boasts a rugged coastline dotted with natural harbors and protected bays, ideal for its renowned maritime activities. Inland, rolling hills covered in vineyards and olive groves give way to fertile plains where cheese production thrives. 

A network of rivers and canals crisscrosses the country, facilitating transportation and trade. The eastern border is marked by a range of low mountains, providing natural protection and valuable mineral resources. Forests of oak and pine cover parts of the interior, while the southern region features a mix of varied vegetation and cultivated fields. 
## Notable Locations
### Concordia
**Concordia** is the capital city of Valentia, and one of the largest cities within [[Aravoth]]. The city is the size of a small country, with winding canals facilitating transportation along with colossal gothic architecture for both churches and universities. Palaces dot the landscape, with the House Trastámara particularly excellent, although their palace does not match the historic mansion of the Arch-Vicar. 

In the center of the city is the **University of Concordia**, one of the greatest prides of Valentia along with being the capital for scholars around the world. Their library is said to house more knowledge than any library in the world, except for perhaps the libraries of [[The Silver Havens]]. To the north is a the most travelled harbor in the world, constantly bustling with activity as ships with purple sails enter and leave. 

Embassies of all five great guilds can be found across Concordia, along with thousands of buildings, many of which built centuries ago. Tourism is live and well in Concordia, with nobles from [[Esrith]] and [[Drachma]] alike travelling across the world in order to enjoy the beautiful paradise. A large lake is visible from the Arch-Vicar's palace, while to the west is a beautiful mountain range. Forests and vineyards dot the landscape below, with Concordia itself situated on a rolling hill, allowing for the city to be multi-tiered. 
### Arbós
**Arbós** is a large city located in central [[Valentia]], near the border with [[Esrith]]. It is immediately next to a large forest.
