# [[Noah]]
## Overview
**Noah** is a person mentioned in the [[Haniel's Message]] description. Little is known beyond that [[Hespera]] informed him that the end of the world was near. 

In fact, [[Noah]] is actually an epithet used for [[Tyrmorn]], a man transported in time to the era of [[The Splintering]].%% He is considered the herald of the war to the [[Aravothil]], which allowed for the races to exist. If he had not been informed by [[Hespera]] and thereby able to tell the world of the war, the [[Aravothil]] would not exist. %%
