## Ability
### Immovable Stand
**Defensive**: Once per day, as a reaction when you would be forcibly moved, you can choose to be immobilized for 1 round. 

While you're immobilized in this way, you can be moved only if a creature succeeds at a DC 40 Athletics check to Force Open your armor. You can also be moved if 8,000 pounds of pressure are placed upon you, though this is likely fatal.
