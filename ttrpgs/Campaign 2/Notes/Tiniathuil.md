# [[Tiniathuil]]
## Overview
**Tiniathuil** is a Fruit wielded by [[Raphael]] when he slew [[ttrpgs/Campaign 2/Notes/Azâzêl]]. The handle itself is short and ornamental, with an inscription of the sun, while the blade itself is longer than most scimitars and slightly jagged, formed of a metal forged under extraordinary temperatures. The handle is still melting hot, except for those Resonated with the blade. 

It comes with [[Raphael's Burden]] already slotted into it.
