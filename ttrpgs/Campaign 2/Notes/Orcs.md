# [[Orcs]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Enshrak_Orc-removebg-preview.png]]
> *Orc of Abaddon wearing ritual face paint*
## Overview

The **Orcs** are one of the [[Races|races]] of [[Aravoth]]. Native to [[Abaddon]], they are a proud, strong people with hardened physiques who value physical might and glory in combat.

The majority of the Orcs of the world reside in [[Abaddon]], in loosely congregated tribes, although there are many that also live in the rest of the world, especially in [[Drachma]] where there are millions of them that are enslaved. A few slaves remain in [[Meridiem]], too, although the numbers are diminishing.
## Description
Orcs are tall and powerfully built, with long arms and stocky legs. Many Orcs top 7 feet in height, though they tend to adopt broad, almost bow-legged stances and slouch forward at the shoulders. Orcs have rough skin, thick bones, and rock-hard muscles, making them suited to war and other physically demanding tasks. Orc skin color is typically some shade of grey, though some Orcs have other skin colors that reflect adaptations to their environments.

Orcs consider powerful builds, heavily scarred skin, large tusks, and tattoos attractive, regardless of gender. Orcs reach physical adulthood around the age of 17, with many Orcs living to be up to 60 years old. 
## Personality
Orcs embody a culture of strength and resilience. These proud warriors value physical might and combat prowess, viewing scars and triumphs as badges of honor. Orc society thrives on direct communication and unwavering loyalty to allies. Their holds pulse with the energy of physical contests and feats of strength, where pain and glory intertwine as measures of respect. 
## Society
Orc culture embraces elements reminiscent of nomadic horseback warriors. Skilled riders and archers, they maintain a deep connection to ancestral traditions passed down through oral histories. Their society, while hierarchical, allows for mobility based on individual merit and strength. Orcs who survive numerous hardships and achieve great victories command immense respect within their communities, their stories woven into the fabric of Orc lore.
### Right of Challenge
A tradition of the Orcs, the Right of Challenge allows an Orc to challenge anyone in society, and if they win, they take the losers place in the hierarchy. Battles are always to the death, and are incredibly important in Orc society. The Right of Challenge allows the strongest warrior to always be the leader of a given tribe of Orcs, but the death of thousands of nearly as powerful warriors is likely one of the reasons why the Orcs have yet to successfully invade greater [[Meridiem]] in decades.
### Abroad
The Orcs far from [[Abaddon]] are greatly prejudiced against due to their society's actions during the [[Brothers' War]], even to this day. In general, the Orcs are systematically discriminated against in society, reduced to the roles of indentured servants and the lowest of the low class. However, there are some noble Orc families within [[Esrith]] and [[Valentia]], and many hybrid Orc-[[Dwarves|Dwarf]] families across [[Nidavellir]]. These noble families were generally treated with the respect they deserved. 
## Ability
The Orcs have an almost supernatural might that is unrivalled by even the strongest of the other races. This is one of the main reasons they are valued so highly as manual workers and slaves. 
## History
The origin of the Orcs is mysterious, but it is written in [[Elves|Elven]] lore that they first originated from [[The Silver Havens]], before being cast out by the [[Elves]] to eastern [[Ophiri]] by crossing the northern pole. It is believed that these frigid conditions compounded with the volcanic environment of [[Abaddon]] caused their grey skin. 

The first Orcs of [[Abaddon]] appeared sometime during the very early [[2nd Epoch]], before [[Humans]] populated [[Meridiem]]. Some moved south to the [[Isles of the Blessed]] during this time in search of treasure. In addition, a few migrated north to [[Ophiri]], and some of the stronger warriors created outposts in [[Meridiem]]-proper. 

Finally, after the [[Nexus]], it is believed that the first [[Humans]] appeared a few thousand years after the [[Orcs]] began to live in [[Abaddon]]. After co-existing with some modicum of piece for a long period of time, [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]] established the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] and fought the Orcs in the [[Duel of Giants]], thereby subjugating them under the rule of [[Humans]]. 

After thousands of years more passed, [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]] rose in the ranks, slaying every one of his enemies in the Right of Challenge with practiced ease. At this point, the Orcs became more vain and vicious than ever before, almost supernaturally. In this chaos, He became their leader, and secretly engaged in guerilla warfare with the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], igniting a civil war through his siege of [[Empyr]] and assassination of [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]]. However, after this battle ended, he mysteriously disappeared, leaving his people to be annihilated by the resistance forces. This fractured [[Meridiem]], but left the Orcs weak and leaderless, forced to wander [[Abaddon]] for eternity.
