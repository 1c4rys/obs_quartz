## Overview
**Aule** (pron. AHH-LEH) is an [[Elves|Elf]], born in [[The Silver Havens]]. He has lived for approximately 800 years before striking a deal with a devil to gain eternal life. Aule is characterized by his long life, spanning around 10,000 years, where he mostly lived in peace, wandering around the world. He is an expert of armormaking. 

Before venturing to [[Pangaia]], he lived in the southwestern forest in [[Valentia]], with his good friend [[Turing]]. 
## Description
Aule is an Elf with a height of around 6’0”. His hair is platinum blonde and styled in a traditional, long Elvish manner. Though Aule is several thousand years old, he has the appearance of a human in their early 30s. Aule generally wears adorned robes as loungewear, and an engraved medium armor set when in combat.
