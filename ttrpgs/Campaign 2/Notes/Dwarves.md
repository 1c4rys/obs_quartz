# [[Dwarves]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Harsk-removebg-preview.png]]
## Overview
The **Dwarves** are one of the [[Races|races]] of [[Aravoth]]. Native to [[Nidavellir]], they are a short, stocky people who are often stubborn, fierce, and devoted.

While many Dwarves live in underground cities near the surface, primarily in [[Meridiem]] and northern [[Ophiri]], the majority of Dwarves live within [[Nidavellir]], the sacred birthplace of their race and the location wherein their greatest economic output can be gathered--[[Orichalcum]].
## Description
Dwarves were a short race, as their name implied, standing from 4'3"–4'9" (1.3–1.45 meters) on average. What Dwarves lacked in height they made up for in bulk; they were, on average, about as heavy as [[Humans]]. A Dwarf could weigh anywhere from about 160–220 lbs (73–100 kg). Dwarven males were a bit taller and heavier than their female counterparts. Like [[Humans]], Dwarves had a wide variety of eye and hair colors, although the majority are fair of skin due to their frigid heritage. 

The biggest difference between [[Humans]] and Dwarves is body hair--of which the Dwarves had a lot. In order to adapt to the ruggedly cold environments of [[Nidavellir]], the Dwarves have grown great quantity of body hair across their body and especially on their face. It is not uncommon to see Dwarves of all sexes with bodies entirely covered in a layer of body hair, except for many spots on their face. 

Dwarves were a long-lived race, though not so much as [[Elves]] or [[Halflings]], and reached physical maturity somewhat later than [[Humans]]. A Dwarf was traditionally considered an adult once he or she reached age thirty-five. Dwarves aged much like [[Humans]] but over a longer period of time, remaining vigorous well past 70 years. Most Dwarves lived to see their centennial and a few lived to be over 150. A Dwarf was considered to be young until they reached the age of 35. 
## Personality
Dwarves tend to value honor and closely follow the traditions of their clans and kingdoms. They have a strong sense of friendship and justice, though they are often very particular about who they consider a friend. They work hard and play harder—especially when strong ale is involved. 
## Society
In the fatherland, the Dwarves are a tough, adventurous, and fearless people with a strong warrior ethos. They are independent and fiercely proud of their heritage, placing great value on honor, loyalty, and bravery in battle. They hold a deep connection to nature and the seas, primarily due to their difficult lives in harsh, cold environments. The Dwarves place a strong focus on community, with close-knit families and a sense of duty to their peoples, but also have a fierce sense of personal freedom. 

[[Orichalcum]], a metal only found deep within the mountains of [[Nidavellir]], the existence of which has created a deep culture of blacksmithing within the Dwarves. It is not uncommon for many Dwarven families to live a nomadic lifestyle, journeying across the world in order to trade their weapons, armor, or just the raw materials. Regardless, the homeland of the Dwarves is deeply important to them as a culture, with many of them travelling back yearly during the Winter Solstice to view the [[Arc]] from deep in the north. 
### Families
Especially in [[Nidavellir]] but also in lesser cases elsewhere, Dwarven societies are divided into families based both on blood and political allegiance. These families were usually led by hereditary rulers, often monarchs of a sort and descended from the founder of the clan. Dwarves strongly valued loyalty to these rulers and to their family as a whole and even objective Dwarves tended to side primarily with their kin over other races or communities. 
## Ability
The Dwarves have an almost uncanny ability to craft, making them by far the greatest craftsman on [[Aravoth]]. The finest weapons across the world were crafted by Dwarves and forged of [[Orichalcum]]--the [[Nexomancy#Automaton|Automaton]]s created through [[Nexomancy]] no exception.
## History
Like many races, the exact origins of the Dwarves were lost in myth and legend. Most Dwarves believe that their ancestors were originated in the center of [[Aravoth]] itself, forged by [[Eitri]] into his avatars. They believe that [[Nidavellir]] itself was a gate to the center of the world, a place where the first Dwarves exited their primordial womb. 

The first known Dwarves appeared during the early [[2nd Epoch]], like many of the other races, migrating by boat to northern [[Ophiri]] in search of greater opportunity, eventually allowing for the migration of [[Dwarves]] to every corner of the world. The Dwarves were respected by the [[Drachmi]] people of [[Ophiri]], primarily due to their intense work ethic and culture of loyalty, which the [[Drachmi]] too believed. This allowed for rapid ingratiation into their culture, allowing for [[Ophiri]] to be the second-largest population of Dwarves across the globe. One of these Dwarves that travelled south was the craftsman [[Thordin]], a legendary figure said to have been the brother of [[Eitri]] himself and fiercely effective at invention. 

Sometime during the middle [[2nd Epoch]], years before the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] were founded, the first true nation-states upon [[Nidavellir]] were established. These nations were crudely organized and therefore were frequent overruled, with dozens of nations upon each of the isles growing in response. 

In the early [[3rd Epoch]], as the [[Orcs]] were viewed as an existential threat after the fall of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], the Dwarves of [[Nidavellir]] sent many of their warriors south to battle them back. After this victory, many chose to stay, migrating across [[Meridiem]] to the former [[Dawn Kingdoms]], especially [[Esrith]]. 

In later [[3rd Epoch]], soon after [[Drachma]] was well and truly formed, the threat of colonization loomed large over the Dwarves of [[Nidavellir]] due to their possession of [[Orichalcum]]. Despite this threat that still continues to today, the warriors of [[Drachma]] have yet to have made considerable progress due to how treacherous the waters of [[Nidavellir]] are. However, In recent years, the first [[Drachmi]] stronghold has been formed within the isles of [[Nidavellir]]-- a fortress known as [[Pytheae]].
