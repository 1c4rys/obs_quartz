# [[The Splintered Sea]]
## Overview
**The Splintered Sea*** is the largest ocean in the world, located east of [[Drachma]], [[Meridiem]], and north of [[Kisia]]. It is the location where [[Eden]] arose. There is considered to be a large number of terrifying [[Abominations]] slumbering within its depths.
