# [[3rd Epoch]]
## Overview
The **3rd Epoch** is the current Epoch, taking place after the [[1st Epoch]] and [[2nd Epoch]]. The first thousand years after the fall of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] were punctuated by war and disarray, as the only true uniting force in the world was gone. Over the centuries, however, many of the largest countries in the history of the world were founded. 

Just a few years ago, 2639 TE, due to the alignment of the [[Shepherds]], [[Eden]] was unveiled from it's paradoxical origin, greatly changing the world. 
## History
The early 3rd Epoch was primarily an era of battle, suffering, and decay, as the former [[Dawn Kingdoms]] fought over territory, before settling into an innumerable number of city states, with the only exception to the rule being Verden, since renamed to [[Valentia]], which has remained under the same banner since this time. [[Cyndra]], another smaller nation state, too kept its line of kings up until recently. Telran has collapsed, only composed the [[Free City of Letnya]] remaining, while Rhiscar and Aendor have been entirely absorbed into [[Esrith]].

After this time of conflict, [[Tarsis]] remained a loosely connected set of city-states, still following their own religion, [[Elohim]]. Around 1600 TE, the loosely connected domains controlled by a variety of families lead by Daimyo were united under one banner, evolving into [[Kisia|Kyushima]]. Around 2100 TE, the empire of [[Drachma]] was first truly founded, quickly conquering an innumerable number of countries whose names have been lost to the tides of history upon [[Ophiri]]'s eastern seaboard facing [[The Splintered Sea]]. Soon after, [[Esrith]] was reunited after [[Meridiem's Second War]], the largest country within [[Meridiem]] since the fall of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], resorbing Rhiscar and acting as the primary competitor to [[Drachma]]'s control over the western hemisphere. 

The early 27th century TE was punctuated by great conquering by the newest Drachmi emperor, [[Drach VII]]. Cyndra, a country in northwestern Meridiem, was totally conquered, along with a military presence established in western Ophiri within Tarsis. 

In 2639 TE, during the Winter Solstice wherein the Shepherds were expected to create the [[Arc]], a one in a million event occurred, causing the alignment of both moons and the rings of Aravoth resulting in the tides located off the southeastern coast of Drachma to rapidly fall, unveiling the untouched and largely unexplored continent of [[Eden]].
