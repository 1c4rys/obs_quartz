# [[1st Epoch]]
## Overview
The **1st Epoch** was the period of time when the races of [[Aravoth]] first evolved into what they are today. It lasted 930,000 years. Of all ruins dated by archaeologists, none of it was found to be from the 1st Epoch. Thus, the 1st Epoch is very mysterious, and little is known about it beyond that first fact. Many archaeologists theorize that [[Eden]] was the home of the races of the world during the 1st Epoch, although until recently, this information was unsubstantiated. 

Archaeologists have dated many ruins and [[Edenic Mechanics#Fruits|Fruits]] within [[Eden]], all of which are dated sometime during or immediately after the 1st Epoch. Therefore, it is generally believed that during the 1st Epoch, the [[Aravothil]] were entirely within [[Eden]].
