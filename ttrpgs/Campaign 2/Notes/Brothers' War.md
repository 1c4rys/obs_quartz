# [[Brothers' War]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Brothers' War Image]]
## Overview
The **Brothers' War** was an ancient conflict in the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] between the [[Orcs]] of [[Abaddon]], led by [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]], and the people of the greater [[Dawn Kingdoms]], led by their king, [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]]. It resulted in the death of King [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]], the end of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], and the disappearance of [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]].
## Etymology
It was named the "Brothers' War" because the leaders of both continents were named identically the [[Archon]]s [[Neton (Archon)|Neton]] and [[Denarion (Archon)|Denarion]] in the faith of [[Dawnism]], whom were brothers. Some believe that [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]] and [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]] truly were brothers, although the fact that one is [[Humans|Human]] and the other is [[Orcs|Orc]] leads the general consensus to be that this is false. 
## History
The war began after a long-standing dispute between the [[Orcs]] of [[Abaddon]] and the greater population within [[Dawn Kingdoms]]. Little is known beyond the fact that, at some point, the [[Orcs]] sporadically grew in might and anger, raiding smaller settlements in western [[Meridiem]] outside the border of [[Abaddon]]. [[The Wall]] was built quickly by King [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]], in order to defend against this conflict. The leader of the [[Orcs]], [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]], promised that he had nothing to do with this--but that turned out to be a lie. 

The war truly began when [[Orcs]] pushed further than ever before into the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], reaching right outside the capital city of [[Empyr]]. The king was returning home from diplomatic missions in the [[Isles of the Blessed]], accompanied by his Kingsguard, at the time. He returned to [[Empyr]], and that night the [[Orcs]] laid siege to [[Empyr]], murdering the entire population--hundreds of thousands--in the week following. Immediately after the murder, [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]] disappeared, leaving his [[Orcs]] to fight leaderless against a defense force formed quickly afterwards from the surviving Kingsguard. 

The [[Orcs]] were defeated in this conflict, although the victory was pyrrhic, as the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] collapsed without their steadfast leader and [[Abaddon]] decayed, now just a collection of unorganized tribes constantly battling one another. The rest of the [[Orcs]] were never forgiven for the acts of their compatriots from the motherland, and they still face that prejudice to this day.
