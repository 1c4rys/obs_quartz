# [[Nidavellir]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Castle_of_Kaer_Trolde.webp]]
## Overview
**Nidavellir** is the homeland of the [[Dwarves]] located in [[Aravoth]]. A collection of isles in the deep north, it is the only location where the most precious of metals can be found--[[Orichalcum]]. 
## Etymology
Nidavellir is the term used in ancient [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] lore to refer to the realm of the [[Dwarves]], so it was an obvious choice. 
## Geography
The region is composed of an innumerable number of mountainous islands circling a larger mainland. There are large amounts of horrific [[Abominations]] taking the form of sea monsters deep within the sea of Nidavellir, allowing for deterrence against invading forces such as those of [[Drachma]]. The mountainous islands and mainland has resulted in a great mining enterprise among the [[Dwarves]], and through the existence of [[Orichalcum]], Nidavellir is one of the most important locations in the entire world. 
## Countries
There are an innumerable number of small nations upon Nidavellir, but there is a central authority that holds some sway over the actions of the nation, known as the **Dwarflords**, composed of the patriarchs or matriarchs of each given family that owns large quantities of land in Nidavellir. 

However, with [[Drachma]] expansion, the fortress city of [[Pytheae]] was established in southwestern Nidavellir, the first successful western expansion into the region. 
## Culture
The [[Dwarves]] of Nidavellir have a strong culture prioritizing the value of warriors and loyalty to one's family, and due to the presence of [[Orichalcum]], it is common in Nidavellirian society for [[Dwarves]] to spend years producing the finest of armor and weapons with the ancient metal, before migrating south for a while to sell it within [[Drachma]] and [[Esrith]], among others. In addition to this, many [[Dwarves]] around the world migrate back to Nidavellir once a year in order to view the [[Arc]] deep in the north. 
### Language
Those native to Nidavellir speak both Dvergramal and and Esrithil, although more recently Dvergramal has become a sort-of archaic language only used for religious reasons and in runecrafting. Still, it is important for someone from Nidavellir to understand Dvergramal, and the majority of the population still do. However, it is likely that within the next century or two, Dvergramal will fade into obscurity. 
### Religion
> *Main article: [[Fornsidr]]*

The [[Dwarves]] of Nidavellir follow the religion of [[Fornsidr]], a polytheistic, ethnic faith circulating around the figure of [[Eitri]] ([[Moradis]]). There are a large quantity of giant figures in the faith, along with many other lesser gods, including [[Thordin]]. There is no text nor an authoritative version, instead varying over time.
