# [[Serenity]]
## Overview
The [[Splinter]] of **Serenity** was one of the seven [[Splinter]]s gifted to the denizens of [[Aravoth]]  by [[Aeternum]], each of which are pieces of her flesh. 

The Splinter of Serenity was given to [[Haniel]], a member of the [[Precursors]] who would later become the basis for the race of the [[Halflings]]. This [[Splinter]] blesses those born within its radius with the powers of Serenity, hence why the [[Halflings|Halfling]] race was formed. Currently, the [[Splinter]] of Serenity is held by [[Haniel]] within the countryside of [[Valentia]].
