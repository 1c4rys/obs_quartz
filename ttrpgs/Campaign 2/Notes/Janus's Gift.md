# [[Janus's Gift]]
## Overview
**Janus's Gift** is a Unique [[Fruits|Fruit]] in the shape of a small, ornamental mirror. After a drop of [[Nihil]]'s Ichor was place on it, it created a portal to an opposite dimension.
## Info
### Archetype
**Unique**: Mirror
*note: Cannot equip Runes*
### Level
0
## Description
*"The line between light and dark is so very thin. Do you know which side you're on?"* 
## Intrinsic
### Two-Faced
Janus's Gift displays the opposite of the dimension one is currently in in its reflection. In addition, beings originating from the current plane are reanimated within the opposite one.
