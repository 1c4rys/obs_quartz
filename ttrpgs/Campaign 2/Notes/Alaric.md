# [[Alaric]]
## Overview
**Alaric** was the leader of the [[Archon]]s in the faith of [[Dawnism]], the son of [[The Empyrean]], and the [[Archon]] of Justice. His symbol was that of the Sun. 
## Description
He was portrayed in varying ways throughout different texts and cultures, but he was believed to be male and white of hair. He was not portrayed as any of the [[Aravothil]], instead being greatly taller and larger than any sentient beings on [[Aravoth]]. 
## Personality
As the [[Archon]] of Justice, he was known for his calmness and intense care for fairness in all situations. He was very friendly with those he talked to, and was well regarded in his society. 
## History
Little is known of his life beyond the fact that he was the first of the [[Archon]]s and lived within [[Eden]]. It is believed he still exists, somewhere.
