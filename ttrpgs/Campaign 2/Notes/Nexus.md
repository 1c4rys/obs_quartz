# [[Nexus]]
## Overview
The **Nexus** was a disaster that struck [[Aravoth]] in the early [[2nd Epoch]] on the Winter Solstice, during the [[Arc]]. It was an event that allowed the [[Abominations]], including ghouls, goblins, and vampires, to cross over from a different planet to [[Aravoth]]. In the process, the [[Aravothil]] were provided the [[Nexusborns]]--vestiges of the catacylsm. 
## History
### Origins
There are scores of learned works, dissertations and treatises about this magical cataclysm from about 100,000 years ago. Because of this event, creatures never seen before entered our world, and still do not have their own ecological niche here. 

The most popular and widely accepted theory about the origins of the Nexus was that this calamity was caused by a celestial anomaly that allowed for the convergence of [[Aravoth]]'s [[Kyushimago religion#The Three Worlds|Spirit Realm]] with that of another planet's. This allowed for these creatures to pass over, although there are no firsthand accounts of this event that are known. 
### Aftermath
Unable to manipulate the forces of magic and with little preparation to fight the monsters that came through the conjunction of the different realms, the [[Aravothil]] were plunged into a strange, dangerous, and uncertain time of conflict which lasted for many generations. 

According to the lore of the [[Elves]], [[Humans]] arrived during the Nexus, their own world having been destroyed. These [[Humans|Human]] ancestors learned how to harness the power of the [[Nexusborns]], and thus the first [[Humans|Human]] warriors were born. Looking for a place in the world, they took up arms against the [[Abominations]], who were unable to withstand the barbarians and ultimately surrendered. This is how [[Humans]] came to rule the world.
