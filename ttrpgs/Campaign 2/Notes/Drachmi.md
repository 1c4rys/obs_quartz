# [[Drachmi]]
## Overview
The **Drachmi** are the vastly diverse [[People|population]] living within [[Drachma]], composed of mostly Humans. 
## Description
The Drachmi are composed of multiple ethnic groups, chief of which is those from eastern [[Ophiri]]. These people possess an olive complexion due to their proximity to the equator, along with darker hair. They are organized and kind, although they were deeply afraid of those different than them. 
## Culture
The majority of the Drachmi are members of [[Helladism]], and thusly they deeply respect their government and live to serve. The birth rates in [[Drachma]] are incredibly high due to this, as the Drachmi hope to ensure the prosperity of their empire. In doing so, they are deeply racist to all those who are not from their glorious empire, as they view them as already damned to an eternity of torment--less than human. The Drachmi are also deeply agrarian and capitalist, generally not caring for their fellow man unless they provide some form of value to the empire. 
## History
The Drachmi originated from the separation of the supercontinents, as many [[Halflings]] and [[Humans]] were brought to [[Ophiri]]. Despite remaining disunited for thousands of years, incursions from the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] during the mid-[[2nd Epoch]] remained unsuccessful. Inspired by the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] form of governance, and perhaps inspired by their religion, many attempts were made at forming a unified [[Ophiri]] nation, although they would be unsuccessful until the second millennia of the [[3rd Epoch]], where [[Drachma]] was finally established.
