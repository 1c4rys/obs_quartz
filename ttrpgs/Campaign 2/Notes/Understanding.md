# [[Understanding]]
## Overview
The [[Splinter]] of **Understanding** was the first of the seven Splinters gifted to the denizens of [[Aravoth]]  by [[Aeternum]], each of which are pieces of her flesh, and the only one purposefully given. 

The [[Splinter]] of Understanding was given to [[Adam]], a member of the [[Wildfolk]] who would later become the basis for the race of the [[Precursors]]. This [[Splinter]] blesses those born within its radius with the powers of Understanding, hence why the [[Precursors|Precursor]] race was formed. Currently, the [[Splinter]] of Understanding is held by no one, after [[Adam]] died during [[The Splintering]]. Influence from the [[Splinter]] of Understanding is found within all [[Aravothil]], due to being the basis for the race that was the ancestors of every other race. Due to not being held by anyone, whoever first descends to [[Edenic Mechanics#The Throne of God|The Throne of God]] within [[Eden]] will rise to godhood and become a member of the [[Archon]]s.
