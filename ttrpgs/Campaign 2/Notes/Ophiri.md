# [[Ophiri]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[4ae254c8b6985afc9c664a95119958e5.webp]]
## Overview
**Ophiri** is the largest natural continent on [[Aravoth]]. Life originated in [[Meridiem]] then migrated northward to Ophiri. It is incredibly culturally and ecologically diverse and home to the largest nation, the empire of [[Drachma]]. 
## Etymology
The word "Ophiri" in Drachmi is a location in the holy scripts of [[Helladism]] associated with great wealth, long-distance trade, and exotic goods. 
## Geography
Ophiri is vastly ecologically diverse. In the southern parts of the continents is a vast tropical paradise similar to that of [[Valentia]]. In central Ophiri, a colossal, arid mountain range stands, while the entirety of the west is the [[Tarsis]], a vast desert region. In the northeast is a frigid wasteland, while the north is similar. There are a vast quantities of rivers throughout the region along with many land bridges and large peninsulas. 

To the north was [[The Silver Havens]], the northeast [[Nidavellir]], and to the south the [[Indigo Strait]] and [[Meridiem]]. Right off its eastern coast is [[Eden]]. 
## Countries
Only one organized, well-defined country exists within Ophiri--[[Drachma]]--although a great number of city-states and whatnot exist in [[Tarsis]] and even northern Ophiri, although none are large enough to be considered a country except for a few of the larger cities deep in the west. 
### Drachma
> *Main article: [[Drachma]]*

**Drachma** is a vast theocratic empire composed of mostly [[Humans]]. Viciously expansionary, many of the peoples from [[Meridiem]], [[Nidavellir]], and [[Tarsis]] view their influence as a threat. Drachma is deeply intertwined with [[Helladism]]. 
## Culture
The continent of Ophiri is more culturally diverse than any other region. From [[Tarsis]] to [[Drachma]] to the upper reaches of the north, there are many different cultures. The religions of [[Helladism]], [[Elohim]], [[Dawnism]], and the lore of the [[Dwarves]] hold deep sway across the continent, although in recent years [[Helladism]] has captured the hearts of many.
