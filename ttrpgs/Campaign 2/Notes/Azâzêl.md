# [[ttrpgs/Campaign 2/Notes/Azâzêl]]
## Overview
**Azâzêl** (pron. UH-ZAY-ZEE-ELL) is a [[Watcher]] first mentioned in the [[Skyreaver]] description. He is said to have been in control of many legions, likely composed of [Sky Dragon](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=1129 )s. It is also mentioned that the "stars themselves bend to his will.[^2]" He has some relation to [[Dawnism#Death|Death]]. 

The [[Azâzêl's Fall]], [[Azâzêl's Sins]], [[Azâzêl's Blame]], [[Azâzêl's Freedom]], [[Azâzêl's Atonement]], and [[Azâzêl's Banishment]] were all touched by Azâzêl. The realm of [[Dûdâêl]] was his seat of power.  

[[Tyrmorn]], a veteran of the battles against Azâzêl's legions, informed the party that only through following his Azâzêl's life and wielding all of his [[Fruits]] could they eventually destroy him once and for all and therefore escape the land of [[Dûdâêl]]. 
## History
He was a [[Watcher]] of [[Nihil]], and was known as one of the most powerful. He fought a battle against the [[Archon]]s, before he was slain by [[Raphael]][^1].
