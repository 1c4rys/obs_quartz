# [[Manekumi]]
## Overview
**Manekumi** is one of two deities in the [[Kyushimago religion]], the other being [[Manekugi]]. She appears as mostly androgynous, although moreso effeminate in modern adaptations. She is considered to be the spirit within the smaller of the two [[Shepherds]], along with being commonly equated with [[Rhiannon]]. 
## Description
She is portrayed as a duplicitous, always possessing a different form. Many portray her as relatively petite, however. It is rare to find a similar interpretation between different followers of the [[Kyushimago religion]], with some even considering her to be male. 
## Personality
It's said that Manekumi wears shapes the way a mortal might wear a scarf. She is every grain of rice and every farmer that harvests them, every stroke of a brush and the painter who made them. She is the kindness of the world and the artistry its denizens create. 
## History
> *Main article: [[Kyushimago religion#Creation]]*

She was born deep in the distant cosmos as twins with [[Manekugi]]. They created [[Aravoth]], but [[Manekugi]] was accidentally killed by her, so she sacrificed her whimsy, forming the [[Kitsune]] in the process, before escaping from the netherworld with her brother and becoming the [[Shepherds]].
