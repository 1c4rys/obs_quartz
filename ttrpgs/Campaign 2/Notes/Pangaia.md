# [[Pangaia]]
## Overview
**Pangaia** is an island located in southeastern [[Drachma]], and its capital of [[Evemerus]] is the closest harbor to [[Eden]]. This has led to Pangaia’s economy booming in recent years, as it grows to become one of the most populous countries. 
## Geography
Pangaia’s geography is relatively similar to the rest of the [[Drachma]] continent, although very different in both size and scope. Pangaia’s mountains are colossal in size, and its trees are hundreds of feet tall. Large plantlife snakes around the northern part of the continent, while the southern bit, where [[Evemerus]] is situated, is all but flat. 

To the north is a large bay, and to the west is a collection of multiple smaller islands, mostly unpopulated.
## Inhabitants
The majority of people in Pangaia are [[Drachmi]] [[Humans]], but a population of [[Halflings]] are also present. Members of every race hope to journey to [[Eden]] from here.

However, beyond the [[Aravothil]] population, tens of thousands of horrific [[Abominations]] lie to the north, deep in Pangaia’s forest. It is the third largest concentration of monsters in the world. This is mostly because monsters from [[Eden]] find that Pangaia is the closest landmass to journey to. While the majority are slain by Redeemers, many make it past them.
