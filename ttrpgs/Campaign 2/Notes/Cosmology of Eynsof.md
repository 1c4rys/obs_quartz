# [[Cosmology of Eynsof]]
## Overview
**Eynsof** is the greater shared universe wherein both [[Toril]] and [[Aravoth]] are within. Eynsof's cosmology is complex and has multiple different divisions and methods of travel, so it is important to outline it.

%%
## The Planes
There are seven realms, and multiple worlds within each plane:
- The Physical Realm
- The Soul Realm
- The Mind Realm
- The Netherworld
- The Waters of Creation
- Abyssos
- Heaven
### The Physical Realm
The **Physical Realm** is the largest of the realms in Eynsof. It is where all mortals reside and where almost all physical items exist. It is the only realm where physical interaction is possible. All living beings are present here and the wide majority of beings will spend the entirety of their life here. It is the realm of the body. 

The Physical Realm is the only realm to experience time completely linearly.
### The Soul Realm
The **Soul Realm** is one of the strangest of the realms, being only accessible through souls. Planeswalkers use the Soul Realm to travel within the Physical Realm via Gates. All mortals will enter the Soul Realm upon the death of their physical bodies, wherein they will then be able to either reenter the Physical Realm or enter the Netherworld. It is the realm of the mind and the soul, but not the body. 
### The Mind Realm
The **Mind Realm** is a realm virtually impossible to access purposefully, and even then, only accessible by mortals. It is accessed every night when one dreams, and it is a world where probability and 'what if's' collide, allowing for events impossible in the Physical Realm to occur and feel real. This realm is also called the Narrative Void. 
### The Netherworld
The **Netherworld** acts as a buffer realm to Abyssos, a location where all souls will at one point reach. It is not necessarily a deplorable place, but it is not pleasant. Little is known beyond that. 

It is considered a pseudo-Elemental Plane, being the origin of True Darkness.
### The Waters of Creation
The **Waters of Creation** is a vast realm composed of many elemental planes wherein the instruments that create and allow for the perseverance of the Physical Realm reside. There are six planes within the Waters of Creation:
- **Plane of Air**
- **Plane of Earth**
- **Plane of Fire (also known as the "Nine Hells")**
- **Plane of Metal**
- **Plane of Water**
- **Plane of Wood (also known as the "Feywilds")**
### Abyssos
**Abyssos**, also known as the [[Realm at the End of Time]], is a vast cosmological Void. It is where [[Nihil]] and the [[Forsaken]] reside and it is the only place where a soul can die. 
### Heaven
**Heaven** is a plane that no longer exists. It is where Narrative Seed first came to be. After the Narrative Seed no longer existed, so did the plane.

## Mechanics
### Deities
There are many **Deities** across Eynsof, and the rules around them are *weird*. Deities are beings that possess a fragment of the Narrative Seed within their souls. Those with fragments of the Narrative Seed follow strange rules different than that of mortals, but gain omnipotence in return. The larger the fragment, the stronger. There is 

Deities can access the Mind Realm, but their fragment of the Narrative Seed is nullified.
### Narrative Seed
The **Narrative Seed** is a seed planted by a being known as the Game Master, creating the planes and the concept of divinity. Through one way or another, the Narrative Seed has influenced every event in the history of everything, and provides supernatural powers. It is not necessarily a being, instead being more of a concept. 

It is impossible for someone under the Domain of a Narrative Seed to be aware of the Game Master, except for other beings from the same plane as the Game Master that the Game Master allows to act as a figure in his game. There are some figures that were formerly acted by an upper plane being, such as [[Tyrmorn]] or [[Koart]]. These characters are the same as they once were, except for losing all memory of their self-awareness. 
### Game Master
The **Game Master** is a being that exists outside of Eynsof, acting as the supreme creator of everything in the world. The Game Master planted the seed that is Narrative Seed, along with conceptualizing all of Eynsof. There are multiple Game Masters in existence, but there is only one Game Master for Eynsof itself. Many other Game Masters, creators of their own universes, have influenced Eynsof's Game Master into creating his universe in a unique way.

The Game Master himself is under the effect of his own Narrative Seed, controlled by someone that the Game Master is unaware of. However, the Game Master has theories--primarily that a higher Game Master exists. 
### Domains
The **Domains** are a concept invented by the Narrative Seed because the Game Master was too lazy to invent a way for the gods of [[Toril]], created by another Game Master, to be involved in the conceptually new world of [[Aravoth]]. Beings created by other Game Masters possess fragments of their own form of the Narrative Seed, and therefore cannot interact without consent by both Game Masters. 

However, entities that the greater Narrative Seed created by Eynsof's Game Master have himself created do not follow this rule. This is because the Narrative Seed is more brilliant than that of any other Narrative Seed, overwhelming the rule of Domains. 
### Planeswalkers
**Planeswalkers** are beings influenced by a Narrative Seed that travel to the domain of a new Narrative Seed, usually through the Soul Realm via Gates.

### Gates
**Gates** are structures created through the conflict of two Narrative Seeds, allowing for transfer between them. This is only possible for beings influenced by the greater of the two Narrative Seeds.
