# [[Elohim]]
## Overview
**Elohim** is a monotheistic religion in [[Tarsis]]. It is deeply ingrained with the culture of western [[Ophiri]] and also uses the [[Genesis]] as their primary religious text, although the impressions are incredibly different than in [[Dawnism]]. 

> *Metacommentary: Think of this as the Judaism to Dawnism's Christianity.*
## Differences from Dawnism
The largest of the differences from [[Dawnism]] to Elohim is the figure of worship. In [[Dawnism]], [[The Empyrean]] is worshipped above all but the [[Archon]]s are debatably just as important. In Elohim, there is only one true god, [[Aravoth]] itself. Followers of Elohim believe that [[The Empyrean]] referred to in [[Genesis]] is instead a reference to the planet of [[Aravoth]], and therefore the being most worthy of worship is the planet they stand on. They do still believe in the [[Archon]]s, but perceive them as supernatural beings that directly work on the order of [[Aravoth]], guiding followers of [[Elohim]] to best serve the god-planet they stand on. Otherwise, both religions are very similar.

> *Metacommentary: The Archons are the equivalent of Archangels in the Elohim religion.*
