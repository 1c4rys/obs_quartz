# [[Aravothil]]
## Overview
The Aravothil were the primary inhabitants of [[Aravoth]]. They first spread across the supercontinent during the [[2nd Epoch]]. They are biologically very diverse and found nearly all across the world, differentiated by common beasts because of their sentience. The Aravothil are composed of: [[Humans]], [[Elves]], [[Dwarves]], [[Orcs]], [[Halflings]], and the [[Kitsune]]. 
## Etymology
The names of the Aravothil are based off of the ancient language of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]]--[[Old Dawnic]], although across the world, they are referred to differently. For example, the Dwarves refer to themselves as Dvergr (pronounced "vurr-gurr").
## Description
Typically, Aravothil are bipedal, with two-arms, two-legs, and walk upright. However, they vary widely in terms of both size and features. Most did not have innate spellcasting abilities, a major exception including the [[Kitsune]]. 
## Society
The most numerous Aravothil societies were those of the Dwarves, Humans, Kitsune, and Orcs.
