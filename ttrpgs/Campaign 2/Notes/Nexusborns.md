# [[Nexusborns]]
## Overview
The **Nexusborns** are vestiges of the [[Nexus]] that appear as weapons, vastly boosting the physical power of their wielders. There are fewer than 200 believed to be in existence. 
## Description
Depending on the Nexusborn, different powers are provided to the wielder. Usually, a given Nexusborn vastly boosts the physical strength of whomever is attuned to it. Most Nexusborns provide the weapon with supernatural abilities as well, along with the ability to damage intangible beings such as wraiths. 

Wounds caused by Nexusborns are impossible to heal without another Nexusborn or with [[Edenic Mechanics#Magic|Magic]]. 

All Nexusborns have [[Old Dawnic]] runes inscribed on them, indecipherable to man. 
## Known Nexusborns
There are few known Nexusborns, but of those mentioned so far:
### Caliburn
**Caliburn** is the weapon wielded by [[Uther]] and given to his eldest son, [[Arthur]]. Since [[Arthur]] disappeared, the weapon is considered lost. Its known abilities is that it provides the user with an undying lifespan and appears in the form of a colossal, dark blue greatsword. 
### Unnamed Nexomancy Nexuborn
Sometime during the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] existence, a Nexusborn with the power to manipulate souls was discovered and utilized for the purpose of [[Nexomancy]]. Little is known beyond that in can control the souls of the deceased and place them within other forms, following the commands of the wielder of the Nexusborn.
