# [[Kitsune]]
## Overview
The **Kitsune** are one of the [[Races|races]] of [[Aravoth]]. Native to [[Kisia]] and fiercely isolationist, they are a charismatic and witty people with a connection to the spiritual that grants them many magical abilities, chiefly the power to shapechange into other forms. 

Kitsune are shapechangers with two forms: one of a fox-headed humanoid and one largely depending on where they were raised. Those raised in populated areas typically have what's called a tailless form—a humanoid body without any fox features that resembles a more common ancestry, such as one of the [[Elves]] or [[Humans]]. In wooded or rural areas, their second form is more likely to be that of a fox.
## Description
Kitsune have alert vulpine ears, pointed snouts, and short, semi-retractable claws on their fingers and toes. Their fur is dense, countershaded, and most often red, tan, black, or white in color. Kitsune have bushy tails that grow in number as they hone their innate magical abilities, to as many as nine.

In their tailless form, Kitsune resemble typical [[Humans]], with those originating from [[Kisia]] possessing warm, tawny skin, with almond-shaped eyes and straight, jet-black hair that shimmers in the sunlight. In general, all Kitsune have some [[Kisia]] blood within them, although it is not infrequent to find [[Kitsune]] in small settlements in southern [[Ophiri]] and central [[Esrith]] that possess the same physical traits as those in the area. 
## Personality
Kitsune are charismatic, witty, and playful, with a natural affinity for subverting expectations and delighting in clever wordplay, pranks, and intricate plots. They are keen observers, using their sharp minds to read social dynamics and adapt seamlessly to various situations. This makes them enigmatic and magnetic, often leaving others charmed but unsure of what the Kitsune might be hiding.

Their dual nature, both spiritual and material, allows them to explore different facets of their personality, sometimes presenting themselves in dramatically different ways depending on the situation. Kitsune enjoy being unpredictable, balancing their cleverness and humor with a genuine interest in understanding and engaging with those around them.
## Society
Kitsune are rarely the majority in whatever communities they inhabit, and many go their whole lives without meeting another Kitsune beyond their family. Their society, as much as it exists, centers primarily around the commonalities that Kitsune find as they pass among other peoples. The same social perspective that helps them imitate others also makes them quick to notice signs that someone may need help or a kind word. Due to this, Kitsune often form strong interpersonal relationships and tend to anchor their social circles.
