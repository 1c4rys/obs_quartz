# [[Abominations]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[MonsterCore_Lineup_1920x1080.png]]
## Overview
**Abominations** are the colloquial name for supernatural beings originating from both the [[Nexus]] and from [[Eden]]. 

[[Nhekaush]] researches them as part of his practice.
## Differences
Abominations originating from the [[Nexus]] are lesser versions than those originating from [[Eden]], although there are no doubt many similarities. For example, dragons exist from both sources, but those from [[Eden]] are both much larger in size and strength than those in the rest of the world. 

It is believed that both originated from the same place, but those originating from the [[Nexus]] have evolved differently, both because of less food sources and because of the higher gravity upon [[Aravoth]] in comparison to many places within [[Eden]]. 
## Habitation
Abominations can be found all over the world, although more recently the quantity of Abominations has reduced greatly. With both advances in [[Edenic Mechanics#Magic|Magic]] and technology, many of the traditional Abominations are not as much of a threat as they once were. However, many Abominations from [[Eden]] have travelled from the continent across [[Aravoth]], especially to neighboring coasts such as that of [[Ophiri]], resulting in almost a second [[Nexus]] event. 

The largest population of original Abominations can be found in two locations: northwestern [[Tarsis]] and the [[Isles of the Blessed]]. In the case of the former, a lack of human population to deter growth along with horrifying conditions has bred some of the strongest Abominations in the world. In the latter, experiments by the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] has resulted in many [[Automatons|Automaton]] abominations populating the region. 

In addition, it is believed that much of the folklore around the world, especially in [[Kisia]] and [[Nidavellir]], is actually higher Abominations with intelligence somewhat equal to humans.
## Biology
Abomination biology varies wildly, although all Abominations have a soul. Many Abominations are not self-aware nor sentient, and a few are. True Vampires are considered some of the strongest Abominations, and possess sentience along with appearing almost identical to [[Aravothil]]. 

Abominations are distinguished from other creatures using two primary criteria: dissimilarity to [[Aravothil]] and lack of sentience or obvious intelligence. Because of this, some sentient races are considered monsters because they are not humanoid and some decidedly non-humanoid creatures are considered sentient races.
### Souls
[[Eden]]ic Abominations' possess more purified souls, likely because of their proximity to [[Eden]]'s [[Edenic Mechanics#Magic|Magic]]. This has resulted in many Abominations possessing [[Edenic Mechanics#Runes|Runes]] deep within their souls that can be used to replicate many of their abilities when slotted into [[Edenic Mechanics#Fruits|Fruits]].
