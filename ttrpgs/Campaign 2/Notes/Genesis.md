# [[Genesis]]
## Overview
**Genesis** refers to the chief religious text of [[Dawnism]] and [[Elohim]], written by the prophet and [[Archon]] [[Cassiel]]/[[Denarion (Archon)|Denarion]]. In modern day, it is still used as a religious text but also as a first hand account in regards to the mechanics of [[Eden]]. 
## Content
Genesis is a vastly important piece of text in the history of [[Aravoth]]. In ancient times, it was the foundation by which the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] were formed, telling the story that laid the foundation for [[Dawnism]] and [[Elohim]]. More recently, it has been used by many of the [[Edenic Mechanics#The Redeemers|Redeemers]] for the purpose of understanding [[Eden]]. 
### Dawnism
> *Main article: [[Dawnism]]*

In the context of [[Dawnism]], Genesis tells the story of the primordial entity known as [[The Empyrean]] and how she provided the [[Aravothil]] with a paradise. The [[Aravothil]] then betrayed her, allowing a malevolent force known as [[Nihil]] to enter the physical realm, creating Death in the process. She sacrificed herself and birthed her [[Archon]]s in the process, who protected the world for their mother. It states that [[Nihil]] awaits in the shadows with his forces, the [[Dawnism#The Forsaken|Forsaken]], awaiting for a time to strike.
### Elohim 
> *Main article: [[Elohim]]*

A similar story is told, although the impression in [[Elohim]] is that the [[Archon]]s are meant to guide the [[Aravothil]] down the right path, rather than to protect [[Aravoth]].
### Eden
> *Main article: [[Edenic Mechanics]]*

The origin for the name of the continent of [[Eden]] is from the name of the paradise in Genesis. Furthermore, in Genesis it is written that after the [[Aravothil]] were cast out from paradise, [[The Empyrean]] died there, upon her [[Edenic Mechanics#The Throne of God|Throne of God]]. It is written that whomever consumes the Fruit of Infinity upon the Tree of Life would be able to revive [[The Empyrean]], and become her consort eternal. This account is the driving factor for Edenic expansion. 
## History
Little is known of its origins, beyond the fact that [[Cassiel]], or [[Denarion (Archon)|Denarion]], wrote it. It was translated into Tarsish sometime before the [[Old Dawnic]] was lost, which was then translated into New Dawnic. It is the most printed book worldwide.
