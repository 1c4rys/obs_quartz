# [[On the Metaphysics of Severance]]
## Overview
**On the Metaphysics of Severance** is a book written by an author dubbed only as [[Armaros]]. It appears as a leatherbound journal. 
## Excerpt
"The phenomenon of soul severance, herein referred to as *[[Nexomancy]]*, constitutes the deliberate or accidental bifurcation of the immaterial essence from its primary locus. Unlike mere displacement or possession, *[[Nexomancy]]* involves the creation of a secondary essence—what we might call the *simulacrum*—which retains aspects of the original consciousness while operating independently.

To understand this, one must first grasp the dual properties of the soul: its *principle*, the immutable core of identity, and its *veil*, the mutable layers of memory, desire, and perception. In *[[Nexomancy]]*, the latter is sheared away, forming a simulacrum imbued with partial autonomy, whereas the former remains anchored in the original vessel. This division, however, is not absolute; the two essences remain tethered through a metaphysical bond known as the *Soul Weave*, which is only physically visible within the parallel dimension hitherto referred to as the *Soul Realm*.

The process of *[[Nexomancy]]* is often achieved through the application of purified *[[True Darkness]]*, a reagent capable of disrupting the delicate cohesion of the soul’s layers. When introduced into the subject’s soul, it catalyzes a resonance that forces the *veil* to externalize. This externalization, while appearing seamless, carries profound risks. The newly formed *simulacrum* may exhibit instability, ranging from incomplete consciousness to an aggressive pursuit of autonomy, often at the expense of its originator.

Perhaps most troubling is the question of authenticity: does the *simulacrum* constitute a fragment of the original, or has it become an entirely distinct entity? Theories abound. Proponents of *Kyushimago* posit that the *simulacrum* retains an intrinsic connection to the original essence, akin to an echo reverberating within an infinite chamber. Conversely, the dominant theorum argues that severance induces a rupture so complete that the resulting entity, though derivative, is no longer tied to its source.

In the experiments conducted by the Archon *[[Samiel]]*'s personal scientists, subjects undergoing *[[Nexomancy]]* often described an initial sense of “unmooring,” followed by the distinct perception of their *veil* “unfolding” into the external world. These accounts provide invaluable insight into the subjective experience of severance, even as they raise unsettling questions about the nature of personhood.

Finally, one must consider the ethical implications. Is it justifiable to partition the soul in pursuit of knowledge or power? Or does the act of *[[Nexomancy]]* represent a fundamental violation of the self? These questions remain unresolved, though the practice persists, shrouded in both fascination and dread.
## Mechanics
This book acts as a "magical writing" that can teach the user the ritual spell *Simulacrum* in the form of a Level 4 Illusion/Arcane Spell:
### Simulacrum
**Cast** 1 day; **Cost** a piece of hair, drop of blood, or other part of the creature to be duplicated, plus rare oils, minerals, and pigments with a total value of 300 gp; **Secondary Casters** 3  
**Primary Check** [Arcana](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=2) or [Occultism](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=11) (master, the check has the [secret](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=142) trait); **Secondary Checks** Arcana, [Nature](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=10), Occultism, [Religion](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=13), or [Society](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=14) (whichever is required to [Recall Knowledge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=26) about the creature being duplicated); [Crafting](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=4); [Deception](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=5)  
**Target(s)** 1 living creature

---
You create an illusory duplicate of the target creature by animating ice or snow sculpted in its shape. The simulacrum is a 4th-level creature with no special abilities. If it needs to attempt a roll or use a DC, use the [moderate number for a monster](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=995), except as noted below. It doesn't have any specific memories from the target, but it can use information about the creature gained from any of the casters to [Impersonate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=46) the target. It looks exactly like the target and has a Deception modifier to Impersonate that creature equal to the modifier of the secondary spellcaster who rolled the Deception check, with a +4 status bonus.  
  
While it doesn't have any of the original's special abilities, like a dragon's breath weapon, the illusions that make up the simulacrum allow it to appear to use those abilities; they just never seem to have an effect. For instance, against a simulacrum dragon's breath weapon, all creatures in the area seem to critically succeed at their saving throws and take no damage. Creatures can attempt to disbelieve the illusion by attempting a Perception check against the Deception DC of the secondary spellcaster who rolled the Deception check.  
  
**Critical Success** You bring the simulacrum to life. It has the [minion](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=109) trait and is under your absolute control. You gain a direct mental link with the simulacrum and can spend an action to command the simulacrum via this link, even at a distance.  
**Success** As a critical success, but there is no special link between you and the simulacrum. You must spend an action to command it verbally or by some other means.  
**Failure** The ritual fails and has no effect.  
**Critical Failure** The simulacrum awakens, but it isn't your minion and is hostile to all the casters. It does everything it can to destroy them, but if it can't immediately slay them, the simulacrum tries to escape and plots their demise.
