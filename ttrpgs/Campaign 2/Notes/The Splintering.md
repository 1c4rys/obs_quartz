# [[The Splintering]]
## Overview
**The Splintering** is a mostly unknown event outlined in [[Genesis]], said to have been the culmination of the war between [[The Empyrean]] and [[Nihil]]. It is thought to have been the cause of the rapid spread of the continents across the globe, although continental drift is also a possible reason as to why. 

In [[Dawnism]], the Splintering is considered the event that marked the end of the [[1st Epoch]], and preceded the [[Nexus]]. 

%%

The God-Moon [[Aeternum]] sacrificed herself to save [[Aravoth]] from [[Nihil]]. [[Adam]], the Aspectbearer of [[Understanding]] was killed in the process, her fragments—the [[Splinter]]s—fell upon [[Aravoth]], embedding into the planet. The magnetic attraction of these [[Splinter]]s caused the tectonic plates to shift rapidly, leading to the geographical and societal separation of various populations.

## History
The separation and subsequent evolution of these societies were marked by the unique influences of each [[Splinter]]. The race of [[Precursors]], initially gifted with the [[Splinter]] of [[Understanding]] by [[Aeternum]], faced near extinction due to the inadvertent unleashing of [[Nihil]] during their attempts to travel backwards in time. [[Aeternum]] ultimately sacrificed herself to contain [[Nihil]], leading to the Splintering and the diffusion of the [[Precursors]] across [[Aravoth]]. 

The Splintering also brought about the onset of [[Edenic Mechanics#Magic|Magic]], which became accessible through the influence of [[Nihil]].
