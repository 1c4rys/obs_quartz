# [[Aeternum|Aeternum]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Aeternum Image]]

## Overview
Aeternum is the former third moon orbiting [[Aravoth]]. Scientists theorize that sometime approximately 100,000 years ago, Aeternum spontaneously disappeared.

The [[Elves]] believe that during this destruction, their race began, descendants of the surviving lunar people. 

%%

Aeternum blessed [[Aravoth]] with her Aspect of [[Understanding]], and her son [[Adam]], providing the [[Precursors]] sentience. However, after the [[Precursors]] unwittingly enabled [[Nihil]]'s escape from the [[Realm at the End of Time]], Aeternum was forced to sacrifice herself in order to imprison [[Nihil]] within [[Perdition]], raining her [[Splinter]]s across [[Aravoth]], enabling her children to ascend to godhood, known as the [[Archon]]s, and the birth of the [[Aravothil]], an event colloquially known as [[The Splintering]].

Aeternum is Eve, in some weird time bullshit way. The same way that Marika is Radagon, Aeternum is Eve.
