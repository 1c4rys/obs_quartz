# [[Azâzêl's Banishment]]
## Overview
**Azâzêl's Banishment** is a Fruit discovered within [[Dûdâêl]] named after [[ttrpgs/Campaign 2/Notes/Azâzêl]]. The item appears as broken manacles forged of [[Orichalcum]]. 
## Info
### Archetype
**Unique**: Bracelets (2)
*note: Can only equip [[ttrpgs/Campaign 2/Notes/Azâzêl]] Runes*
### Level
5
## Description
*"And heal the earth which the angels have corrupted, and proclaim the healing of the earth, that they may heal the plague, and that all the children of men may not perish through all the secret things that the [[Watcher]]s have disclosed and have taught their sons."* 
## Intrinsic
### Nightingale's Champion
Once per dawn, the wielder of Azâzêl’s Banishment can slam the manacles together as an action. For the entirety of this combat, the wielder gains the quickened condition but can only use the extra action each round for Strike and Stride actions.
### Special Intrinsic
???
### Might of Dûdâêl
Only while within the realm of [[Dûdâêl]], the following applies to this item:
- **Offensive**: Attack rolls with this weapon gain a +3 item bonus. In addition, this weapon deals four weapon damage dice. 
- **Defensive**: Increase this armor's item bonus to AC by 3. In addition, you have resistance 10 against all damage.
	- **Shield Archetype**: If a shield, only the following applies. The shield’s Hardness increases by 7, it gains an additional 108 Hit Points, and its BT increases by 54 (maximum 20 Hardness, 160 HP, and 80 BT). In addition, the circumstance bonus to AC while you have Raised a Shield is quadrupled. 
- **Unique**: This item grants a +3 item bonus to saving throws. In addition, your Penance is negated.
