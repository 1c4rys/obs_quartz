## Overview
**Turing** is an [[Automatons|Automaton]] from [[Esrith]]. He is Andrew's character in the campaign. 

His [[Edenic Mechanics#Penance|Penance]] is "Selfish". 
## Description
Turing is an [[Automatons|Automaton]] standing at nearly two meters in height, with a bulky frame. He has one large eye and is grey in color and rusted. He consumes his needed food and water via a hatch below his face. His features are rounded and his figure looks disjointed, as if it has been repaired a multitude of times. He speaks with a strong robotic accent. 
## Personality
Turing is fundamentally a good person who's kindness oftentimes makes him the butt of the joke, and treated as lesser by those around them. At least, that was up until [[Aule]] made friends with him, and treated him as *almost* an equal. He enjoys a good joke and has a slightly nihilistic worldview.
