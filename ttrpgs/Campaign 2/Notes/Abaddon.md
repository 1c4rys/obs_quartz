# [[Abaddon|Abaddon]]
## Overview
Abaddon is a region located in [[Meridiem]], entirely composed of [[Orcs]]. It is the entirety of the land to the west of the Sheol Mountains. The only thing separating Abaddon from the rest of Meridiem is [[The Wall]], a now-loosely armed defensive tool created by the last king of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]]. The land is volcanic, and there's little to no living things beyond the Orcs.

[[Aule]] battled here with his first cohort. 
## History
Knowledge about Abaddon is few and far between, as much information is inaccessible. But what researchers do know is that Abaddon initially was a location known as Aendor, a member of the Dawn Kingdoms. Members of every race except the [[Elves]] and [[Kitsune]] once called this place home--before some unknown event resulted in its collapse. Aendor was no more, and in it's place, Abaddon rose. The Orcs were no longer a peaceful people, corrupted by some dark magic, no doubt due to the machinations of their leader--[[Neton (ruler)|Neton]]. 

For some unknown reason, Neton (ruler)|Neton (ruler)|Neton, a once peaceful man who was closely allied with the last king of the Dawn Kingdoms, [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]], no longer felt this allegiance. [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]] quickly built The Wall, in a period of time that was inhuman, in order to battle the Orcs from a distance. However, this would not work for long, as [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]] gave [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]] too much lenience. [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]] would die soon after, the Dawn Kingdoms finished. 

However, as the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] fell, so did Abaddon. The once powerful and organized legion of Orcs collapsed, leaderless and weak. Only [[The Empyrean]] knows why [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]] abandoned his people. As of now, the [[Orcs]] of [[Abaddon]] exist only in scattered tribes, constantly battling one another to establish dominance, as they mindlessly journey around [[Abaddon]]. 
## Geography
> [!infobox]
> ![[Abaddon Image]]

Abaddon is a nearly entirely dead land covered in ash from the Sheol Mountains, which blows to the west. The Wall lies in the center of the Sheol Mountains. There are no remaining major cities in Abaddon, all lying in ruins. To the west lies [[The Splintered Sea]], and to the north is the southwestern reaches of the [[Indigo Strait]].
