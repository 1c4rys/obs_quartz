# [[Edenic Mechanics]]
## Overview
**Edenic Mechanics** refers to all information relating to how [[Eden]] functions, including  one's **Affinity**, one's **Penance**, the **Void Fruits**, the **Fruits**, **Magic**, the **Redeemers**, and more.

Individuals who consume Void Fruits and die gain an Affinity, granting them either magical or physical abilities, and a Penance, a curse that affects their life significantly. These Redeemers strive to reach the Throne of God, where they can ascend to godhood by consuming the legendary Fruit of Infinity. Magic, a force within Eden, fuels the Fruits and is accessible only to those with a soul.
## Affinity
An **Affinity** is the natural "blessing" given to those that transcend the mortal realm to [[Eden]] by consuming one of the Void Fruits and then dying. This blessing is heavily intertwined with Magic, as usually one's Affinity provides them the ability to wield Magic. In other cases, however, physical boosts are given in lieu of magical ability, although this does not mean that such people cannot wield magical Fruits. Affinities are usually based off of what would best fit the initiate as determined by the fates that be.

> *Metacommentary: Out of game, this is PF2e Character Creation. As in, the mechanical benefits received from Backgrounds, Classes, Dedications, etc., but not from Ancestries/Heritages.*

The exact progression path of an Affinity is unique between every individual. No two souls will have the same progression, although similarities certainly exist. In general, the blessings granted by an Affinity separate people into two groups: Martial and Spellcasting. Martial Affinities are those that provide the user with physical abilities otherwise impossible, while Spellcasting Affinities are those that provide an enhanced attunement to Magic. It is possible for one to have Affinities that are in both camps, although it is exceedingly rare. 
## Penance
A **Penance** is the downside to transcending to [[Eden]], providing the initiate with a curse that vastly changes their life in some way. For example, one might have a Penance that makes them blind, or causes them to lose their sense of self during the night time, or something far less detrimental. In general, the power of the Affinity scales directly with the Penance--the stronger the Affinity, the more detrimental the Penance. 

The Penance is given to someone the instant they enter [[Eden]], but not the exact phrasing. Instead, they are given nothing but the name--the limits of which they must figure out themselves. The name is more often than not purposefully confusing for the purpose of misdirecting, resulting in the deaths of thousands of young adventurers who were unaware of the true bounds of their Penance. 
## Void Fruits
The **Void Fruits** are a necessary item used for the purpose of becoming one of the Redeemers. The Void Fruits are created from the Void Trees, mysterious organisms that appear seemingly at complete random across the globe, bearing Void Fruits for consumption. Void Fruits have a deeply bitter taste to them, but once consumed in full, the final destination of the eater is vastly changed. Instead of death, they are sent to [[Eden]] and granted an Affinity and a Penance, thereby becoming one of the Redeemers. In addition, they would be given the ability to wield the Fruits. 
## Fruit of Infinity
The **Fruit of Infinity** is a legendary fruit written of in [[Genesis]] to allow the user the ability to ascend from mortal to god, and become [[The Empyrean]]'s consort. It is said to be upon the Tree of Life. 
## Fruits
> *Click [Here](https://www.desmos.com/calculator/3tsi5tcame) for a Desmos page for all the below formula (Max Attunement, Runeslots, Max Runeslots)*

The **Fruits** are ancient relics present within [[Eden]] that provide its user with some form of magical ability that transcends the ability of mundane people. Fruits are available in innumerable forms and categories, but they all have some form of Magic within them. Fruits typically scale in power with the difficulty of the area they are acquired from. Fruits are necessary for any Redeemer to survive in [[Eden]].
### Lore
The Fruits are incredibly similar to the [[Nexusborns]], although their exact form of differs. It is likely that in the original world the [[Nexusborns]] are from, they were used for a similar purpose. 
### Mechanics
Fruits are separated into three separate categories: **Offensive**, **Defensive**, and **Unique**. 

**Offensive** is self-explanatory. Any Fruit that is meant for offense is placed into this category. Examples include swords, guns, axes, bows, gauntlets, etc.

**Defensive** is, also, pretty self-explanatory. Any Fruit that is meant for defense is placed into this category. Examples include shields, armor, and clothing. 

**Unique**, however, is a far wider range. Anything not fitting into either of the previous archetypes is placed in this category instead. It is not common for one to come across a Unique Fruit, but when in higher level areas or if you're especially lucky, you might come across one. Usually, these come in the forms of talismans or accessories. 
#### Resonance
All Fruits must Resonate with your soul before its full power is available. It takes 8 hours to Resonate with a Fruit. You can Resonate with a total of 5 Fruits at a given time. It takes 1 hour to remove the connection.
#### Archetype
Archetype refers to the category that a given Fruit fits into. A given Fruit can only fit into one category. Uniques are not usually placed into categories. 
> *Metacommentary: PF2e gear system. As in, light armor, medium armor, heavy armor. That kind of stuff.*
#### Attunement 
Attunement is a metric that represents how much a weapon has etched itself into your soul. The more you train with a weapon and, especially, slay monsters, the more Attuned to the weapon you will become. 

Attunement scales with your level and the weapon's level. After all, a true master of their craft cannot become adept with a weapon that is far above their skill. The max Attunement is equivalent to: $$Maximum Attunement=floor((characterLevel\cdot10)\cdot\frac{\min(characterLevel,weaponLevel)}{\max(characterLevel,weaponLevel)})$$
Attunement is gained through the following methods, while Resonated with the Fruit:
- **Downtime**: "Train" downtime action allows you to gain 5 Attunement. 
- **XP**: Every 100 XP gained while the Fruit is equipped provides 1 Attunement.
- **Combat**: Every challenging combat completed while the Fruit is equipped provides 10 Attunement. If the majority of enemies you are challenging are entirely identical to one's you have fought before, you only gain 5 Attunement.
- **Apprenticeship**: For an extended period of time, weeks to months, along with a large amount of coin, you can hire a master that is proficient in the given **Archetype** of your Fruit in order to gain 25 Attunement points per week.   

#### Runes
Most importantly in making a Fruit as powerful as possible is through the use of Runes. Offensive and Defensive Fruits possess the ability to equip Runes, while with Unique Fruits it could go either way.

Runes are available as a particularly lucky piece of loot from every enemy. Usually, a Rune will modify the way a Fruit works, and only a limited amount can be applied. Because Runes are so common, they are some of the most sold items from [[Eden]]. 

The Runes you are able to equip on a Fruit scale based on the level of the Fruit. On a Level 3 Fruit, for example, you can only equip Runes of Level 3 or below. However, the number of Runes able to be equipped on a given weapon scales based on a different metric: **Attunement**. 

The formula for Runeslots is equivalent to: $$Runeslots=floor(3+{weaponLevel}+\frac{Attunement}5)$$
Runeslots do not necessarily denote the number of Runes you can equip, because the number of Runeslots a Rune takes up is not constant. Usually, the **Energy** of a given Rune is somewhere between 1 and 3 for the early levels, although many especially powerful Runes might be higher. Some Runes provide a negative and positive, instead of just a positive. These Runes are usually lower in Energy. Duplicate Runes cannot be placed on the same Fruit. 

Usually, a Rune is only useable on either an **Offensive** or **Defensive** Fruit, although some Runes are useable on both or neither. Some Runes will only work with certain Archetypes, too. 

Slotting in a Rune takes only a minute, but removing a Rune requires an hour of focused effort. 
#### Intrinsic
In addition to applying Runes, all Fruits possess what is called an Intrinsic. An Intrinsic is an immutable ability the given Fruit possesses, almost like an especially powerful Rune that cannot be removed. The power of an Intrinsic determines whether the Fruit has any value. Unique Fruits, since they sometimes do not accept Runes, are only considered valuable if their Intrinsic is especially powerful. 

Most Fruits have one Intrinsic, although some Uniques have more. Once a given item's Attunement is fully maxed and the player's level is at or above the weapon's level, an additional Intrinsic is revealed. 
## Magic
**Magic** is the all-encompassing term for all supernatural abilities present within [[Eden]]. Magic does not exist in [[Aravoth]] before [[Eden]], but the [[Nexusborns]] allowed for a form of proto-Magic which allowed for supernatural events to occur. Furthermore, the [[Abominations ]]that came to [[Aravoth]] after the [[Nexus]] frequently wield that form of proto-Magic. It is important to note that the form of Magic within [[Eden]] and the type that powers the [[Nexusborns]] are vastly different. Magic is also the force that powers the Fruits. 

> *Metacommentary: Kineticist and Thaumaturge magic isn't really capital-letter Magic, since it's more of a karate-kung-fu-type thing. The best way to rationalize Kineticist magic is through some interbreeding with Elementals, while Thaumaturge magic is best explained through Abomination stuff.*

There is very little that is understood about how Magic works. Many theorize that it is based off of some cosmic connection, but only one thing is truly understood--only those with a soul can become Redeemers and wield Magic. It is impossible to infuse Magic directly within mundane items unless it's through runecrafting or another Fruit/Affinity that makes it possible. 
## The Redeemers
The **Redeemers** are those that consumed the Void Fruits, gained their Affinity and Penance, and battle within [[Eden]] towards the constant goal of reaching the Throne of God and ascending to godhood. 
## The Throne of God
The **Throne of God** is the legendary location spoken of in [[Genesis]] said to be the center of [[Eden]], where [[The Empyrean]] herself lies, awaiting a champion to save her from her slumber. In doing so, the champion must consume the Fruit of Infinity from the Tree of Life, thereby ascending to godhood and becoming [[The Empyrean]]'s consort eternal.
