# [[Hella]]
## Overview
Hella is the primary deity in the religion of [[Helladism]]. It is believed that she will descend back to [[Aravoth]] after her nation of followers, [[Drachma]], populates all of [[Aravoth]]--including [[Eden]]. 

Hella entrusted each of the [[Watcher]]s with dominion over one aspect of the sentient psyche, in order to create the perfect society for Hella. 

%%

Hella and [[Nihil]] are one in the same. 

The story of Hella was described from [[Samiel]] to Drach I, beginning [[Helladism]]. The [[Drachmi]] unwittingly battle for the return of the world-destroying entity of [[Nihil]], a grand manipulation by the [[Forsaken]].
