# [[Precursors]]
>[!infobox]
>![[the-giant-by-goya.jpg]]
## Overview
The **Precursors** were the first race on [[Aravoth]], and the inhabitants of [[Eden]]. 
%%In fact, they were members of the [[Wildfolk]] that were blessed by [[Aeternum]]'s [[Splinter]] of [[Understanding]], gaining sentience. Living within [[Eden]], their ruler was the [[Archon]] [[Adam]]. The Precursor race unwittingly allowed allowed for [[Nihil]]'s escape in an attempt to travel backwards in time, leading to both the death of [[Adam]] and the destruction of their entire race, dispersed across the world to eventually grow into the [[Aravothil]] due to influence from the other [[Splinter]]s.%%
## Appearance
The Precursors were gigantic in size. %% They possessed brilliant white skin, along with varying hair colors along the lighter of colors. Their eye color varied wildly, depending on the type of clan, but the majority of Precursors possessed blue, white, or red eyes. The Precursors possessed little body hair, and appeared relatively close to humans in terms of physiology, although their bone structure was wildly different to allow for their large size. %%
## History
%%The Precursor race first existed in the form of th[](Wildfolk.md)]], beings that possessed little complex thought, before at the beginning of th[](1st%20Epoch.md)]], the God-Moo[](Aeternum.md)]] gave th[](Wildfolk.md)]] the power of sentience through he[](Splinter.md)]] o[](Understanding.md)]]. 

For the entirety of the [[1st Epoch]], lasting 930,000 years, the Precursor race flourished within [[Eden]], advancing their society with technology, since they did not yet have access to [[Edenic Mechanics#Magic|Magic]]. They invented Runecrafting and Automatons, along with advanced technology such as radio and rudimentary computing. With [[Adam]] at their helm, they prospered. However, the Precursor race flew too close to the sun--they betrayed their God-Moon, the God of Eternity, [[Aeternum]], and moved backwards in time utilizing the [[Splinter]] of [[Understanding]] and a world-sized Hadron Collider--resulting in the death of their race and the escape of [[Nihil]]. [[Adam]] was slain, and [[Aeternum]] sacrificed herself to save the [[Precursors]], dispersing them all across the world.%%

The [[Precursors]] were likely the ancestors of the modern-day [[Aravothil]].
