# [[The Silver Havens]]
## Overview
**The Silver Havens** is the ancient homeland of the [[Elves]]. It is the oldest society on [[Aravoth]]. Due to a lack of contact, almost nothing is known of this place.

It is thought to be located north of [[Ophiri]] and west of [[Nidavellir]], but only accessible to [[Elves]].
