# [[Rhiannon]]
## Overview
**Rhiannon** was one of the [[Archon]]s in the faith of [[Dawnism]], a child of [[The Empyrean]], and the [[Archon]] of Trickery. Their symbol was the smaller of the two [[Shepherds]], also called Rhiannon.

They are equated with [[Manekumi]] in the [[Kyushimago religion]], although [[Manekumi]] is usually portrayed as more feminine. 
## Description
Rhiannon is portrayed very differently depending on the source, but usually they are written to be rather short and effeminate. However, the predominant description is a figure with flowing dark hair and striking makeup, frequently smiling playfully, half-concealed by a mask, dressed in intricate robes adorned with subtle patterns.
## Personality
Rhiannon's personality is playful yet mysterious, always one step ahead in a game only they know the rules to. They exude charm and wit, often using humor and trickery to keep others guessing, but beneath the mischievous exterior lies a profound wisdom. Their mood shifts like the phases of the moon, at times distant and contemplative, and at others, warm and mischievously inviting, always dancing between the two. 
## History
Rhiannon was a child of [[The Empyrean]], and after being born to her, it was described in [[Genesis]] that they were profoundly disappointed in existence. They wanted a people that could engage them in conversation for even a moment before being defeated. This is why they created the [[Kitsune]], a race that mirrored many of Rhiannon's characteristics. Now, Rhiannon is said to still live within [[Kisia]], stirring chaos to teach lessons or, more likely, to amuse themselves. 

It is thought that they might have at one point been in a romantic relationship with [[Neton (Archon)|Neton]], their twin brother, although their love could very well be platonic. However, it is certain that [[Neton (Archon)|Neton]] and Rhiannon would do anything for one another, and love no one as much as their twin.
