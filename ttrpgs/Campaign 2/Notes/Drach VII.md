# [[Drach VII|Drach VII]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Drach VII Image]]
## Overview
Drach VII is the current supreme leader of the [[Drachma]] Empire and the highest authority of the [[Helladism|Helladist]] religion. He rules with an iron fist, born into the grand lineage that has led Drachma for ages. He is shrewd and calculating, possessing little worry for morality and heavily prioritizing the rule of law. His rule of Drachma is highly aggressive, often pursuing expansionist policies similar to those of his predecessors, resulting in both the attempted annexation of [[Tarsis]] and the conquering of more of [[Cyndra]]'s land. He chose his people well and crushed many plots against him. He was ruthless toward traitors and moved towards his goals with great determination.
## General Details
### Appearance
Drach VII, his true name Duny, is an older human male, nearing the twilight years of his life, although his hair remains an imperial black. He has ruled for nearly thirty years, aging him considerably, resulting in the formation of a widow's peak. He has dark brown eyes, like most [[Drachmi]]. He is tall and imposing, just over 6' in height, with sharp, angular features accentuating his power. He is clean-shaven, as the [[Drachmi]] culture leans towards, and favors ornate, regal robes mostly in black with accents of gold. From his earlier days in the [[Drachmi]] military, he possesses a large scar just above his right eyebrow. 
### Personality
He is cold, calculating, and imperious, exuding an aura of absolute power and regality. He rarely displays emotion, speaking with heavily measured words carrying the rule of law above all else. He seems uncaring, without worry for the death of many as long as it helps expand his empire. 
## History
### Early Life
Duny was the nephew of [[Drach VI]], and was third in the line for rule. Because of this, he enlisted in the military as a teenager, hoping to make a name for himself. In the next decade of his service, he grew quickly in the ranks to becoming a Sergeant Major, one of the highest ranks in the [[Drachmi]] military, both due to his strategic excellence and the way he commanded respect from those above and below him in the hierarchy. Due to this, along with the weakness of [[Drach VI]]'s son, [[Tiberius]], [[Drach VI]] personally chose Duny as his heir, passing the rule to him after [[Drach VI]]'s unexplainable death at just 47. 
### Emperor
Drach VII became the Emperor of [[Drachma]] 29 years ago, succeeding his uncle, [[Drach VI]]. Immediately, Drach VII enacted aggressive expansionist policies in [[Tarsis]], immediately conquering multiple city-states in the greater [[Tarsis]] area. Years later, he further invaded eastern [[Cyndra]], killing tens of thousands as he increased Drachma's size. Drach VII continues the tradition of rewriting history, utilizing it to explain away the widespread conquering as for [[Hella]], which was obviously a farce. In his military conquests in [[Tarsis]], he wrote De Bello Tharsis, an outline of his battles against what he viewed as barbarians, acting as both Commander In-Chief and Emperor, along with the Helladist leader. In the later years of his rule, Drach VII has been forced to grapple with resource strains on his vastly overexpanded empire, along with continued border skirmishes with the conquered people of [[Tarsis]], leading to little expansion in his twilight years.
