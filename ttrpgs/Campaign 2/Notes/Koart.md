# [[Koart]]
## Overview
**Koart** is a resident of the [[Realm at the End of Time]] and an ally of [[Tyrmorn]]. After he was afflicted with [[Dawnism#Death|Death]] by [[Baal]] as apart of their deal, he began to live in the [[Realm at the End of Time]], before eventually assisting the party in their journey to exit the horrid place.
