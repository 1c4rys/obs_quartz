# [[Kyushimago religion]]
## Overview
The **Kyushimago religion** is a faith in [[Kisia]] and some settlements in [[Ophiri]] and [[Meridiem]], circling around the two [[Shepherds]].

The larger of the two [[Shepherds]] is known as [[Manekugi]] (translated as "he who beckons") and the smaller is known as [[Manekumi]] (translated as "she who beckons"). 
## Beliefs
### Creation
It is believed that [[Manekumi]] and [[Manekugi]] are twins, children of the cosmos. Searching for millennia, they finally discovered lone star, without any habitable planet, wherein they built [[Aravoth]]. The two were also frequently represented as lovers. While creating the planet by themselves, [[Manekugi]] was accidentally killed in a practical joke by [[Manekumi]], therefore being sent to the Netherworld (Nenokuni). 

[[Manekumi]] succeeded in building [[Aravoth]], but as her life waned, she wished to see her beloved once again. She sacrificed the part of her that caused him to die, her whimsy, thereby creating the [[Kitsune]]. She made the voyage to the Netherworld and successfully brought [[Manekugi]]'s spirit back to the Physical Realm. In spirit form, the two orbited [[Aravoth]] for thousands of years, so they could look down upon their creation, creating a gravitational pull that resulted in the creation of the [[Shepherds]]. 
### The Three Worlds
The Kitsune believe that there are three worlds: the Physical Realm, the Spirit Realm, and the Netherworld. It is believed that throughout one's life, they will go to all three realms eventually. Rotating between the Physical Realm and the Netherworld during reincarnation, before migrating to the Spirit Realm upon the end of their reincarnation cycle. It is believed that the people who are sent to the Spirit Realm can still influence the world in a major way, looking over [[Aravoth]] as the emissaries of [[Manekugi]] and [[Manekumi]], becoming the asteroids of the rings of [[Aravoth]], also known as the Grandchildren.
### The Self
Due to possessing many forms, many [[Kitsune]] have variable forms of self, and alternative personalities to a point not present in any other race. Therefore, in order to preserve this sense of self, it is said all people’s personalities are gifts to be enjoyed. 
## Practices
### The Arc
During the [[Arc]] each year, it is believed that the Spirit Realm is closets to the Physical Realm, so in order to commune with one's loved ones, the [[Kitsune]] engage in festivals within [[Kisia]]. However, in order to avoid evil spirits from the Netherworld from seeing their faces and taking them to the Netherworld with them, all [[Kitsune]] wear decorative masks that block their face. Generally, traditional garbs passed down through the years are worn, in order to honor one's ancestors. 
### Death
Due to the great value placed on the modularity of water, [[Kitsune]] that die are sent across the water on a pyre boat, in the hope that the smoke formed from their corpse burning might rise and assist their soul in reaching the Spirit Realm. 
> *Metacommentary: Think Viking funeral*
## Similarities to Dawnism
Both [[Dawnism]] and the Kyushimago religion share the characters of the [[Shepherds]], who are both represented very similarly.
