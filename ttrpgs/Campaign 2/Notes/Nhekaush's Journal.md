# [[Nhekaush's Journal]]
## Overview
**Nhekaush's Journal** is a Fruit given to [[Nhekaush]] on his birthday by his parents, the last gift they would ever give him. It appears as a mundane, leather-bound journal with gold accents.
## Info
### Archetype
**Unique**: Grimoire
*note: Cannot equip Runes*
### Level
0
## Description
*"You feel a strange connection, as if the tome in your hand is a gateway to a higher plane. For even a moment, you feel the eyes of infinitely complex creatures wash over you. Then, the feeling is gone."* 
## Intrinsic
### Game Master's Encyclopedia
Nhekaush's Journal automatically chronicles all information [[Nhekaush]] understands into the leather-bound pages, infinite in length. Just by thinking of the idea, the page automatically flips to the page wherein the wielder wishes to go to. Others can read the book and understand it, but only the journal itself can compose information. It is only active when [[Nhekaush]] is conscious, and information cannot be composed if it is further than 30 feet from [[Nhekaush]]. 
### Fatebound
Nhekaush's Journal is fated to remain in his possession forever. Through no force can it be removed from his person, and is always accessible via a pocket dimension it fits safely within. At will, from any distance, Nhekaush can place it within his pocket dimension. 

No known force in the universe can remove this journal from [[Nhekaush]]'s possession or destroy it. Nhekaush's Journal also is permanently Resonated to [[Nhekaush]], although it does not count for his 5 slots.
