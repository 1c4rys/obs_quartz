# [[Kisia]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Shenmen_Dark_Forest_copy.webp]]
## Overview
**Kisia** (pron. "kish-uhh") is a shogunate composed of almost entirely [[Kitsune]]. Kisia is heavily isolationist, both due to their distance from the other continents and due to cultural differences, hence why the wide majority of [[Kitsune]] on [[Aravoth]] remain within their home country. Kisia is composed of one Shogun and five Daimyo, with the Shogun ruling over the five Daimyo in a feudal-type society. 
## Etymology
The country's endonym is **Kyushima**, meaning "island of the nine" in the native language. The term Kisia evolved from repeated anglicization over the centuries of relative contact
## History
Civilization on the island of Kyushima has persisted since the early 2nd Epoch. Only recently, around the year 1600 TE, however, has Kisia united under one flag, under the Amaterasu Dynasty. Over the thousand years that Kisia has existed, the ruling families excluding the Shogun have shifted slightly. 

In recent times, the Shogunate has decreased in strength under the leadership of Waru Amaterasu, the son of the previous Shogun and a man entirely unfit for the job. His engagement in less than savory acts in order to remain in power along with a lack of confidence in his own rule has led to many to consider a coup. The other Daimyo too possess a continued lack of confidence in the current Shogun, Of these Daimyo, the newest of them is particularly worried, the young Kikoshi Inari. 

As [[Eden]] became accessible to people for the first time, a small subset of the population, mostly up and coming Samurai, have been provided with [[Edenic Mechanics#Void Fruits|Void Fruits]] and sent to Eden in order to collect Fruits of value and increase the power of [[Kisia]]. 
## Social
Kisia is an acutely xenophobic country, unkind to anyone not a pure Kitsune, with the only exclusion being the small subset of [[Elves]] that intermingled with the Kisia. Social power on the continent is based on familial heritage, with the sons and daughters expected to take up their father or mother's work, or become an apprentice. 

The Daimyo are specifically selected by the Shogun, and the Samurai are chosen by the Daimyo. Samurai are the primary military force, therefore meaning they are expected to be elite in strength, which means they are chosen based on tail-count (the more tails, the stronger). The majority of Kitsune are around one- or two-tailed, a given town will have one or two three-tailed, a given region will have a half dozen four- or five-tailed, while at any given time there’s only three or four dozen six- or seven-tailed present as the most elite Shogun’s personal retainers. The social structure is the following:
- Shogun
- Daimyo
- Samurai
- Merchants
- Common people
