# [[Manekugi]]
## Overview
**Manekugi** is one of two deities in the [[Kyushimago religion]], the other being [[Manekumi]]. He appears as a morally grey pinnacle of masculinity, considered the strongest man. He is considered to be the spirit within the larger of the two [[Shepherds]], along with being commonly equated with [[Neton (Archon)|Neton]]. 
## Description
Manekugi is portrayed as a being of pure strength. His voice could be heard for hundreds of yards in every direction, a loud, resonate sound that inspired fear in all--even his fellow [[Deities|deities]]. His visage radiated bright shine of a blue moon and his hair burned with cosmic white. 

In his true form, he is a colossal fox nearly the size of a house, wielding a cyclopean sword in his maw. His fur is a light blue color, and somehow even as a fox he has muscle.
## Personality
Manekugi shows no mercy towards those he deems enemies. He used complete force to overwhelm his foes, of which there were many. He viewed anyone that posed a threat to his sister as someone that must die, and would kill them brutally to ensure others do not follow in their path. He is the encroaching snow, the invader, the blizzard destroying everything in its path, as well as the dead who rise from their graves. It was said that Manekugi never took more than one strike to claim victory. 
## History
> *Main article: [[Kyushimago religion#Creation]]*

He was born deep in the distant cosmos as twins with [[Manekumi]]. They created [[Aravoth]], but he was accidentally killed by her, so [[Manekumi]] sacrificed her whimsy, forming the [[Kitsune]] in the process, before escaping from the netherworld with her brother and becoming the [[Shepherds]].
