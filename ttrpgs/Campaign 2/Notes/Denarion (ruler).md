# [[Denarion (ruler)]]
> ![infobox]
> ![[Pasted image 20250215193250.png]]
> *Dawn King Denarion, years before his rule*
## Overview
**Denarion** was the first king of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], named after the [[Denarion (Archon)|god of the same name]]. He united the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] into one, and ruled for thousands of years. He was believed to be a descendent of the original [[Denarion (Archon)|Denarion]]. 
## Description
Average-height and hardy, little is known of his appearance beyond from accounts passed through the ages. However, it is well known that he had vibrant red hair, hence why he was connected to the Archon. He wore white and gold robes, and had a large, red beard, heavily braided. He was described as looking right past the prime years of his life, and had large scars across his face from battle as a youth. 

It was because he had such strong divine blood that he lived for so long.
## Personality
He was described as a wonderful king, and deeply persuasive. It required great power to unite the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], to the point where after his absence they collapsed. He was written to have a powerful and commanding figure, frequently believed that his divine blood was the strongest of all upon the continent except for perhaps that of [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]]'s. 
## History
### Early Life
Nothing is known of his early life, before the first written descriptions of his existence within early conflicts against the warriors of [[Abaddon]]. He quickly rose in the ranks in the [[Esrith]] military, and was widely respected by his men. He became a citizen of [[Esrith]] at this time, before he ventured off for many years, turning into almost a legendary figure. He returned, a hundred years later, to a vastly different [[Meridiem]]--wherein he was elected the Sovereign of [[Esrith]] by an incredible margin. Through both conquest and diplomacy, he convinced every nation from the [[Isles of the Blessed]] to the Indigo Strait to the reaches of [[Abaddon]] to unite under his banner, founding the [[Dawn Kingdoms]]. 
### Dawn King
Denarion ruled for centuries, as the Dawn King. He was well regarded as a king, and was able to meet the needs of many. Without great competition on a world stage, beyond the strongest warriors in [[Tarsis]], he had no problem ensuring prosperity for his people. Advances in science and technology were innumerable, as life expectancy and general quality of life skyrocketed. The [[Orcs]], for the first time ever, became heavily ingrained in society, while members of every race came to live within the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] to pursue economic prosperity and build their families. Rebellions occurred, but due to a lack of material greed, Denarion was able to ensure that the needs of the people were met. The [[Dawn Kingdoms]] have been described historically as a paradise--a place where needs were met, and society prospered.

In addition, he had an almost-supernatural ability to choose allies that were honest men, and through this, he was not once betrayed--until he was. 
### Brothers' War
> *Main article: [[Brothers' War]]*

[[Abaddon]] was always less connected to the greater [[Dawn Kingdoms]], and always had its own ruler. A man named [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]] eventually rose in the ranks, their strongest warrior, and challenged the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] directly. He initially followed the rule of Denarion, but that did not last long. [[The Wall]] was built, eventually, and before long, the [[Orcs]] marched to [[Empyr]], annihilating its populace and ending Denarion's life.
### Legacy
He has become a legendary figure in the history of the former [[Dawn Kingdoms]], with those that share his vibrant red hair being believed to be his descendants. Many have rose in power through the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] by claiming that they were the next coming of Denarion, the current king of [[Esrith]], [[Uther]], not an exception.
