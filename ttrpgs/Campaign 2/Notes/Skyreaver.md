# [[Skyreaver]]
## Overview
**Skyreaver** is a Fruit from the finger of the terrifying [Sky Dragon](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=1129) who was a guardian of a Void Tree. The handle is a scaly beige, while the blade itself is the dark blue claw of the finger. The blade is large enough to fit into a humanoid's hand comfortably. 
## Info
### Archetype
**Offensive**: Kukri
### Level
4
## Description
*"The legions of [[ttrpgs/Campaign 2/Notes/Azâzêl]] descend from the firmament in a vicious hurricane of [[Dawnism#Death|Death]]. The stars themselves bend to his will."* 
## Intrinsic
### Static Strikes
The Skyreaver builds static electricity as it moves. On the wielder's second successful strike in a turn, the spell Electric Arc is cast. One of the targets must be the enemy damaged by Skyreaver.
