# [[Neton (ruler)]]
## Overview
**Neton** was the king of a united [[Abaddon]] during the latter [[2nd Epoch]], named after the [[Neton (Archon)|Archon of the same name]]. He started the [[Brothers' War]], and swiftly defeated the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] and killed [[Denarion (ruler)|their king]], before disappearing. 
## Description
Little is known of his appearance. He is thought to be tall and vaguely Orcish. He wielded a large blade, and had hair lighter of color. He was an incredibly strong warrior. He wore nothing but battle gear. 
## Personality
He was ruthless and worried not for anyone he deemed a threat to [[Abaddon]]. He also, however, showed little care for his own people, throwing them into the [[Brothers' War]] despite knowing there would be hundreds of thousands of deaths. However, his was fiercely powerful, inspiring fear in his people. 
## History
He rapidly became the king of [[Abaddon]] despite being very young, and after the [[Duel of Giants]], he was able to quickly gain support for another war, the [[Brothers' War]]. After his apparent victory by slaying the king of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], he mysteriously disappeared.
