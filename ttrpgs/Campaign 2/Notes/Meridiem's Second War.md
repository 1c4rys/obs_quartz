# [[Meridiem's Second War]]
## Overview
**Meridiem's Second War** was the multi-year war taking place in the 2200s TE, wherein the Sovereign [[Uther]] of [[Esrith]] established [[Esrith]] in a bloody conquest across [[Meridiem]], using his [[Nexusborns]] to defeat his enemies easily. [[Valentia]] and the [[Free City of Letnya]] were allowed autonomy after the war because they pledged  [[Nexusborns]] for him for the entirety of the war. 

[[Uther]] won this battle and founded the nation of [[Esrith]].
