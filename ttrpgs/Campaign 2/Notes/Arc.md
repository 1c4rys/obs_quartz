# [[Arc]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Arc Image]]

## Overview
The **Arc** is a cosmic event visible from [[Aravoth]] that occurs on the Winter Solstice of every year. It occurs when the [[Shepherds]] "shepherd" the Grandchildren, the rings of Aravoth, around the planet in a way that creates a beautiful Arc when combined with the reflected light on the moons from the star at the center of the Aravothil system.

In the [[Kyushimago religion]], it is believed that the [[Kyushimago religion#The Three Worlds|Spirit Realm]] is closest to the physical realm during the Arc, leading to a great quantity of festivals occurring right around this time.  
## History
The Arc has occurred every year in history except for once, recently in 2639 TE. Instead of the moons creating the beautiful Arc, the [[Shepherds]] stopped in the sky, perfectly parallel. The Grandchildren followed, creating an incredible pull on the tides off the coast of [[Aravoth]]. This resulted in a great change in the world, as the lost continent of [[Eden]] was hoisted up from the depths, seemingly untouched by time. 

Through the work of many distinguished scientists in a university in [[Valentia]], it was determined that the last time this event occurred signified the separation of the supercontinent and the end of the [[1st Epoch]], nearly 110,000 years ago.
