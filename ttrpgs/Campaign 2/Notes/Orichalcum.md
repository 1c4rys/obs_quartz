# [[Orichalcum]]
## Overview
**Orichalcum** is a divine ore found only within the Isles of [[Nidavellir]], considered the strongest metal and the [[Dwarves]] primary economic boon. 
## Production
The raw ore was found deep in the mines of [[Nidavellir]]. It was believed that [[Eitri]] himself graced this place, causing the ore to turn divine. Orichalcum was a dark black, almost green color in its natural form, but when purified, it turned into a dark blue. 
## Properties
Per some sources, Orichalcum melted at an estimated 3410ºC, requiring exceptionally hot magical fires, and boiled at 5900ºC. The metal conducted [[Edenic Mechanics#Magic|Magic]] rather well, making it excel at compatibility with most enchantments. 
## Cost
Orichalcum is incredibly expensive. An Orichalcum drill could be purchased for around 340 tc, a set of shackles made of the metal for almost 700 tc, and a pure bar of the metal sells for the fair price of 17 sc. Hence, it is almost exclusively used by nobles and the stronger militaries, since it is so vastly expensive. 
## Applications
It was most used in armor and weaponry across the many militaries of the world along with private use. In addition, it was necessary for [[Nexomancy]], since only the powerful metal--which wouldn't rust for centuries--was the closest form to real skin possibly artificially. 

In addition, it is written that [[Moradis]] (or [[Eitri]]) wore bracers made of pure [[Orichalcum]].
