# [[Meridiem]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Meridiem Image]]
## Overview
**Meridiem** is the second-largest natural continent on [[Aravoth]]. It is where life first took shape outside of [[Eden]], and where the fabled [[Dawn Kingdoms]] originated, along with where [[Abaddon]] was located. 
## Etymology
The word "Meridiem" originates from a New Dawnic translation of an ancient term in the [[Old Dawnic]] meaning "south."
## Geography
The continent included terrain that was as varied as any other. To the south was the [[Isles of the Blessed]], and to the immediate north was [[Drachma]]. In the west, there was the Sheol Mountains, and past that was [[Abaddon]], the lost region entirely composed of warmongering [[Orcs]]. 

A variety of mountain ranges can be located in central Meridiem, while a large swamp is present in the far south. In the direct center, a modicum of a desert can be found, although it is far smaller than that of [[Tarsis]]. A large quantity of fertile land allows for great agricultural production, leading to a widely agrarian economy. Furthermore, the mountains provide a great source of steel, which the dominant economic power on the continent, [[Esrith]], trades extensively. 
## Countries
Currently, three large, independent nations exist in Meridiem, although there are a large quantity of city-states that exist especially in the south that are unaccounted for. In addition, [[Abaddon]] was once a true nation, but is now composed of dozens of chieftains leading large tribes of [[Orcs]], which does not satisfy the requirement for becoming a nation. Additionally, up until recently [[Cyndra]] was another nation, but they were recently turned into a [[Drachma]] satellite nation. These three nations are the following:
### Esrith
> *Main article: [[Esrith]]*

The largest of the nations within [[Meridiem]] and the dominant economic power, **Esrith** is composed of the remnants of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] of Esrith (the namesake), Rhiscar, and Aendor. 

Ruled by one Sovereign, Esrith is a constitutional monarchy founded due to the Sovereign's control over a large number of the [[Nexusborns]]. It is as close to a successor state of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] ever achieved, although it is still marred by problems.
### Valentia
> *Main article: [[Valentia]]*

A hub of culture and learning in the west, **Valentia** is home to the swordfighting technique of Tempo along with a vastly diverse population. One of the richest nations, especially when compared to their smaller population, they excel in brokering trade deals across [[Aravoth]], along with providing their superior navy to their allies like [[Esrith]]. 

Valentia is ruled by an Arch-Vicar that is chosen by the nobles through a long and convoluted process, and serves for his entire life. It is the only remaining country to have remained under the same governmental structure since the days of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]].
### Free City of Letnya
> *Main article: [[Free City of Letnya]]*

Located in former Telran, the **Free City of Letnya** was a city-state and the second-largest city upon the continent, directly bordering the [[Isles of the Blessed]] to the south. The southernmost major city in the eastern hemisphere, it acts as a buffer against the pirates of the [[Isles of the Blessed]].
