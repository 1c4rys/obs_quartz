# [[Guillaume Alighieri]]
## Overview
**Guillaume Alighieri** is the current Arch-Vicar of Valentia. A [[Halflings|Halfling]] in the later years of his life, it is thought that a great conflict will awaken within [[Valentia]] upon his death. He has ruled for nearly a century.
