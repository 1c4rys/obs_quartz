# [[Lilith]]
>[!infobox]
> ![[Pasted image 20241020165549.png]]
## Overview
**Lilith** is one of the [[Watcher]]s, and by far the most powerful of them. She is the equivalent to the [[Archon]] [[Adam]] in the [[Watcher]]s, acting as the latter's leader. She is enigmatic and rarely shows herself, although she did make a deal with [[Aule]] that resulted in [[The Sundering]]. 

Just like [[Adam]], she was once a member of the [[Wildfolk]].
