# [[Splinter]]
## Overview
The **Splinters** are fragments of the God-Moon [[Aeternum]], each embodying different Aspects. These Splinters played a crucial role in shaping the races and societies on [[Aravoth]] after [[The Splintering]]. The Splinters are [[Aravoth]]'s version of the fragments of the [[Cosmology of Eynsof#Narrative Seed|Narrative Seed]].

| Aspect            | Race           | Bearer      |
| ----------------- | -------------- | ----------- |
| [[Understanding]] | [[Precursors]] | [[Adam]] |
| [[Serenity]]      | [[Halflings]]  | [[Haniel]]  |
| [[Permanence]]    | [[Elves]]      | [[Raphael]] |
| [[Whimsy]]        | [[Kitsune]]    | [[Gabriel]] |
| [[Perseverance]]  | [[Humans]]     | [[Cassiel]] |
| [[Might]]         | [[Orcs]]       | [[Samiel]]  |
| [[Invention]]     | [[Dwarves]]    | [[Hasdiel]] |
These Splinters not only contributed to the physical and cultural evolution of each race but also imparted unique traits and abilities that aligned with their respective Aspects. The Aspects themselves became focal points around which the races developed distinct characteristics and societal norms. Due to the [[Precursors|Precursor]] race being influenced by [[Understanding]], all [[Aravothil]] are part [[Precursors|Precursor]]. 
## Aspectbearing Mechanics
The Splinters require a host to function, in the form of [[Aeternum]]'s seven children, the [[Archon]]s. The [[Archon]]s are colloquially known as Aspectbearers, as they each bear a [[Splinter]] of their one, an Aspect of [[Aeternum]] that they themselves embody. An Aspectbearer becomes immortal to death by natural causes, but can be slain by an entity of their power or above--of which there are very few. However, [[Adam]], [[Cassiel]], and [[Raphael]] are deceased, meaning their Aspects remains wielded by others, inevitably dooming the respective race, hence why the [[Precursors]] collapsed and the [[Humans]] are collapsing.
## History
During [[The Splintering]], wherein Aeternum sacrificed herself to imprison [[Nihil]] within [[Perdition]], six of the seven Splinters rained down upon Aravoth--all but Understanding. Understanding was gifted by Aeternum to the Aravothil, grooming them to become sentient lifeforms in the form of the [[Precursors]]. In a way, however, this was a mistake, for the newfound knowledge the [[Precursors]] possessed resulted in [[Nihil]]'s release from the [[Realm at the End of Time]] and his near destruction of the cosmos.  In modern day, the [[Archon]]s live in the fringes of society, not wanting to make themselves known, although the reformation of [[Eden]] has stirred the [[Archon]]s, in order to defend their mother's [[Aravoth]] from the evil force that is [[Nihil]]. 

In the case of [[Adam]], his Aspect remains unclaimed, being given to whomever reaches the center of [[Eden]] first. In the case of [[Cassiel]], [[Samiel]] stole it. In the case of [[Raphael]], it was unwittingly stolen by [[Aule]].
