# [[The Wall]]
## Overview
**The Wall** is the large, man-made object separating [[Abaddon]] from greater [[Meridiem]] on the border of the Sheol Mountains. It is the largest man-made object ever made. It was created early on in the [[Brothers' War]] for the purpose of deterring incursions by the [[Orcs]].

It was thought to have been built in a supernatural period of time, as some have cast doubt on the possibility that mundane [[Aravothil]] were capable of building it.
