# [[2nd Epoch]]
## Overview
The **2nd Epoch** took place immediately after the [[1st Epoch]], and during this period is when the first evidence of organic life outside [[Eden]] can be found. Society first took shape in [[Meridiem]], before spreading to western [[Ophiri]] and then elsewhere. For some reason, the sentient races of [[Aravoth]] were generally centered in their own area. The 2nd Epoch began due to the dispersion of the [[Aravothil]] across Aravoth, and ended after the fall of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] due to the death of [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]]. Sometime during the early parts of the 2nd Epoch, the [[Nexus]] occurred. 
## History
The first years of the 2nd Epoch were punctuated by rapid expansion throughout the many continents of the world. At the time, it is theorized that all of the continents were one large supercontinent, before separating into their own races and dispersing. This rapid shifting of the continents is not possible through continental drift alone, leading some to theorize how exactly the continents were able to shift so substantially in such a small period of time. Around this era, [[The Silver Havens]] and [[Nidavellir]] were habitated by the [[Elves]] and [[Dwarves]] respectively. The Nexus also occurred sometime before the separation of the supercontinent, allowing monsters from a nearby galaxy to spread to Aravoth, in the process bequeathing the [[Nexusborns]] upon the Aravothil. 

The middle period of the 2nd Epoch was the spread of [[Dawnism]], the chief religion of most of [[Meridiem]]. The first king of the Dawn Kingdoms, a man named [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]], united the continent under his rule shortly after the religion began to spread. 

The Dawn Kingdoms experienced great prosperity during this time, for the roughly one thousand years it existed, before in the short [[Brothers' War]], the [[Orcs]] of Abaddon, under the control of [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]], assassinated the king at the time, [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]], and fractured the once prosperous Dawn Kingdoms. This event signaled the end of the 2nd Epoch.
