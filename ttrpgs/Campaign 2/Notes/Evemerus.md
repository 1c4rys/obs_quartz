# [[Evemerus]]
## Overview
**Evemerus** is a city on [[Pangaia]] and one of the largest cities in [[Ophiri]]. Quickly growing due to its proximity to [[Eden]], Evemerus is high in population of both [[Aravothil]]... and [[Abominations]]. 
## Layout
The city itself is on a large hill, tapering upwards from the wide harbor. The closer to the northern forest, the poorer the people, as this is where most of the [[Abominations]] are situated.
