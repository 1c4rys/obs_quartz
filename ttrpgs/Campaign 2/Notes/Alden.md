# [[Alden]]
## Overview
**Alden** is a [[Edenic Mechanics#The Redeemers|Redeemer]] that defended the ferry from [[Abominations]] in the water as it journeyed through the [[Indigo Strait]]. He is from [[Esrith]] but currently in service of [[Valentia]]. 

He spoke with [[Nhekaush]] on his journey across the strait.
