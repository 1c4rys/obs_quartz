# [[Mordred]]
## Overview
**Mordred** is [[Arthur]]'s younger brother and the son of [[Uther]]. He is the current Sovereign of [[Esrith]]. 
## Description
Mordred is relatively young, having been king for less than a decade. He has pale skin and dark hair and his eyes are piercing and intense. He is lean and athletic, wearing armor decorated with sun-like symbols. Mordred's face is handsome, but his features are recorded as being a bit off--with a thin, cruel mouth and a twisted smile. He is relatively short. 
## Personality
Due to his youth and paranoia about assassination, Mordred's ability to rule was questioned by those under his rule and he maintained a weakened authority over the Thanes. He also has a strong complex about being compared to his father. He vacillates between extremely spoiled, demanding respect for the sheer fact of being the Sovereign, and fearful, keenly aware that he does not measure up as a ruler. Furthermore, members of his inner circle write that his paranoia has been furthered by his ability to see strange creatures in mirrors. He has narcissistic personality disorder, caring little for the feelings of others and being very concerned about his reputation.

While Mordred is by no means an exceptional leader, he does have some potential, albeit deeply-buried. Despite his own uncertainty in his ability to rule, he's capable of making decisive action and manages to control the Thanes, if just barely. 
## History
He was always the younger son, and never expected to become Sovereign. After [[Arthur]] ran away, and [[Uther]] was forced to past the metaphorical torch to someone new, that role fell upon Mordred.

Sovereign Mordred's rule has been described as a bastardization of his father's. While [[Uther]] ruled with an iron fist, squashing any and all rebellion, Mordred is barely able to control the Thanes and make decisive action. His biggest fault, however, is his uncertainty in his ability to rule. More than anything, he wants to surpass the legacy of his father and his brother, but his lack of confidence is his deepest vice.
