# [[Might]]
## Overview
The [[Splinter]] of **Might** was one of the seven [[Splinter]]s gifted to the denizens of [[Aravoth]]  by [[Aeternum]], each of which are pieces of her flesh. 

The [[Splinter]] of Might was given to [[Samiel]], a member of the [[Precursors]] who would later become the basis for the race of the [[Orcs]]. This [[Splinter]] blesses those born within its radius with the powers of Might, hence why the [[Orcs|Orcish]] race was formed. Currently, the Splinter of Invention is held by Samiel along with the [[Splinter]] of [[Perseverance]], as he strives for [[Nihil]]'s return across the galaxy.
