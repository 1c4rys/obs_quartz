# [[Whimsy]]
## Overview
The [[Splinter]] of **Whimsy** was one of the seven Splinters gifted to the denizens of [[Aravoth]]  by [[Aeternum]], each of which are pieces of her flesh. 

The [[Splinter]] of Whimsy was given to [[Gabriel]], a member of the [[Precursors]] who would later become the basis for the race of the [[Kitsune]]. This [[Splinter]] blesses those born within its radius with the powers of Whimsy, hence why the [[Kitsune]] race was formed. Currently, the [[Splinter]] of Whimsy is held by [[Gabriel]] within the eastern nation of [[Kisia]].
