# [[Azâzêl's Blame]]
## Overview
**Azâzêl's Blame** is a Fruit discovered within [[Dûdâêl]] named after [[ttrpgs/Campaign 2/Notes/Azâzêl]]. The weapon appears as a long golden rope with a extremely sharp dagger at the end. 
## Info
### Archetype
**Offensive**: Kusarigama
### Level
5
## Description
*"And make an opening in the desert, which is in [[Dûdâêl]], and cast him therein."* 
## Intrinsic
### Path of Corruption
Upon inflicting damage with Azâzêl’s Blame, the target gains the Corrupted condition. Each value of the Corrupted condition is equivalent to -1 on Will saves. If this value reaches 6, the target is afflicted by [[Dawnism#Death|Death]]. When an enemy is afflicted with [[Dawnism#Death|Death]], the wielder gains the Corrupted condition. A full rest reduces the Corrupted value by 1.

### Might of Dûdâêl
Only while within the realm of [[Dûdâêl]], the following applies to this item:
- **Offensive**: Attack rolls with this weapon gain a +3 item bonus. In addition, this weapon deals four weapon damage dice. 
- **Defensive**: Increase this armor's item bonus to AC by 3. In addition, you have resistance 10 against all damage.
	- **Shield Archetype**: If a shield, only the following applies. The shield’s Hardness increases by 7, it gains an additional 108 Hit Points, and its BT increases by 54 (maximum 20 Hardness, 160 HP, and 80 BT). In addition, the circumstance bonus to AC while you have Raised a Shield is quadrupled. 
- **Unique**: This item grants a +3 item bonus to saving throws. In addition, your Penance is negated.
