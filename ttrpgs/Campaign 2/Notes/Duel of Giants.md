# [[Duel of Giants]]
## Overview
The **Duel of Giants** was a war that occurred for 10 years in the early-[[2nd Epoch]] between the two giants of the continent of [[Meridiem]], the [[Orcs]] of [[Abaddon]] and the Dawn King [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]]'s alliance.  It culminated in the defeat of [[Abaddon]] and the creation of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]].
