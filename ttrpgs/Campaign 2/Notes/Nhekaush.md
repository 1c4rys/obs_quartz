# [[Nhekaush]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Pasted image 20250223192709.png]]
## Overview
**Nhekaush Siurana** is a [[Humans|Human]]-turned-dhampir from [[Valentia]]. He is Jayme's character in the campaign. 

His [[Edenic Mechanics#Penance|Penance]] is "Lost from Light". 
## Description
Nhekaush is a [[People#Valentine|Valentine]] [[Humans|Human]]-turned-dhampir with a height on the shorter side, around 5'10". His hair is a dark black and lies a few inches above his shoulders. He looks to be somewhere around his mid-30s, and has a small goatee. His features are angular and his figure is toned. He speaks with a heavy [[Valentia|Valentine]] accent. 
## Personality
Nhekaush is a deeply inquisitive person who, beyond all else, seeks to learn new things. His vampirification has created a deep mistrust of authority figures, although he has no qualms with assisting those in harm's way. His knowledge of folk spellcasting has merged with his great talent for the swordfighting technique [[Valentia#Tempo|Tempo]], thereby creating a unique fighting style characteristic of adventurers of yore. 
## History
He was born in [[Valentia#Concordia|Concordia]], the capital city of the north [[Meridiem]] country of [[Valentia]], to the ruling family of the city. Growing up spectacularly rich, a conclave of vampires led by [[Jacqueline de Trastámara]] assassinated his father and massacred his family, before promptly instilling themselves in power under the guise that the former ruling family simply abdicated. 

In the process, Nhekaush was transformed into a dhampir and, much to the chagrin of the vampires, survived the encounter, taking refuge in a city closer to the [[Esrith]] border. He eventually returned to [[Valentia#Concordia|Concordia]], many decades later, and become a professor at the university, focusing on [[Abominations]]--and, secretly, trying to find a way to become a [[Humans|Human]] again.
