## Description
*Wraiths represent a truly fascinating breed of Abomination--influenced by the Netherworld and the Realm at the End of Time, concurrently. This furious cocktail of darkness produces one of the most formidable entities known to walk—or, more accurately, to hover above—the mortal plane.*
## Ability
### Ethereal Strike
**Offensive**: Your weapon can affect incorporeal creatures as if they were corporeal. Additionally, the weapon deals an additional 1d6 negative damage to living creatures. Against incorporeal undead, you ignore their resistance to physical damage.
