# [[Cyndra]]
## Overview
**Cyndra** is a former member nation of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], now a satellite of [[Drachma]]. They have little autonomy, and act as a [[Drachma]] outpost on the continent of [[Meridiem]].
## History
Cyndra was united under King [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]] as the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] were established, and after the [[Brothers' War]], the line of kings was preserved--a feat only one other nation, [[Valentia]], was able to accomplish. This continued up until, in a dangerous move, [[Drach VII]] conquered Cyndra for [[Drachma]] in a bloody conquest, killing tens of thousands and wiping out the royal family. Cyndra now exists as a puppet nation, led by [[Drach VI]]'s son and former heir to the throne, Gorteth [[Tiberius]].
