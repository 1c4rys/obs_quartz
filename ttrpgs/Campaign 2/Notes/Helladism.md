# [[Helladism|Helladism]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Helladism Image]]
## Overview
 Helladism is the national religion of [[Drachma]], and is heavily intertwined with Drachma's government. The leader of the Church is the Drach, currently [[Drach VII]], and it is believed that only the Drach follows the Helladist god, [[Hella]]. All other followers of Helladism must follow [[Hella]] through the Drach, in the supreme goal of uniting all of the world under one ruler, in order to eventually allow [[Hella]] to descend back down to [[Aravoth]] and live among her people. Nearly every country, excluding [[Kisia]], has a population of Helladists, although the wide majority are within Drachma.

## Beliefs
The Helladist text, the Hagia Graphi, recounts the origin of Aravoth. It tells of Hella, who traversed the cosmos for eons in search of a world. Upon discovering Aravoth, she dwelled among its inhabitants. However, [[Hella]] found the people devoid of compassion and moral virtue. Realizing the need for renewal, she resolved to reshape the [[Aravothil]], infusing them with empathy and joy. An ancient entity known as [[Iumater]], who had long influenced Aravoth, perceived Hella's actions as a threat. In a final act of defiance, Iumater cast [[Hella]] from the world and faded from existence, allowing the Aravothil to flourish anew. Now, the [[Drachmi]] strive to facilitate Hella's return while thwarting forces that seek to revive Iumater's legacy.

It is believed that as [[Hella]] was sent away, the [[Watcher]]s remained, eight beings entrusted by [[Hella]] to defend the world from those who wished to do it harm. Each of the [[Watcher]]s have dominion over some form of the sentient psyche, as they are said to guide followers of Helladism to returning [[Hella]] to the physical realm.
