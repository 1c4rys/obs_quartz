# [[Esrith]]
> ![infobox]
> ![[Dragon_and_knights.jpg]]
> *The battle to reclaim Lothian*
## Overview
**Esrith** is a hierarchical, constitutional monarchy established in the 2200s TE in the the former [[Dawn Kingdoms]] in eastern and central [[Meridiem]], composed of Rhiscar, Aendor, and the former Esrith. It is the closest to a successor state of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] ever achieved, although it is still marred by problems. 

It is the primary competitor to [[Drachma]]'s economic control over the west. 
## History
Esrith was founded in the 2200s TE, taking its namesake from the original nation existing there before the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] were founded. Its first king [[Uther]], red of hair and the king of the city-state Lothian, founded the nation through conquest, by using a particularly strong [[Nexusborns|Nexusborn]]--[[Caliburn]]. With this duplicitous blade, it was said the wielder could not die of natural causes. He fought against the many other city-states within [[Meridiem]], subjugating them under his will. Similar to [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]], he provided those with [[Nexusborns]] with high ranks in his government, convincing many to assist him in his conquering. 

In a long struggle, hitherto referred to as [[Meridiem's Second War]], he conquered large swaths of land in [[Esrith]], Aendor, Rhiscar, and even some of Telran. In doing so, he allied with both [[Valentia]] and the [[Free City of Letnya]], promising them autonomy in return for pledging their [[Nexusborns]] to him for the next decade. He won this war, and was officially titled the Sovereign of Esrith afterwards. With the undying [[Caliburn]] by his side, he lived for more than 400 years, before recently passing the sword to his son, [[Arthur]], who mysteriously disappeared soon after receiving it. Instead, the burden of ruling Esrith was given to [[Arthur]]'s younger brother, [[Mordred]].

Sovereign [[Mordred]]'s rule has been described as a bastardization of his father's. While [[Uther]] ruled with an iron fist, squashing any and all rebellion, [[Mordred]] is barely able to control the Thanes and make decisive action. His biggest fault, however, is his uncertainty in his ability to rule. More than anything, he wants to surpass the legacy of his father and his brother, but his lack of confidence is his deepest vice. Because of this, Esrith is weaker than ever before, just moments from rebellion, as many citizens wonder: "Where is [[Arthur]]?"
## Government
The government of Esrith is multifaceted and efficient, with a hierarchical system established that allows each region to assist the whole. The leader of the government is the Sovereign, currently [[Mordred]]. 

Beyond the Sovereign, there are eight principalities ruled by Thanes. Nearly every principality is named after the original city-state or tribe they once belonged to, and each has its own military, entirely independent of one another. While the Thanes do work together under the Sovereign, the Sovereign's control is limited, maintained mostly through his control over the [[Nexusborns]]. Borders between the principalities are largely defined by natural borders. The Thanes are hereditarily chosen. There is no form of democracy within Esrith. 

Each principality has a specific objective that the entire principality is centered around:
- Defense
- Law
- Taxation
- Agriculture
- Commerce
- Loyalty
- Faith
- Culture
The sole purpose of each principality is to be designated that goal and accomplish it, allowing the greater Esrith to prosper due to it. If any principality falls short, they all fall. 
## Culture
The Esrithil culture is a blend of ancient traditions and newer customs that emerged after the unification under Sovereign [[Uther]]. Esrithil society places great emphasis on loyalty to one's lord and kinship bonds. The concept of warrior bands sworn to a leader is prevalent, particularly among the nobility and military classes.

Feasting halls play a central role in Esrithil social life, where Thanes host elaborate banquets for their retinues. These gatherings serve as venues for strengthening alliances, settling disputes, and sharing news. Bards are highly respected for their ability to recount heroic tales and preserve the nation's history through oral tradition.

The Esrithil have a rich tradition of craftsmanship, particularly in metalworking. Their exceptional steel-forging techniques are complemented by intricate designs influenced by their cultural heritage. Jewelry and weapons often feature complex knotwork patterns and animal motifs, showcasing the artistic prowess of Esrithil artisans.

Lastly, the Esrithil have developed a complex system of oaths and honor-codes. These verbal contracts are considered binding and play a crucial role in maintaining social and political relationships throughout the realm.
## Society
Esrithil society is divided between the slaves, commoners, the military, and the ruling class. The last of which is in turn divided between the nobility and the monarchy. 
### Nobility
The nobility of Esrith were composed of the most wealthy and influential households, the majority of which are in some way related to the eight Thanes. Nobles were born, not made, but there were exceptions in the form of local lords. All nobles in Esrith were those red of hair, due to the connection to [[Denarion (Archon)|Denarion]] the [[Archon]] that is believed to be possessed due to this. The majority of the noble families were Clans that began during the [[Dawn Kingdoms]]. 

Local lords, appointed to govern localities in the name of the crown, were a subclass in relation to the Esrithil noble houses, referred to as the "landed nobility" in order to distinguish the two. The crown could appoint a notable commoner, who has either distinguished himself or been recommended by the townspeople, or someone who is the default ruler of a particular place by their personal influence alone. However, because local lords must be nobles, the appointment of a commoner serves to ennoble the individual, who is granted a noble title. They did not necessarily have to be red of hair. 

Esrith's laws were explicitly written to reflect that the noble houses enjoyed their lands and titles at the "favor of the crown", meaning a family could lose their lands and titles if they were disloyal to the nation--or failed at their duty.
### Military
The military of Esrith is incredibly large and deeply hierarchical. Each principality possesses their own military, but the Thane of Defense has the ability to assign up to one-half of a given Thane's military forces, but not the crown's, to a given duty. 

At the top of the military hierarchy are wielders of the [[Nexusborns]], of which there are more than three-dozen across Esrith. Second in command is generals appointed by the Thane of Defense--mundane, red-haired warriors who have proven themselves to be worthy of promotion, or particularly powerful [[Edenic Mechanics#The Redeemers|Redeemers]]. Then, a complex line of military personnel followed, with those with red-hair placed above. 

Members of the military are automatically raised above the commoners, with the [[Nexusborns]] being viewed as almost pseudo-nobility. It was common for every Thane to have a party of three or four wielder of [[Nexusborns]] as his personal entourage, known as the **Knights**. 
### Commoners
The common folk of Esrith were those were not from a noble family, the military, or were a local lord. The commoner class itself was split into two divisions: red-haired and dark-haired. Those with red hair were automatically viewed as superior, because they had "royal blood", with those of dark hair treated with prejudice by the red-haired. Those with dark hair were frequently referred to as "mongrels" by those higher in society, although it was less common in modern day. 

The highest role a non-military commoner could gain, except for local lord, was joining the merchant class. The merchant class allowed Esrith to have such a large economy, so they were deeply valued. Merchant influence was seen frequently when a merchant might save a noble house from financial trouble. Despite this power, the merchants of Esrith always viewed the interests of their nation above their own, primarily due to the favorable merchant mercantile conditions maintained by the Thane of Commerce. 
### Slaves
The slave society in Esrith gets smaller and smaller by the day, but it still exists. Mostly populated by prisoners of war from [[Abaddon]] and [[Ophiri]], slavery exists as an economic boon for farmers in western Esrith, near to [[Abaddon]]. The workers who were [[Orcs]] were mighty and able to complete large quantities of manual labor, hence why the Thane of Law has been apprehensive to totally ban it. However, as the population of [[Orcs]] in eastern [[Meridiem]] wanes, inevitably slavery will not exist anymore--hopefully.
## Economy
Esrith has an incredibly strong economy. Due to their alliance with [[Valentia]], they are able to reap the rewards of their seafaring technology, providing Esrith with large cabals of merchant ships. Esrith has many large mountain ranges, which allows for a strong iron and steel business. The Esrithil steel-forging method is the greatest in the world, leading to great trade with the many cities of [[Drachma]] as they expand, which is why Esrith’s economy is the largest in [[Aravoth]]. Additionally, the wide fields of Esrith allow for a prosperous farming economy. 
### Currency of Esrith
Following the same currency as the original [[Dawn Kingdoms]], the Esrithil currency is separated into three coins: cherubs, thrones, and seraphs. 1 throne is equivalent to the weight of 1/1000th of the average bushel of wheat in gold. 100 cherubs was equivalent to 1 throne, and one-thousands throns were equal to 1 seraph. 

- The throne had an inscription of a wheel with an open eye located upon the arc. 
- The cherub had an inscription of a single-eyed bird.
- The seraph had an inscription of a six-winged bird covering its eyes. 
## Geography
Esrith is located in central [[Meridiem]], east of the Sheol Mountains and south of [[Valentia]]. 

Due to its sheer size, the many ecosystems present within Esrith is innumerable. From wide mountain ranges, to swamps, to lagoons, to coves, Esrith has it all. This allows for great diversity in the trading portfolio.
## Notable Locations
### Lothian
Lothian is the current capital of Esrith, its largest city, and the third-largest city in [[Meridiem]].
