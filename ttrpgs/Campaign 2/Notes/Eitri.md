# [[Eitri]]
## Overview
**Eitri** is the central god of the [[Nidavellir]]ian religion of [[Fornsidr]]. Equated with the [[Dawnism|Dawnic]] [[Moradis]], he is said to have forged the [[Dwarves]] within the center of [[Aravoth]], with [[Nidavellir]] as the gateway there. He is also said to have created [[Orichalcum]], and is the brother of the legendary craftsman [[Thordin]].
