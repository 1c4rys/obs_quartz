# [[Watcher]]
## Overivew
The **Watcher**s are beings considered to be the angels of [[Hella]]. In [[Eden]], however, the Watchers are mentioned extensively as the enemies of [[The Empyrean]] and the [[Archon]]s, leading some to question if they are in fact benevolent or not. 

There is one known [[Watcher]]: [[ttrpgs/Campaign 2/Notes/Azâzêl]]. However, it is likely there are many more that are simply unknown.
