# [[The Empyrean]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[The Empyrean Image]]
## Overview
**The Empyrean** is the primary deity in the faith of [[Dawnism]] and the mother of the [[Archon]]s. It is written in [[Genesis]] that she engaged in a cosmic battle with [[Nihil]], sacrificing herself in the process, thereby separating the supercontinent and ending [[Nihil]]'s machinations once and for all... hopefully. 

Due to the stories told in [[Genesis]], many believe that the grand goal of the [[Edenic Mechanics#The Redeemers|Redeemers]] is to reach the end of [[Eden]] and ascend to godhood, reviving the Empyrean and becoming her consort eternal.
## Description
The Empyrean is rarely represented as a humanoid, usually being portrayed as a massive celestial body composed of pure, white hot flame. However, when she is portrayed as a humanoid, she is portrayed with fair skin and long, blonde hair, along with a beautiful, flowing dress. Her eyes were a stark white, and her face was motherly and mature. 

She is considered the creator of the universe and the basic essence and the source of all creation. She is the most venerated of all deities in [[Dawnism]]. 
## Personality
The Empyrean is portrayed as morally good, although it is difficult to define the morals of her because she is so incredibly inhuman. She believed that life was the most precious of things, hence why she created it, and deeply detested [[Dawnism#Death|Death]]. She viewed the [[Aravothil]] as her children, and wished to take care of them like a mother might. 
## History
> *Main article: [[Dawnism#History]]*

The Empyrean was believed to have created the physical realm from nothing, then birthed the [[Archon]]s whom created the celestial bodies. [[Aravoth]] was established before [[Nihil]] appeared, as the Empyrean was forced to create [[Dawnism#Death|Death]] to save the [[Aravothil]]. The Empyrean then sacrificed herself to imprison [[Nihil]] within [[Perdition]].

It is believed that the Empyrean's body lies at the end of [[Eden]], and upon consuming a [[Edenic Mechanics#Fruit of Infinity|Fruit of Infinity]], one will become a god. This newfound god is then able to revive her, becoming her consort forever.
