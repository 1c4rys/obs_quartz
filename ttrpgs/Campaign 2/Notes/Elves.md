# [[Elves]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Elf01.png]]
## Overview
The **Elves** are one of the [[Races|races]] of [[Aravoth]]. Native to [[The Silver Havens]], they are a tall, long-lived people with a strong tradition of art and deep connection to nature. 

The majority of Elves lived in [[The Silver Havens]], leaving only a small population of Elves left that lived in the rest of the world. It is likely that by the end of the [[3rd Epoch]], Elves will be extinct. 
## Description
Elves tended to be fair and beautiful by the standards of [[Humans]], though they were often frail as well. Elves stood on average 5′6″‒6′2″ (1.6‒1.8 m) and weighed 130‒170 lb (59‒77 kg). Elves had a similar range of complexions to humans, with native Elves typically coppery or pale skinned and migrant Elves having darker pigmentation. Often, Elven hair was dark, either brown or black, with copper red or blond hair also found amongst many Elves, although orange or even green hues were not completely unheard of. Elven eyes were commonly brown, hazel, or an emerald green. Elves had pointed ears and had no hair on their body save eyebrows, eyelashes, and scalp hair. 
## Personality
Elves value kindness, intellect, and beauty, with many Elves striving to improve their manners, appearance, and culture. Their studies delve into a level of detail that most shorter-lived peoples find excessive or inefficient. Elves are often rather private people, steeped in the secrets of their groves and kinship groups. They're slow to build friendships outside their kinsfolk, as Elves who spend their lives among shorter-lived peoples often become morose after watching generations of companions age and die. Elves were a relaxed people, with a degree of patience and detachment granted to them by their long lifespans.
## Society
### Culture
Elves had an affinity for freedom more than anything else, preferring the way of unrestricted liberty to the restraints of civilized law and order. Liberty, both of one's self and of others, tended to be the greatest virtue in Elven cultures. This love for freedom was often tempered by a good and generous nature, though not always.

Due to the Elves long-lifespan, an isolationist culture was adopted in order to avoid forming relationships with people who lived for fractions of the lifespan of the Elves.

Elves tended to take up the adventurer's lifestyle for a number of reasons. Some did so out of, more than anything, boredom or a sense of wanderlust that drove them to explore beyond the boundaries of their homelands.
## Ability
Elves were universally long-lived, achieving what they consider adulthood at 180 years of age and living for up to 1200 and more years thereafter. [[Edenic Mechanics#The Redeemers|Redeemers]] of Elven descent also possessed far greater of an attunement to [[Edenic Mechanics#Magic|Magic]], for an unknown reason.
## History
Like many races, the exact origins of the Elves were lost in myth and legend. It is believed by the majority of the Elves that they are closest to deities of the [[Aravothil]], primarily because they believe they were the first race, born during the tumult of [[Aeternum]]'s destruction. From what is known, the Elves do not really have any form of organized religion. [[The Silver Havens]], however, are deeply important in their society, to the point where few Elves ever venture away from it. Almost zero non-Elves ever ventured to [[The Silver Havens]], so almost nothing is understood of what made [[The Silver Havens]] so important. 

The first known Elves appeared during the [[2nd Epoch]], alongside many of the other races. A small subsect of Elves migrated to [[Kisia]] during this time, for an unknown reason, but quickly gaining the respect of its denizens. 

Sometime during the [[2nd Epoch]], after Elves were first sighted, the leader of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] learned of [[The Silver Havens]]' existence, creating a bond that would stand the test of time. Inevitably, [[Drachmi]] from [[Ophiri]] learned of its existence and location just to their north, and attempted to journey there, but would always fail. It is unknown if these adventurers survived.

Eventually, as the [[3rd Epoch]] arrived and [[Nidavellir]] was faced by the threat of colonization, all attempts by the Drachs of [[Drachma]] to conquer [[The Silver Havens]] failed without any measure of success. In some way, the defense system of [[The Silver Havens]] was nigh-impenetrable. 

In modern days, the many Elves exist abroad, most focused in [[Kisia]] but also present in [[Esrith]] and [[Nidavellir]] in strong populations.
