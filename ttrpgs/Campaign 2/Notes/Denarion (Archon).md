# [[Denarion (Archon)]]
## Overview
**Denarion** was one of the [[Archon]]s in the faith of [[Dawnism]], the son of [[The Empyrean]], and the [[Archon]] of Commerce. His symbol was that of the [[Arc]].  
## Description
He was portrayed as an aged man with dark red hair, although it is likely that his appearance and that of the [[Denarion (ruler)|king of the same name]] have been inadvertently intertwined. He is the most well-recorded of the [[Archon]]s, possessing powerful blue eyes and wearing minimalistic clothing.  
## Personality
Denarion was stoic and steadfast, although he possessed a love for especially rare curios from across [[Aravoth]]. He was wise beyond his years and an excellent writer, seen through how he was able to write the [[Genesis]] as his alias of [[Cassiel]]. 
## History
Not an exception to the other [[Archon]]s, little is known of his life. He undoubtedly lived outside of [[Eden]], given that he was well-recorded under the alias of [[Cassiel]], although his divinity was not proven. In modern times, it is theorized that he still wanders [[Meridiem]], leading many to dedicate their lives to seeking him.
