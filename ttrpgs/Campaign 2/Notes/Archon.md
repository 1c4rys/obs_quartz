# [[Archon]]
## Overview
An **Archon** is the name of the [[Deities|deities]] in [[Dawnism]], beings of great power. Dawnic followers believe that the Archons are beings that are deeply engraved within the world, although there is little direct evidence of this. The profiles of the Archons can be found in nearly every religion on the planet, a notable exception being [[Helladism]]. 

Typically, [[Alaric]] is placed at the highest among the Archons, the closest to their mother. 
## Description
All Archons are considered to be the children of [[The Empyrean]], the divine creator of the world. The belief is that while [[The Empyrean]] created the physical realm, the Archons were the ones that built the celestial bodies. All of which were considered to have controlled some aspect of mortal affairs, many of which had multiple. 

Archons are believed to possess some form of omnipotence, able to both shape the physical realm to their wishes, along with possessing a limited amount of control over life and death. It is considered impossible for any number of mortals without divine blood to defeat an Archon in a battle. Their appearance throughout different historical contexts greatly differ, although, with the exception of [[Rhiannon]], their sex is not variable. 

It is believed that many followers of Dawnism have the blood of the Archons within them. For example, those with dark red hair are thought to be the ancient descendants of [[Denarion (Archon)|Denarion]], the Archon of Commerce.
