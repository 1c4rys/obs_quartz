# [[Thordin]]
## Overview
**Thordin** was a legendary figure in the culture of the [[Dwarves]]. He is considered to be the brother of the deity, [[Eitri]], and a master craftsman. He was thought to have lived in the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] for a large quantity of time.
