# [[Perseverance]]
## Overview
The [[Splinter]] of **Perseverance** was one of the seven [[Splinter]]s gifted to the denizens of [[Aravoth]]  by [[Aeternum]], each of which are pieces of her flesh. 

The Splinter of Perseverance was given to [[Cassiel]], a member of the [[Precursors]] who would later become the basis for the race of the [[Humans]]. This Splinter blesses those born within its radius with the powers of Perseverance, hence why the [[Humans|Human]] race was formed. Currently, the Splinter of [[Perseverance]] is held by [[Samiel]] along with the Splinter of [[Might]], as he strives for [[Nihil]]'s return across the galaxy. [[Samiel]] slayed [[Cassiel]] years before, during the [[Brothers' War]], ending the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], and resorbed the power for himself.
