# [[Jacqueline de Trastámara]]
## Overview
**Jacqueline de Trastámara** is the current countess of [[Valentia#Concordia|Concordia]], the capital of [[Valentia]]. In fact, she is a True Vampire, thousands of years old. She orchestrated the extermination of [[House Siurana]] and unwittingly turned [[Nhekaush]] into a Dhampir.
