# [[Neton (Archon)]]
## Overview
**Neton** was one of the [[Archon]]s in the faith of [[Dawnism]], the son of [[The Empyrean]], and the [[Archon]] of War. His symbol was the larger of the two [[Shepherds]], also called Neton.

He is equated with [[Manekugi]] in the [[Kyushimago religion]]. 
## Description
Neton stands tall with an imposing, muscular frame clad in dark, scale-like armor. His face blends Orcish and human features, crowned by waist-length pale blue hair. He wields a massive odachi and eschews helmets, showcasing his confidence in battle. His presence exudes an aura of raw strength and martial prowess befitting an [[Archon]] of War.
## Personality
Neton embodies the ruthless nature of war itself. He shows no mercy to those he deems enemies, employing overwhelming force to crush opposition. His moral compass is as grey as the smoke rising from a battlefield - he'll protect allies with ferocious loyalty but annihilate perceived threats without hesitation. Neton's wrath is likened to a devastating blizzard, leaving destruction in its wake. 

Legends speak of his ability to claim victory in a single, decisive strike. While not inherently malevolent, Neton's single-minded pursuit of martial supremacy often blurs the lines between necessary violence and cruel excess. He respects strength above all and holds little regard for those he considers weak or indecisive.
## History
Neton was the son of [[The Empyrean]], and after being born to her, it was said that he journeyed the planet, lacking a purpose. More than anything, his only desire is to die--gloriously. To die by the hand of someone stronger than him. His latest location seems to be away from [[Aravoth]].
