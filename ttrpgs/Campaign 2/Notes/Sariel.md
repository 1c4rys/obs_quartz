# [[Sariel]]
## Overview
**Sariel** is a mysterious [[Watcher]] whose corpse was discovered in a coffin near the **Throne of God**. He was also associated with true darkness in the mural within the capital city of [[Dûdâêl]].

He appeared relatively handsome and with brown hair, wearing the [[Cloak of Sariel]].
