# [[Uther]]
## Overview
Sovereign **Uther** was the first ruler of the reunited [[Esrith]], establishing the nation after a bloody conquest known as [[Meridiem's Second War]] through the use of vast stocks of [[Nexusborns]]. His son, [[Mordred]], succeeded him, although he had wished for his eldest son [[Arthur]] to do so instead.

He wielded [[Caliburn]], allowing him to live for hundreds of years without aging.
## Description
He was not particularly attractive, but had a certain way to him, not dissimilar to the charm of vampires in fairytales--a threatening presence that almost forces you to feel respect for the man. He had flowing red hair, showing his divine heritage, up until the last days of his life as it turned grey. While wielding [[Caliburn]], his face was just slightly wrinkled, remaining almost as athletic as he was in his youth. 

He was clean-shaven, and was missing his left eye, covered by an eye patch. 
## Personality
He was the epitome of conviction--a man with a dream that he knew he might not survive, but was fiercely devoted to it regardless. He was well-respected by all those around him, and had an affinity for the finest of [[Nidavellir]]ian ale. He was often compared to the Dawn King [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]], and although the ancient king was likely better regarded, they both had a similar spark within them, something that only a true leader can possess.  
## History
Uther was the head of the Pendragon Clan in [[Esrith#Lothian|Lothian]], and possessed a large quantity of relatively average [[Nexusborns]] due to conquest by his father. After conquering a few nearby city-states in bloody and extended battles, he came upon [[Caliburn]], which granted him the ability to live forever. With this insidious weapon by his side, he quickly gained grand swaths of land across [[Meridiem]], eventually allying with [[Valentia]] and the [[Free City of Letnya]], promising them autonomy, allowing the forces of [[Esrith]] to overwhelm the rest of the continent, excluding the south and [[Abaddon]], with pure power. 

Crowned the Sovereign, he ruled for hundreds of years, before eventually having two children: [[Arthur]] and [[Mordred]]. [[Arthur]], his eldest, was given [[Caliburn]] when he came of age, as the heir apparent. However, he disappeared at some point in his mid-20s, and has yet to be seen since. Thus, as Uther reached death, [[Mordred]] was forced to take up the mantle as Sovereign.
