# [[Empyr]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Empyr Image]]
## Overview
**Empyr** was the capital city of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], located in northeast [[Esrith]]. It is nothing but dust now, entirely destroyed during the [[Brothers' War]] thousands of years ago.
## Geography
Empyr was located in northeast [[Esrith]], upon a mountain. It was a mult-tiered structure built for defense. Now, while the central spire remains, the majority of the capital city has been entirely destroyed by the passage of time. 
## History
Sometime around 80,000 SE, Empyr was originally the capital of the original [[Esrith]], before [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]] established it as the capital of the greater [[Dawn Kingdoms]] after the [[Duel of Giants]]. For thousands of years it persisted as a center of trade and culture for the world, with a population in the millions, before at the dawn of the [[3rd Epoch]] it was destroyed during a raid from the [[Orcs]] of [[Abaddon]] during the [[Brothers' War]]. 

It has been repeatedly used as a stronghold by former [[Dawn Kingdoms]] city-states as a symbol, but to little success. In modern day, it is completely untouched by [[Esrith]], instead choosing the great city of Lothian as its capital.
