# [[Eden]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Eden Image]]
## Overview
**Eden** is a vastly unexplored continent that recently appeared upon [[Aravoth]] in 2639 TE. With its appearance, [[Edenic Mechanics#Magic|Magic]] was unleashed upon the world. It is a continent flush with unknown monsters of unimaginable strength--and weaponry just as powerful called [[Edenic Mechanics#Fruits|Fruits]].
## Etymology
The term Eden originates from the name of an ancient paradise in the text [[Genesis]] within the faith of [[Dawnism]]. 
## Geography
Eden is located east of [[Ophiri]], south of [[Nidavellir]], and northwest of [[Kisia]], taking up a great quantity of [[The Splintered Sea]], entirely isolated from the rest of the world. The seas around Eden are accompanied by a deep, dense fog that acts as a natural border to accessing inner Eden. All of those who have attempted accessing Eden through this route have not survived to tell the tale.

The geography of the continent itself is almost completely unknown, but it is written to be a beautiful land made perfect by the influence of [[Edenic Mechanics#Magic|Magic]]. 
## Inhabitants
Eden was not originally inhabited by any sentient life, but in recent years many [[Edenic Mechanics#The Redeemers|Redeemers]] have migrated to live within Eden, although due to the rarity of the [[Edenic Mechanics#Void Fruits|Void Fruits]] this population is very low. Instead, the majority of Eden is populated by horrific abominations, not dissimilar to the monstrous lifeforms that appeared post-[[Nexus]]. However, these lifeforms are both larger in number and strength than the other [[Abominations|monsters]] in the rest of the world, primarily due to the influence of Eden's inherent [[Edenic Mechanics#Magic|Magic]]. It is theorized by many of the top researchers of the world that the monsters within Eden are the original forms of these monsters. 
## Edenic Mechanics
> *Main article: [[Edenic Mechanics]]*

The only way to enter Eden is by consuming one of the [[Edenic Mechanics#Magic|Magic]], a form of the [[Edenic Mechanics#Fruits|Fruits]] that rarely appeared in the rest of the world after the appearance of Eden, wherein upon their death, their soul would be transferred to Eden and they would become one of the [[Edenic Mechanics#The Redeemers|Redeemers]]. In the process, they gain the ability to wield [[Edenic Mechanics#Magic|Magic]] along with an [[Edenic Mechanics#Affinity|Affinity]], but are also beset with a horrible and life-altering [[Edenic Mechanics#Penance|Penance]].

Within Eden, there are a vast quantity of [[Edenic Mechanics#Fruits|Fruits]], ancient relics akin to the [[Nexusborns]] but created directly from [[Edenic Mechanics#Magic|Magic]]. These [[Edenic Mechanics#Fruits|Fruits]] have varying use cases and powers, but they are the primary defense mechanism against the [[Abominations]]. The [[Edenic Mechanics#Fruits|Fruits]] are only useable by those who have consumed one of the [[Edenic Mechanics#Void Fruits|Void Fruits]].

The ultimate goal of the [[Edenic Mechanics#The Redeemers|Redeemers]] is to reach the Throne of God at the center of Eden and consume the [[Edenic Mechanics#Fruit of  Infinity|Fruit of Infinity]] from the Tree of Life.
