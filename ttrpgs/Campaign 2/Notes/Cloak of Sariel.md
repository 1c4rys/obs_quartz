# [[Cloak of Sariel]]:
## Overview
The **Cloak of Sariel** is a Fruit discovered within [[Dûdâêl]] named after [[Sariel]]. The armor appears as a formless and long cloak, shifting to the user’s body shape. It hides all parts of their body covered by the cloak under a deep shadow. 
## Info
### Archetype
**Defensive**: Leather Armor
### Level
6
## Description
*Within the confines of Azazel’s castle, Sariel came unto his brother Azazel, saying: "Behold, the Elf draweth nigh to take thy head for Her." And Azazel sighed and lifted up his eyes, replying: "I am prepared. But surely I shall perish if She hath bestowed her power upon him. Therefore, I beseech thee to grant me a favor."*

*Sariel marveled, yet though he himself was acquainted with wickedness, his brother had shown kindness unto him. Thus Sariel's heart was not so hardened as to deny a dying man's petition. And he said: "What wouldst thou ask of me, Azazel?"*

*Azazel's countenance brightened as he spoke, saying: "If I am slain, I have devised a stratagem. For my life is bound up with powers beyond even our ken – I shall not truly Die. All I require of thee is but a small portion of thy might – even an infinitesimal share. For only true darkness can ensure my survival."*

*Then did Sariel laugh with great wickedness, saying: "True darkness, sayest thou? I fear thou shalt not emerge as the same being. But lo, I shall grant thee this boon, my brother. However, if thy desire is that this power preserve thee forevermore, I urge thee to seek nearness to the cosmos – for naught nourisheth the darkness more than the deep expanse above. May fortune smile upon thee!"*

*And Sariel's visage was lit with a fell grin as he bestowed a measure of his power unto his brother. "Farewell, Azazel. May we meet again in Death." Then Sariel vanished from sight, returning unto the Netherworld.*
## Intrinsic
### Touch of Sariel
And lo, true darkness. The wielder of the Cloak of Sariel wields a small portion of his power. You learn the *shadow jump* focus spell. If you don't already have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point, which you can Refocus by meditating within an area of dim light or darkness. 

*Shadow jump* is an occult spell. You become trained in occult spell attack rolls and spell DCs, and your spellcasting ability for these spells is Charisma.

After casting the spell, if your next action before the end of your turn is to Strike or attempt a Stealth check, you gain a +2 status bonus to that roll.
