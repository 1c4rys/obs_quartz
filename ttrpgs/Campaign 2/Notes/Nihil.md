# [[Nihil]]
## Overview
**Nihil** is viewed as the ancient enemy of [[The Empyrean]] and the inventor of Death in [[Dawnism]]. The leader of the [[Dawnism#The Forsaken|Forsaken]], he is thought to have originated from the void. 

%%Nihil is, in fact, the eternal nemesis of the God-Moon [[Aeternum]], representing nothingness. He was unleashed when the [[Precursors]] attempted to travel backwards in time. Nihil has complete power over Time and Nothingness, with its ultimate purpose being the ultimate cessation of the universe, into a still statue of its former self.%%
## History
He is said to have tricked the early [[Aravothil]] into allowing him to enter the physical realm, wherein he engaged in battle with [[The Empyrean]]. Reaching a standstill, he convinced her to invent Death, and in return she was able to gain the upper hand. With this, she used her form to trap him and his [[Dawnism#The Forsaken|Forsaken]] deep within [[Perdition]], sacrificing herself in the process. It is believed that this colossal battle resulted in the rapid spread of the continents across the globe.
