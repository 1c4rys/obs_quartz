# [[Silvanus]]
## Overview
**Silvanus** was one of the [[Archon]]s in the faith of [[Dawnism]], the son of [[The Empyrean]], and the [[Archon]] of Nature. His symbol was the forest, representing the natural life of [[Aravoth]].

He is considered the patron god of the [[Elves]], and many believe he at one point lived among them. His whereabouts are unknown as of late. In [[Languages#Elder Speech|Elder Speech]], his name is translated as **Ardhon**. 
## Description
He is portrayed through many different lenses, but it is believed he is somewhat similar to [[Elves]] in physiology, with platinum blonde hair, shoulder length hair. Beyond that, all that is known is that he is written to possess the ability to fly, primarily through large, insect-like wings on his back. He is written to be as light as a feather and as deadly as the strongest of blades. The only of the [[Archon]]s said to be able to challenge him in a duel is [[Neton (Archon)|Neton]], the [[Archon]] of War. 
## Personality
He is thought to be stoic and unwavering, not unlike a large oak tree. He did not have a mind for humor nor wit, instead preferring to spend the majority of his life away from others, deep in the forests. Because of this, it was common that he was viewed negatively by other [[Elves]], but this was just because he cared little for the needs of the [[Aravothil]]. He was obsessed with the preservation of nature. 
## History
Silvanus is one of the best recorded deities and the most believed to exist in some form or another because it has been written that at one point in time, he lived within [[The Silver Havens]] amongst the [[Elves]]. He was thought to have ruled as some form of authority figure, but he did not have any desire to rule the [[Elves]]. The [[Elves]] would profess their undying adoration for him, but all he cared about was how nature was cared for in [[The Silver Havens]]. Thus, the [[Elves]] adopted a culture of conservation. 

In modern day, some still say he lives within [[The Silver Havens]], but it is thought that these people are incorrect. He is thought to have moved on from the homeland of the [[Elves]], living where he always wanted to: in solitude.
