# [[Dawnism]]
## Overview
**Dawnism** is a polytheistic [[Religions|religion]] practiced in [[Meridiem]] and found all across [[Aravoth]] that comprises the collective spiritual, cultural, and legal traditions of several ethnic groups and states, collectively known as the [[Dawn Kingdoms]]. 

Following the primordial deity [[The Empyrean]], Dawnic followers wished to lead a life that proved them worthy of defending [[Aravoth]] from the warriors of the ancient enemy [[Nihil]], the Forsaken. It was first established by the prophet [[Cassiel]], a man considered to be interchangeable with the Archon [[Denarion (Archon)|Denarion]]. 
## Etymology
Dawnism is named after the ultimate desire of the religion--to allow for [[The Empyrean]] to rise again.
## Beliefs
### The Empyrean
> *Main article: [[The Empyrean]]*

In the Dawnic religion, people believe in [[The Empyrean]], the force of good and creator of the [[Aravothil]] and the [[Archon]]s, along with animals and plantlife and [[Aravoth]] itself. All things are said to exist because of her, and her existence is held to be self-evident.

[[The Empyrean]] is thought to be uncreated, all-powerful, omniscient and all-seeing, immortal, omnibenevolent, and all-loving. Adherents of Dawnism believe that [[The Empyrean]] is also responsible for sorting out the dead and deciding on their afterlife. To achieve the results one desires, one has to decide upon which of the seven [[Archon]]s' armies they wish to enlist in, by picking a Domain of the [[Archon]]s and following it to the best of their abilities.

Authority is believed to be rooted in connection and closeness to [[The Empyrean]] and her [[Archon]]s, so for example, red-haired individuals claim that they are closer to [[Denarion (Archon)|Denarion]] than the rest, and thus are holier than them and have the authority to rule over them. Kings are also thought to have their authority rooted in a divine mandate from [[The Empyrean]].
### The Archons
> *Main article: [[Archon]]*

The **Archons** are believed to be forces of good who have protected [[Aravoth]] from forces of evil, and help to teach and protect the world against evildoing. 

Each Archon is an emissary of [[The Empyrean]], and possesses her abilities to a limited extent. Typically, [[Alaric]] is placed as the highest among the [[Archon]], with the others beneath him. 
### Forsaken
The **Forsaken** are viewed as the polar opposite of the [[Aravothil]] and the forces of the [[The Empyrean]], forged in the distant future after the world was destroyed. They are claimed to live within [[Perdition]] with their master, as the [[Archon]]s ensure that they are never unleashed. The incursions of the Forsaken upon [[Aravoth]] are known as the Floods.
### Death
**Death** is believed to have been invented by [[The Empyrean]] after she was forced to by [[Nihil]] after he appeared in the physical realm. It is thought that those that lead a fruitful life and prove themselves worthy will "trick" [[Nihil]] by joining the side of the [[Archon]]s in battle against his Forsaken. 

Dawnism's Death is not considered entirely congruous with true death. Dawnism's Death is better equated with Corruption.
### Floods
The incursions of the Forsaken upon [[Aravoth]], they are deeply feared by the followers of [[Dawnism]]. When they die and reach the netherworld, followers of [[Dawnism]] believe that they will fight the Forsaken for eternity, and if they fail or there aren't enough warriors, the Floods will occur. 
## Religious Texts
Dawnism has a wide variety of sacred texts used to guide their interpretation of [[The Empyrean]], chief of which was a text written by [[Cassiel]] known as the [[Genesis]], in the native [[Old Dawnic]] and translated to New Dawnic, which is the only piece of text well-regarded as originating from an [[Archon]]. However, much of the original text is lost, with only bits and pieces remaining to inform the people how to follow [[Dawnism]]. 
## History
### Primeval History
Dawnic followers believe that millions of years ago, [[The Empyrean]] created the physical realm from nothing, and she birthed children, the [[Archon]]s, to create the celestial bodies, establishing a world of peace and harmony. This perfect existence was disrupted when [[Nihil]], a malevolent force of darkness corrupted the [[Aravothil]] into allowing for him to escape. [[The Empyrean]] bargained with [[Nihil]], creating Death to sate his sociopathic urges, but his avarice knew no bounds. Both life and death cannot exist at once, so [[The Empyrean]] sacrificed herself to imprison [[Nihil]] within [[Perdition]]. As she died, her [[Archon]]s protected the world in their mother's stead. It is believed that this colossal battle resulted in the rapid spread of the continents across the globe.
### 2nd Epoch and Beyond
One of these children, [[Cassiel]], was said to have travelled to the continent of [[Meridiem]], wherein he spread the word of [[The Empyrean]]'s sacrifice and began the religion of Dawnism. Many believe he was in fact the [[Archon]] of Wealth, [[Denarion (Archon)|Denarion]]. The religion spread rapidly, and through its control, a man named [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]], no relation to the [[Archon]], was able to quickly rise in power and establish himself as the king of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], a vast collective of nations upon [[Meridiem]]. After the [[Brothers' War]] and his eventual death, Dawnism was undeterred, and continues to be popular to this day.
