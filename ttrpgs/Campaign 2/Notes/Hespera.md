# [[Hespera]]
## Overview
**Hespera** was one of the [[Archon]]s in the faith of [[Dawnism]], the daughter of [[The Empyrean]], and the [[Archon]] of Fertility. Her symbol was a flower, representing the cultivation of beauty upon [[Aravoth]]. 
## Description
She was portrayed as young woman with beautiful, light-red hair, more natural looking than that of her brother [[Denarion (Archon)|Denarion]]'s. She was known for having a large, flowery laurel upon her head, along with full, red lips and fair skin. She is believed to be the most beautiful woman in the world as described in [[Genesis]].
## Personality
Hespera was described as extremely vain, witty, self-absorbed, cunning, jealous, self-centered but also very passionate, intelligent, and romantic. She likes to be recognized as the 'most beautiful being in the entire world'.
## History
Little is known of her life nor where she currently resides, but many believe she lives within [[Valentia]]. Over the years, many have claimed that their children are the sons and daughters of Hespera, but none have been proven. One thing all claimed children possessed was vibrant, beautiful red hair, just like their supposed mother.
