# [[Dawn Kingdoms|Dawn Kingdoms]]
> [!infobox]
> ![[Dawn Kingdoms Image]]
## Overview
The **Dawn Kingdoms** were an ancient, monarchal, confederate, republican empire in [[Meridiem]], whose influence was felt across [[Aravoth]] for thousands of years. The people of the Dawn Kingdoms were great in number and even greater in culture, composed of nearly every [[Aravothil]] race. During its glory years before the [[Brothers' War]], the Dawn Kingdoms would exist in near perfect tranquility. 

The empire was the pinnacle of human civilization pre-[[3rd Epoch]]. Despite being founded by a humble soldier, the Dawn Kingdoms became the mightiest nation upon the globe due to their control over the [[Nexusborns]], coming to harness the powers given by the [[Nexusborns]] in ways that would change [[Aravoth]] for generations. Existing in peace and prosperity, they were only destroyed after the [[Orcs]] of [[Abaddon]] rebelled and their king was assassinated, causing the empire to collapse into a number of city-states and loosely collected countries.
## Government
The people of the Dawn Kingdoms were governed by one Dawn King for its entire existence--[[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]]. Without him, the empire collapsed. Directly beneath him were the leaders of each **Province**, usually following the line of kings of the original nations which became the Provinces, known as a **Governor**. 

Beyond the Dawn King, the body most responsible for the day-to-day operations of the empire was the **Olympic Council**, a democratically-elected position that voted on the direction that the empire would move towards. The Olympic Council was composed of 13 members, each of which served 6 year terms and were unable to be reelected. The Dawn King was the Chairman, who possessed 3 votes, while the **Assistant Chairman**, who was elected by the council biannually, possessed 2 votes. The Olympic Council convened twice every week.

## Society
The Dawn Kingdoms was particularly known for their lack of wealth inequality. The difference between the median 1% and median 99% was closer than any modern nation, despite retaining their capitalist systems. Slavery was illegal in the Dawn Kingdoms, but indentured servitude was not. 

The main form of social stratification within Dawnic society was through the **Clans**, families founded by wielders of the [[Nexusborns]]. The Dawn King allowed these Clans to exist and gave them special benefits because of the value that one of the [[Nexusborns]] provides in battle. Because of this, the Governors in society were usually members of the greater Clans. Many of these Clans continue to exist to this day. 
## Religion
> *Main Article: [[Dawnism]]*

The main religion of the Dawn Kingdoms was **Dawnism**, a vastly important religion that predated even the Dawn Kingdoms. It was first established by the prophet [[Cassiel]], a man considered to be interchangeable with the Archon [[Denarion (Archon)|Denarion]]. At the head of the religion was [[The Empyrean]]. 
## Survivor States
Following the collapse of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], two of the Provinces were saved from the conflict and remained for centuries, one became a vastly powerful city-state, and one was refounded centuries later:
### Cyndra
> *Main article: [[Cyndra]]*

**Cyndra**, the lands in the northwestern reaches of the **Sheol Mountains**, continued to persist and carried on, until they were conquered by the [[Drachma]] Empire recently.
### Valentia
> *Main article: [[Valentia]]*

**Valentia**, formerly Verden, was located in northern [[Meridiem]], their purple sails the namesake for the Indigo Strait. They continued to be culturally diverse and possessed many of the greatest universities in the world. 
### Free City of Letnya 
> *Main article: [[Free City of Letnya]]*

**Free City of Letnya** is a city wherein the former great Clan of Telran now persists. It is one of the largest independent cities in the world, primarily due to their proximity to the [[Isles of the Blessed]] and their incredibly strong navy. 
### Esrith
> *Main article: [[Esrith]]*

**Esrith** was the second largest of the original Provinces, and the nation wherein the Dawn King originated from. It was recently refounded, resorbing the former countries of Rhiscar and Aendor into itself, and adopting a similar governmental model to the original Dawn Kingdoms, viewed by many as the most direct successor, although the existing competition throughout the world makes their existence less prosperous. 
## History
Due to the lack of information available after the thousands of years have passed since this time, there is little information about the faults of the Dawn Kingdoms, but it is certain that they existed. No empire could be perfectly prosperous forever, so it is important to recognize that much of the information known has been censored by the passage of time. 
### Founding
Upon [[Meridiem]], a paradise of rivers, forests, lakes, and plains, the Dawn Kingdoms were first established. The Dawn Kingdoms were founded by the **Nephilim** [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]] in the early [[2nd Epoch]], estimated to be around 4000 SE. They were founded after the aforementioned Nephilim became the ruler of [[Esrith]], later warring the many other nation-states of [[Meridiem]] and forcing them to bend the knee. He promised a society where all were equal, and where each nation was mostly autonomous, allowing for each nation to complete their goals and prosper without meddling from other nations. 

[[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]] used the [[Nexusborns]], of which he mysteriously possessed nearly half of all in existence, to reward those who agreed to ally with him against his main threat, [[Abaddon]]. Telran, [[Cyndra]], and [[Valentia|Verden]] bent the knee, while after faced by military threat, Rhiscar and Aendor soon followed. His attempts to unite [[Meridiem]] culminated in the [[Duel of Giants]], a great battle between the [[Orcs]] of [[Abaddon]] and the rest of [[Meridiem]], wherein after nearly 10 years of fighting, the Dawn King won--establishing the Dawn Kingdoms, once and for all.
### Golden Age
The Dawn Kingdoms prospered. Wealth inequality was lower than ever in history, and although minor skirmishes were present, especially between unruly [[Orcs]], they were swiftly eliminated. The Olympic Council was swiftly established during this era by the Dawn King in order to keep his power in check. The Olympic Council was purely democratic and, due to their frequency of meeting, were able to ensure general peace. 

The [[Nexusborns]] grew to be important items in determining who would rise to power, as over the years nearly two-thirds of the quantity originally possessed by the Dawn King were dispersed across the Governors and their Provinces. However, this lead to great social stratification, leading the wide majority of those in power to be wielders of [[Nexusborns]] and therefore members of the greater Clans. Despite this, it was not infrequent for [[Nexusborns]] to be passed to members not within the Clan, allowing for some form of social mobility. In addition, many of the members of the Olympic Council were not part of Clans. Being in a Clan was a benefit, but not a necessity for rule.

Culturally, too, the Dawn Kingdoms were vastly important. In Verden, the first universities were established, while the freedom at the time allowed for the creation of many unique fighting styles and forms of artistic expression. Much of the most famous art throughout history originated from the Dawn Kingdoms, having stood the test of time. The Golden Age was also a period of great diversity, as nearly every race journeyed to the Dawn Kingdoms for economic prosperity. 

Great strides were made in the form of technology too, with a particularly powerful one of the [[Nexusborns]] allowing for the invention of the study of [[Nexomancy]], a form of engineering that allowed in the creation of [[Nexomancy#Automaton|Automaton]]s, created sentient due to some strange quality originating from the [[Nexus]]. 
### Collapse
The halcyon days of the Dawn Kingdoms had long passed by the time [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]] came to rule in [[Abaddon]]. He was more radical than any other ruler in [[Abaddon]], born from a supernatural change in the temperance of the [[Orcs]] within the motherland. Fiercer and more irritatable than ever, the [[Orcs]] made him their leader. [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]] first acted as any other Governor would, but that would change when Many [[Orcs]] journeyed past [[Abaddon]] to rape villages for monetary gain. [[The Wall]] was built as a preventative measure. [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]] feigned ignorance, and [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]] made the biggest mistake he would ever make--trusting him. 

On his way back from diplomatic missions in the [[Isles of the Blessed]], the Dawn King witnessed first hand the advancement of the [[Orcs]]. He made it back to the capital city, [[Empyr]], before he could be killed--but it was too late. In an event later known as the [[Brothers' War]], [[Orcs]] sieged the city, and with no warning signs, [[Empyr]] was lost--and the immortal ruler of the Dawn Kingdoms, [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]], was slain. The entirety of [[Meridiem]] would be controlled under the [[Orcs]], but [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]] mysteriously disappeared, leaving the [[Orcs]] leaderless. A strike force was established of the strongest [[Nexusborns]] across the realm, and the [[Orcs]] were swiftly pushed back, all but a handful of their [[Nexusborns]] stolen. 

Without the Dawn King's steadfast leadership, members of the Olympic Council battled over who would become the new king. Their cold war turned hot, as thousands died across [[Meridiem]]. Finally, the Dawn Kingdoms were fractured once and for all, each Province becoming their own nation. Over the years, most have fallen into a small number of city-states, some resorbed into other nations, and few remain. The Dawn Kingdoms were destroyed. 
### Legacy
#### Nexomancy
The many robotic lifeforms created by the usage of [[Nexomancy]] continue to persist to modern day. In [[Meridiem]], it is simply a fact of life that colossal robotic lifeforms continue to roam the plains, seemingly without purpose. Life-sized [[Nexomancy#Automaton|Automaton]]s, despite being low in number, are ingrained in society across the world. [[Nexomancy]] has yet to be recreated by anyone in the time since, the [[Nexusborns|Nexuborn]] that enabled it likely lost to history. 
#### Esrith
> *Main Article: [[Esrith]]*

The nation of [[Esrith]] was established around the year 2100 TE, modeled off of the ancient Dawn Kingdoms in both style of government and culture. Despite not being anywhere near as prosperous as their ancient ancestors, [[Esrith]] is one of the most powerful nations in the world.
