# [[Permanence]]
## Overview
The [[Splinter]] of **Permanence** was one of the seven [[Splinter]]s gifted to the denizens of [[Aravoth]]  by [[Aeternum]], each of which are pieces of her flesh. 

The [[Splinter]] of Permanence was given to [[Raphael]], a member of the [[Precursors]] who would later become the basis for the race of the [[Elves]]. This Splinter blesses those born within its radius with the powers of Permanence, hence why the [[Elves|Elven]] race was formed. Currently, the [[Splinter]] of Permanence is held by [[Aule]], after he unwittingly made a deal with [[Lilith]] to steal it from [[Raphael]], resulting in the collapse of [[The Silver Havens]].
