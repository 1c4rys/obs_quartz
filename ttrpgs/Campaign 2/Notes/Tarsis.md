# [[Tarsis]]
## Overview
**Tarsis** is a desert region located in western [[Ophiri]] that is steeped in history and cultural diversity. The religion of [[Elohim]] was founded here and is followed by the majority of the population. Never once united under one common leader, Tarsis is a divided region composed of dozens of city-states with varying levels of power. 

Recently, the nation of [[Drachma]] has attempted incursions into Tarsis for the purpose of conquering them, and have succeeded in some parts, causing Tarsis to become embroiled in war. 
## Geography
Tarsis is absolutely colossal in size, easily the largest desert in the world. The majority of the land is composed of rolling dunes of sand, although there are many large, arid mountains. In general, there is very little water in Tarsis, leading the water trade to be incredibly lucrative. It is written that a wielder of a [[Nexusborns|Nexusborn]] that created infinite water was the richest man the world has ever seen. There are many oases, however, full of fresh water, although many of them have dried up. 

The north is far colder than the southern and central regions, but still a desert in many regards. 

There are two large bodies of water, one a sea and the other an ocean, near Tarsis. 
- **White Sea**: Named after the high salt content, the White Sea is in southwestern Tarsis near many of the urban settlements. Its outlet is the Tarsish Ocean.
- **Tarsish Ocean**: The Tarshish Ocean is one of the most sailed oceans in the world due to trade from [[Valentia]] to the south. It is the smallest ocean in the world and is located west of Tarsis. 
## Inhabitants
The people to call Tarsis home are a variety of smaller ethnic groups, chief of which are the [[People#Northern Tarshish|Northern]] and [[People#Southern Tarshish|Southern]] Tarsish, of which the [[People#Northern Tarshish|Northern]] Tarsish are far larger in population. Most of the former live in many of the larger urban settlements in central Tarsis, while the latter reside in the mountain ranges to the south most directly bordering [[Drachma]]. In more recent years, both peoples have become more and more mixed, as the majority consider themselves to be part of one wider ethnicity, the Tarsish. 

In the northwest, there is some of the largest concentrations of [[Abominations]] in the world. Thus, the area is not inhabited by many people. 

The wide majority of Tarsish are [[Humans]], although it is not uncommon to find the occasional [[Dwarves|Dwarf]] or, more likely, [[Halflings|Halfling]]. In some of the southern reaches where slavery is still common practice, [[Orcs]] are large in population. Because of this large slave population, there are many free men who are [[People#Cambion|Cambion]]s. 
## Life and Society
Tarsish society is organized around its city-states, which serve as hubs of commerce, culture, and religious activity. The harsh desert environment has shaped their daily lives, with water conservation and management being crucial skills. The economy is diverse, with trade in spices and water being particularly important. Many Tarsish engage in mercenary work, contributing to a strong martial tradition that has helped them resist numerous invasion attempts throughout history.

[[Elohim]]'s religious practices permeate all aspects of life. Religious leaders and scholars hold significant influence in Tarsish communities. The veneration of [[Aravoth]] as a living deity manifests in daily rituals and a deep respect for the land, despite the strain on natural resources due to population growth.

Tarsish culture boasts a rich artistic tradition, including intricate desert-adapted architecture, poetry, and craftsmanship. Music and storytelling play vital roles in preserving history and cultural identity.

The society has sophisticated irrigation systems and water conservation techniques. These technological adaptations are crucial for survival and prosperity in the arid climate. In the southern regions where slavery is practiced, there's a complex social hierarchy involving free Tarsish, enslaved [[Orcs]], and [[People#Cambion|Cambion]].
## History
Life first originated in Tarsis from life in [[Meridiem]] migrating northwards, and due to proximity to the equator, the Tarsish are darker of skin. The religion of [[Elohim]] originated here, quickly taking hold. It is believed that [[Elohim]] is one of the oldest religions in the world, and most people still speak in the language the first translation of [[Genesis]] was written in--[[Languages#Tarsish|Tarsish]]. 

In [[Elohim]], the being of worship is actually [[Aravoth]] itself, which has lead to a deep respect in the land by many of the older generations of Tarsis. However, the burgeoning population has lead much of the sacred oases to be expended, and the White Sea shrinks by the day. Around the time that [[Elohim]] first took hold in Tarsis, the first true city-states were established. Many of these cities have existed for tens of thousands of years, standing the test of time. 

Due to the large economy in Tarsis primarily because of the large population and access to rare spices along with wide mercenary forces, many foreign forces have at one point or another attempted invasion. Sometime near the later parts of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] existence, there are reports that they attempted incursions into Tarsis, but were unable to gain a foothold.

[[Drachma]], too, has attempted to absorb Tarsis into their empire for centuries, since the early [[3rd Epoch]] before they were even united, although up until recently they have failed. After [[Drach VII]] came into power, however, his military experience proved incredibly helpful for conquering parts of Tarsis. Now, many of the more barren regions of eastern Tarsis are under Drachma's control, along with a few larger settlements in the further west. This has led to widespread fear across the Tarsish, as they pray to [[Aravoth]] for a miracle.
