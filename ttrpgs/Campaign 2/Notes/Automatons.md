# [[Automatons]]
## Overview
The main creations of [[Nexomancy]] were the **Automatons**, a race of robots not dissimilar to the rest of the [[Aravothil]]. They are relatively similar to their original race in terms of shape, due to CAD. Some [[Abominations|Abominable]] Automatons were produced, in vast [[Orichalcum]] structures meant to appear similar to their original form, too. Around 80,000 were ever produced, and less than a tenth of them remain. When a given soul is transferred to an Automaton form, all memory is lost from the original form.

[[Turing]] is an Automaton. 
## Description
Automatons are constructs made of [[Orichalcum]], designed for durability in combat and the inability to rust. They typically have humanoid shapes. Each contains an _Automaton Core_, a powerful artifact housing its soul and energy source.

While Automatons don't need to eat or sleep, they require breathable air to vent magical exhaust and a period of recalibration for their cores. They don't age and can potentially live for thousands of years.

An Automaton's body can be destroyed, but its core is more resilient. If the core malfunctions or is destroyed, the soul is released. Some Automatons learn to voluntarily release their souls, leaving behind a mindless construct. This design aimed for functional immortality, but in practice, magical intervention is often needed to fully restore a damaged Automaton.
## Personality
A given Automaton has their own personality, shaped by countless experiences. Most Automatons behave reclusively, preferring to avoid others due to fear of attachment or misunderstanding. Even Automatons who are more willing to live in the open understand that their unique nature makes them a prime target for hunters, scholars, or worse. 

The passage of time has revealed one of the Automatons' greatest weaknesses: their mortal psyches. Only the strongest willed have managed to retain their memories, sense of self, and lucidity after all this time. 
## Society
Automatons are forced to live solitary, with few Automatons ever seeing another one of their own in a hundred years. Automatons more commonly interact with other races, eliciting curiosity rather than fear. While their unique appearance makes them stand out, they can find a semblance of normal life in large cities. However, automatons must always be wary of those who might seek to study or exploit their bodies or cores.
## Cognitive Automaton Dissonance (CAD)
**Cognitive Automaton Dissonane**, known as CAD, is a fatal cognitive disorder caused by the soul rejecting its new body, resulting in a painful and horrifying death. CAD is why Automaton bodies are created to be as similar to their original form as possible, to avoid this eventuality. This is why many Automaton choose to eat and drink, despite gaining no benefit from it. They also therefore possess genitals and a robotic form of every other normal body part necessary to ensure the mind survives. 
## History
All Automatons were produced during the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] era of history, and none have been produced since. They were primarily produced for war and servitude, taking the role of soldiers and butlers alike. The majority of Automatons have lost their memory over the years, with many turning into barely sentient creatures due to damage from living for so long.
