# [[Nexomancy]]
## Overview
**Nexomancy** is a type of engineering that allowed for the creation of Automatons--sentient robotic life forms built of [[Orichalcum]]. It utilized one of the [[Nexusborns]] to siphon the soul of an [[Aravothil]] into a robotic carapace, usually sentient ones.

All were produced during the era of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]]. 
## Mechanics
There is not much remaining information on how to actually create Automatons with Nexomany, so all known information is not well understood. Firstly, it is known that Nexomancy was only possibly during the [[Arc]], because the conditions were similar to the [[Nexus]]. It also required a specific type of [[Nexusborns|Nexusborn]] that was able to replicate the [[Nexus]] on a smaller scale, redirecting souls to a robotic hull composed of [[Orichalcum]]. 
## Automatons
> *Main article: [[Automatons]]*

The main creations of Nexomancy were the **Automatons**, a race of robots not dissimilar to the rest of the [[Aravothil]]. They are relatively similar to their original race in terms of shape, due to CAD. Some [[Abominations|Abominable]] Automatons were produced, in vast [[Orichalcum]] structures meant to appear similar to their original form, too. Around 80,000 were ever produced, and less than a tenth of them remain. 

When a given soul is transferred to an Automaton form, all memory is lost from the original form.
