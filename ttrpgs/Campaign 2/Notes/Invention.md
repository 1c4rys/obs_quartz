# [[Invention]]
## Overview
The [[Splinter]] of **Invention** was one of the seven [[Splinter]]s gifted to the denizens of [[Aravoth]]  by [[Aeternum]], each of which are pieces of her flesh. 

The [[Splinter]] of Invention was given to [[Hasdiel]], a member of the [[Precursors]] who would later become the basis for the race of the [[Dwarves]]. This [[Splinter]] blesses those born within its radius with the powers of Invention, hence why the Dwarven race was formed. Currently, the Splinter of Invention is held by [[Hasdiel]] within the northern nation of [[Nidavellir]].
