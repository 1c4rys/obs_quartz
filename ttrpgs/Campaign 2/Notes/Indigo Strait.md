# [[Indigo Strait]]
## Overview
The **Indigo Strait** was a strait located between the continents of [[Meridiem]] and [[Ophiri]]. It was a place vibrant with trade, with traders from [[Valentia]] travelling to [[Tarsis]] and vice versa, along with to greater [[Ophiri]]. 

Its namesake is the purple sails of the ships of [[Valentia]], which one hears of as far away as [[Kisia]] and [[The Silver Havens]].
