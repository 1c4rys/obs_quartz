# [[Old Dawnic]]
## Overview
**Old Dawnic** was a [[Languages|language]] spoken in the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] both before and after the [[Brothers' War]] and the empire's collapse. It has been completely lost, with all remaining inscriptions seemingly appearing as mindless jumbles of information
## Speakers
Old Dawnic was the primary language of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], excluding [[Abaddon]]. Now, it is said to be a holy language only spoken by the [[Archon]]s--but [[Aule]] is, somehow, fluent.
## History and Legacy
Old Dawnic is one of the oldest known languages, dating back thousands of years ago to nearly the beginning of the [[2nd Epoch]]. It is theorized that Old Dawnic is the precursor language of both New Dawnic and Valentine along with numerous others, but the differences in script casts some doubt upon this theory.
