# [[Shepherds|Shepherds]]
>[!infobox]
>![[Shepherds Image]]

## Overview
The Shepherds are two of three historical moons of [[Aravoth]]. They rotate opposite one another, and are are nearly identical in size, although [[Neton (Archon)]]/[[Manekugi]] is larger than [[Rhiannon]]/[[Manekumi]]. [[Neton (Archon)]] is 1511 miles in diameter, and [[Rhiannon]] is 1295 miles in diameter. The two moons causes the [[Arc]], as they pull the rings of [[Aravoth]] in a way that creates it. 
## Etymology
In the [[Kyushimago religion]], the two moons are named Manekugi and Manekumi respectively. In the mythology, Manekugi and Manekumi are lovers, and caused the creation of the world from nothing, both becoming moons to look down upon their creation. Manekugi translates to "he who beckons", and Manekumi translates to "she who beckons", signifying how they shepherd the rings of Aravoth to create the Arc.

In [[Dawnism]], the two moons are named after Neton and Rhiannon, the Archon of War and Archon of Trickery respectively. In Dawnic mythology, Neton and Rhiannon are twins, Neton is viewed as masculine, while Rhiannon is considered as androgynous. 

Some Valentine religious scholars believe that the characters of Neton, Rhiannon, Manekugi, and Manekumi are inspired from the same two historical figures, although their identities are hitherto unknown.
