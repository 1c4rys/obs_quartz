# [[Arthur]]
## Overview
**Arthur** is the son of the former Sovereign of [[Esrith]], [[Uther]]. His father gave him [[Caliburn]], because it was expected he would become the Sovereign, but Arthur disappeared sometime afterwards. Therefore, his brother, [[Mordred]], became the heir to the throne. 

[[Tyrmorn]] met Arthur during his journeys with his first cohort in [[Toril]], at the time taking the name of "**The Pathfinder's Blade**". 

Within the temple surrounding [[Toril]]'s Gate, Arthur inscribed, 'To whomever reads this note. I made it here and I will guide this passage all the way to the farthest ends of the universe. Nothing will stop me. I returned to where I'm from. Find me in Eden.' 
## Description
He was described as gallant and confident, and a fierce fighter. He has dark red hair just like his father, along with fair skin.
