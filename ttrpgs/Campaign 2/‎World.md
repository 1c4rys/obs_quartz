# The World of Aravoth
## World
### Character Creation
#### [[Races|Ancestries]]
> *Portal: [[Races]]*

There are six races, seven if the [[Nexomancy#Automatons|Automatons]] are included. The races are:
- [[Dwarves]]: a short, stocky people who are often stubborn, fierce, and devoted.
- [[Elves]]: a tall, long-lived people with a strong tradition of art and magic.
- [[Halflings]]: a short, tranquil people who exhibit remarkable curiosity and humor. 
- [[Humans]]: a diverse and adaptable people with wide potential and deep ambitions. 
- [[Kitsune]]: a charismatic and witty people with a connection to the spiritual that grants them many magical abilities, chiefly the power to shapechange into other forms
- [[Orcs]]: a proud, strong people with hardened physiques who value physical might and glory in combat.
- [[Nexomancy#Automatons|Automatons]]: man-made, immortal machines built through [[Nexomancy]] composed of the souls of sentients... or [[Abominations]].

### World
#### [[Aravoth]]
**Aravoth** is the setting for the campaign. It is a relatively normal world by most standards, except for the strange continent of [[Eden]].

Aravoth is the third planet from the Sun in the Aravothil System, the only known habitable world in the system. Aravoth is orbited by two moons, the [[Shepherds]], and was once orbited by an ancient moon known as [[Aeternum]]. In addition, a large quantity of rings orbit Aravoth, colloquially referred to as the Grandchildren. Every Winter Solstice, the Shepherds pass one another, shepherding the Grandchildren to create a beautiful celestial event known as the [[Arc]].  

There is limited magic in [[Aravoth]], only useable through the use of weird magical weapons called the [[Nexusborns]]. That is, until [[Eden]] was rediscovered.
##### [[Eden]]
**Eden** is a continent of magic that existed thousands of years ago that was just now rediscovered during an alignment of the [[Shepherds]]. Through its mechanics, the PF2e character creation system is rationalized in the world. I suggest you read [[Edenic Mechanics]] if you want to learn more. 
#### Continents
- [[Meridiem]]: A colossal continent to the southwest, a diverse landscape inhabited by members of every race and nearly every culture. 
- [[Ophiri]]: The largest continent, it is composed of almost entirely desert except for the mountainous east which is covered in tundra, mostly composed of Humans and some Halflings, along with Dwarves from [[Nidavellir]].
- [[The Silver Havens]]: The smallest and and northernmost continent, it is entirely composed of Elves.
- [[Nidavellir]]: A collection of isles in the deep north, and the location wherein the wide majority of Dwarves reside. Heavily mountainous. 
- [[Kisia]]: Referred to as Kyushima by its inhabitants, it is an archipelago to the southeast, and deeply isolationist. Little is known beyond that.
- [[Tarsis]]: A desert region in western [[Ophiri]] composed of dark-skinned Humans. Deeply arid, but just as deep in history.
- [[Abaddon]]: A volcanic and deadly location in western [[Meridiem]], where the Orcs reside. It is now separated by [[Meridiem]]-proper by [[The Wall]]. 
- [[Isles of the Blessed]]: Its name deeply ironic, the [[Isles of the Blessed]] are a string of islands south of [[Meridiem]] that is home to a large quantity of pirates, hunting the region for the rare [[Orichalcum]] present within the [[Abominations|Abomination]] [[Nexomancy#Automatons|Automatons]] let loose here thousands of years ago.
#### History
The following is a brief explanation of the history of the world. It is not super important, but it would be nice if you gave it a once over just to understand some of the context of the world. I'll assume you guys are at least vaguely aware of most of it:
##### [[1st Epoch]]
The period of time when the races first began to evolve into what they are today. No ruins in the world are dated to this era, except for in [[Eden]]. Therefore, it is theorized life originated in [[Eden]] and the majority or all of the 1st Epoch was spent there. 

At the end of the 1st Epoch, the supercontinent separated rapidly, creating the world we have today. It lasted for about 930,000 years. 
##### [[2nd Epoch]]
This is the period of time life first began outside of [[Eden]], beginning in [[Meridiem]], before spreading elsewhere. [[The Silver Havens]] and [[Nidavellir]] were rapidly populated. The [[Nexus]] occurred around this time. The [[Old Dawnic]] was invented after [[Cassiel]], an [[Archon]] in disguise, wrote the [[Genesis]] thereby founding [[Dawnism]]. A few thousand years later, the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] were founded by [[Denarion (ruler)|Denarion]]. After the [[Brothers' War]], [[Neton (ruler)|Neton]] killed him with the [[Orcs]], ending the vastly influential nation, but ending the [[Orcs]]' forces too after their leader disappeared. This event signified the end of the 2nd Epoch.
###### [[Nexus]]
The **Nexus** was a vastly important event during the early [[2nd Epoch]] where creatures known as [[Abominations]] transferred over from another planet. It is believed by the [[Elves]] that [[Humans]] were one of these peoples, and they quickly defeated the monsters and dominated the world afterwards. At this point, the [[Nexusborns]], powerful weapons from another universe, transferred as vestiges of the cataclysm. 
##### [[3rd Epoch]]
The current epoch, it was punctuated by battle, suffering, and decay, as the former [[Dawn Kingdoms]] fought over territory. [[Tarsis]] in the deserts of [[Ophiri]] remained a group of loosely connected city-states. The [[Kitsune]] nation of [[Kisia]] was united under one Shogun. [[Drachma]], a vast empire from [[Ophiri]], was formed under the religion of [[Helladism]]. [[Esrith]], the second-largest of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]], reunited and absorbed two other former [[Dawn Kingdoms]], although it was nothing like the original [[Dawn Kingdoms]]. Another of the [[Dawn Kingdoms]] were conquered by [[Drachma]], and [[Tarsis]] was invaded. 
###### Appearance of [[Eden]]
In 2639 TE, during the [[Arc]], the [[Shepherds]] and Grandchildren aligned causing the tides off the coat of[[The Splintered Sea]] to rise and form [[Eden]]. 

The [[Edenic Mechanics#Void Fruits|Void Fruits]] began to populate the world, allowing people to populate [[Eden]]--but only after experiencing [[Dawnism#Death|Death]].
